# A1 Communication Manual: 17 Situational Categories Coverage Audit

This audit evaluates the coverage of 17 core CEFR A0–A1 situational communication categories across the 10 existing System pages in `manuals/en/communication/a1/` (both `part-1.html` through `part-10.html` overview pages and topic pages in `topics/`).

---

| # | Situational Category | Coverage Status | Covered in (File / System) | Gap Notes & Placement Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | **Asking for Personal Info** | ✅ Fully Covered | System 1 (`greetings-and-introductions.html`) & System 2 (`asking-simple-questions.html`) | Full coverage of name, origin, age, job, contact details, and basic personal WH- questions. |

## Summary of Findings

- **Fully Covered Categories (9/17):** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 13
- **Partially Covered Categories (4/17):** 8 (Telling time/plans), 12 (Agreement), 15 (Apologizing), 16 (Needs/wants)
- **Identified Content Gaps (4/17):** 9 (Weather), 10 (Health/feeling unwell), 14 (Asking permission), 17 (Congratulating/celebrating)

### Targeted Placement Map for Follow-Up Expansion
1. **System 2 (Asking Simple Questions / `asking-simple-questions.html`):** Add *Asking Permission* ("Can I...?", "Is it okay if...?").
2. **System 9 (Keeping Simple Conversations Alive / `keeping-simple-conversations-alive.html`):** Add *Weather Small Talk*, *Health & Feelings*, *Extended Apologies*, *Physical Needs/Wants*, and *Congratulating & Social Celebrations*.
