📘 How to Use COSYmanuals with Students

COSYmanuals is structured as a private, direct-link resource repository. Contracted teachers can share specific unit or marathon URLs directly with students during or prior to lessons.

  • Flipped Classroom Model: Send unit pages as pre-lesson readings for students to review vocabulary and grammar rules on COSYtools before live sessions.
  • Structured Live Lessons: Utilize speaking prompts and roleplay scenarios during live calls or classroom sessions.
  • Asynchronous Homework: Assign marathon daily challenges and practice links to build student consistency.

📝 Lesson Plan Template

Recommended 60-Minute Lesson Architecture for COSYmanuals Units:

1. Warm-Up & Review (10 mins): Review homework, discuss previous unit's target vocabulary.

2. Core Grammar & Vocabulary (15 mins): Introduce new concepts using interactive reference tables.

3. Guided Practice (15 mins): Interactive exercises, gap fills, or guided roleplays.

4. Spoken Application (15 mins): Discussions, debates, and contextual production tasks.

5. Wrap-Up & Self-Assessment (5 mins): Review unit checklist and set next homework task.

📊 Assessment Rubrics

Evaluate oral and written performance according to CEFR standards:

Criterion Needs Work (1-2) Proficient (3-4) Mastery (5)
Fluency & Flow Frequent hesitation & pauses. Smooth speech with minor pauses. Natural cadence and transition use.
Grammar Precision Frequent structural errors. Minor tense errors, high clarity. Accurate complex tense usage.
Vocabulary Variety Repetitive, simple words. Good range of unit key terms. Rich, nuanced idiomatic terms.

💡 Best Practices for Online & Offline Classes

💻 Online Classes

  • Share direct manual links in video call chats.
  • Use breakout rooms for partner speaking drills.
  • Incorporate live interactive quizzes.

🏫 Offline In-Person Classes

  • Print manual units using the PDF print button.
  • Pair students for physical card roleplay activities.
  • Assign offline workbook exercises for homework.
