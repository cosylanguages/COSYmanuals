##  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This audit evaluates the content coverage of COSYmanuals' English A1 manuals against the canonical 20-unit A0–A1 General English Course syllabus defined in `docs/a0-a1-unit-crosswalk.md`.

### Key Audit Findings

1. **Grammar Manual (`manuals/en/grammar/a1/topics/`)**:
   - **Total Files**: 58 files (47 individual topic pages + 11 Part overview indices).
   - **Coverage Status**: **100% Covered** (28/28 required crosswalk grammar topics present).
   - **Key Strengths**: Comprehensive coverage from foundational mechanics (`to-be.html`, `articles.html`) through tenses (`present-simple.html`, `present-continuous.html`, `past-simple-regular.html`, `past-simple-irregular.html`, `going-to.html`, `will.html`) and modal verbs (`can-cant.html`, `could-couldnt.html`, `id-like.html`).

2. **Vocabulary Manual (`manuals/en/vocabulary/a1/topics/`)**:
   - **Total Files**: 80 files (69 individual topic pages + 10 Part overview indices + 1 manual guide).
   - **Coverage Status**: **100% Covered** (35/35 required crosswalk vocabulary topics present).
   - **Key Strengths**: Unmatched depth across all foundational CEFR domains including personal details, clothing, housing, routines, food/beverage, professions, city/transport, weather, health, technology, and travel.

3. **Communication Manual (`manuals/en/communication/a1/topics/`)**:
   - **Total Files**: 10 files (10 situational topic pages).
   - **Coverage Status**: **50.0% Covered** (10 covered / 10 missing out of 20 total required communication topic slots across the 20 units).
   - **Identified Gap**: While core situational areas (greetings, asking questions, shopping, restaurant ordering, directions, phone/messaging, and capstone exchanges) are present, **10 missing situational communication topic pages** across the 20-unit course lack dedicated communication topic pages and must be authored in Phase 1 follow-up tasks.

## 🚩 FLAGGED LIST OF MISSING TOPICS FOR PHASE 1 AUTHORING

Below is the prioritized list of **10 missing situational communication topic pages** that Phase 1 follow-up tasks need to author under `manuals/en/communication/a1/topics/`:

1. **`family-and-social-exchanges.html`** (Unit 3: Family & Relationships)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Talking about family members, marital status, age of relatives, introducing family members to others, and asking about family background.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"This is my husband / wife."*, *"How old is your brother?"*, *"He is 10 years old."*, *"Do you have any brothers or sisters?"*

2. **`describing-people-and-belongings.html`** (Unit 4: Appearance, Body & Belongings)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Describing what people look like, physical appearance (height, hair color, eyes), what people are wearing, and identifying lost or personal belongings.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"What does he look like?"*, *"She has dark hair and blue eyes."*, *"Is this your coat / umbrella?"*, *"I'm looking for my bag."*

3. **`housing-and-accommodation.html`** (Unit 5: Home, Rooms & Living Space)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Describing your house or apartment, talking to a landlord/flatmate, asking about rent, rooms, and house rules.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"I live in a two-bedroom flat."*, *"Where is the kitchen / bathroom?"*, *"How much is the rent per month?"*, *"Is heating included?"*

4. **`daily-routines-and-schedules.html`** (Unit 6: Daily Routine, Time & Schedules)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Discussing daily timetables, morning/evening habits, asking what time someone gets up, works, or eats, and coordinating daily schedules.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"What time do you usually wake up?"*, *"I start work at 9 o'clock."*, *"I usually have dinner with my family at 7."*

5. **`workplace-and-job-interactions.html`** (Unit 9: Work, Professions & Workplace)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Explaining your job duties, asking colleagues for basic work assistance, introducing yourself in professional settings, and discussing office hours.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"What do you do?"*, *"I work as an engineer at..."*, *"Could you help me with this report?"*, *"My office hours are from 9 to 5."*

6. **`weather-and-small-talk.html`** (Unit 14: Weather, Seasons & Climate)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Initiating small talk using weather comments, describing current weather conditions, seasonal preferences, and making casual social conversation.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"Lovely day, isn't it?"*, *"It's quite cold / rainy today."*, *"What's the weather like in your town?"*, *"I love sunny weather."*

7. **`health-and-medical-exchanges.html`** (Unit 15: Health, Body & Feeling Unwell)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Explaining physical symptoms to a doctor or pharmacist, asking for medication, describing pain/illness, and understanding basic medical advice.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"I don't feel well."*, *"I have a headache / sore throat / fever."*, *"How often should I take this medicine?"*, *"Take two pills after meals."*

8. **`talking-about-past-events.html`** (Units 17–18: Past Simple States & Life Stories)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Narrating past experiences, weekend activities, holidays, personal life milestones, and answering "How was your weekend?"
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"How was your weekend?"*, *"It was great / quiet."*, *"I went to the museum / visited my parents."*, *"Where were you yesterday?"*

9. **`making-plans-and-suggestions.html`** (Unit 19: Future Plans & Travel Intentions)
   - **Communicative Focus**: Making plans with friends, suggesting activities, accepting or declining invitations politely, and confirming time and meeting locations.
   - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"What are you going to do this weekend?"*, *"Shall we go to the cinema?"*, *"Sounds great! Let me know where to meet."*, *"Sorry, I can't. I'm busy."*

10. **`travel-and-hotel-reservations.html`** (Unit 19: Travel & Booking)
    - **Communicative Focus**: Booking hotel rooms, checking in/out at hotels or airports, asking about luggage, transport tickets, and room facilities.
    - **Target Sentence Frames**: *"I'd like to book a double room for two nights."*, *"Is breakfast included?"*, *"What time is check-out?"*, *"Can I have the Wi-Fi password?"*
