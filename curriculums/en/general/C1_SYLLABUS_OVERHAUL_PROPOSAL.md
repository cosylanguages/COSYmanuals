# C1 Advanced English Syllabus Overhaul & Modernization Proposal

> **Document Status**: Proposal & Master Brainstorming Specification
> **Level Target**: CEFR C1 (Advanced) with C2 Masterclass Stretch Topics
> **Scope**: 12 Units (52 core lesson topics + 14 progress test / exam / feedback sessions)

---

## 🎯 Executive Overview & Strategic Vision

The purpose of this overhaul is to transform the existing C1 English syllabus into a deeply engaging, intellectually stimulating, and pedagogically rigorous course.

### Core Objectives
1. **Renaming & Modernizing**: Replace generic or dated lesson titles with compelling, contemporary concepts rooted in real-world debates, technology, psychology, economics, and global trends.
2. **Grammatical Rigor**: Systematically embed advanced C1–C2 syntactic structures (inverted conditionals, split clefts, dense nominalization, subjunctive mood, hedging devices, information packaging, and complex discourse markers).
3. **Lexical Richness**: Equip learners with domain-specific terminology (academic, legal, financial, technological, socio-political), high-frequency collocations, idiomatic expressions, and loan words.
4. **Contextual Depth**: Frame every topic around meaningful, real-world questions that foster high-level critical thinking, persuasive argumentation, and nuanced debate.
5. **CEFR Alignment & Stretch**: Maintain clear distinction between core C1 consolidation topics and C2 stretch topics (philosophical, scientific, and high-level diplomatic/political themes).

## 🎓 CEFR Level Distribution & Pedagogical Recommendations

1. **C1 Core vs C2 Stretch Allocation**:
   - **C1 Core Focus (Units 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 11)**: Focuses on professional fluency, pragmatic competence, complex opinion writing, and high-level workplace/societal discourse.
   - **C2 Masterclass Stretch (Units 5, 6, 10, 12)**: Elevates students to near-native stylistic precision, abstract scientific/philosophical reasoning, dense academic prose, and complex geopolitical debate.

2. **Structural Continuity**:
   - Preserves all 12 core units, 52 main topics, optional (`opt.`) enrichment sessions, mature (`18+`) designated topics, and progress/exam milestones.
