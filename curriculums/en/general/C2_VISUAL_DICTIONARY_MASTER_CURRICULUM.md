    Coherentism evaluates beliefs based on their consistency within a broader belief system.

  - **Sentence Frames:** `Rationalist philosophy asserts that + [A priori knowledge] + provides the foundation for + [Epistemic certainty]`

  - **Micro-Dialogue:**

    - B: Empiricism holds that all knowledge originates from sensory experience, whereas rationalism relies on a priori deduction.
    - A: How does Kant attempt to synthesize both viewpoints?
    - B: By proposing that while all knowledge begins with experience, it is structured by innate mental categories.
    - A: What role does falsifiability play in this synthesis?
    - B: It ensures that empirical claims remain open to rigorous counter-testing.

    `Cartesian doubt methodically strips away sensory assumptions to reveal + [Primary Conscious Certainty]`
    `Addressing the epistemic regress problem requires adopting either + [Foundationalism or Coherentism]`

  - **Micro-Dialogue:**
    A: How does solipsism resolve the problem of external world skepticism?
    - B: By asserting that only one's own mind is certain to exist, dismissing external world inquiries as unprovable.
    - A: What is the main critique of solipsism?
    - B: That it renders intersubjective communication and shared scientific inquiry logically impossible.
    - A: How do pragmatic philosophers respond to radical skepticism?
    - B: By arguing that unresolvable theoretical doubt should be set aside in favor of functional belief systems.
