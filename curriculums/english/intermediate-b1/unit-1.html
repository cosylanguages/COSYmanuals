Unit 1: Life Stories & Past Memories

Curriculum: English Intermediate (B1)

🎯 Unit Objectives

  • Master the contrast between Past Simple and Past Continuous.
  • Expand vocabulary related to life milestones, childhood, and personal memories.
  • Tell a detailed narrative about an important event from your personal past.

📐 Grammar Focus: Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

Use the Past Simple for completed past actions that occurred at a specific time. Use the Past Continuous for actions that were ongoing in the past, often interrupted by another event.

Example: "I was walking home (ongoing action) when I met an old friend (interruption)."

🗣️ Speaking Exercises

  1. Warm-up Prompt: Describe your favorite memory from primary school in 2 minutes. Focus on setting the scene using Past Continuous.
  2. Roleplay Activity: Interview a partner about a major life milestone (e.g., graduating, moving cities, or getting a first job).
