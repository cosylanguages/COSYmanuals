Units Count 12 Interactive Units

🎯 Overview & Learning Objectives

This course is designed for B1 intermediate learners looking to build fluency in everyday, professional, and social interactions. By the end of this curriculum, students will be able to speak confidently about past experiences, future plans, opinions, and abstract concepts.

  • Communicate efficiently in travel, work, and personal situations.
  • Understand main ideas of clear standard speech on familiar matters.
  • Produce connected text on topics which are familiar or of personal interest.
  • Describe experiences, events, dreams, hopes, and ambitions with brief reasons.
