Narrative Tenses: Past Simple, Continuous, Perfect & Perfect Continuous

Combining four past tenses for rich storytelling

TenseRole in NarrativeExample
Past SimpleMain sequential events (what happened)He opened the door and stepped inside.
Past ContinuousBackground scene & ongoing actionsThe rain was falling softly outside.
Past PerfectEarlier events (before main narrative)He realized he had forgotten his keys.
Past Perfect ContinuousDuration before a past pointThey had been driving for hours before finding hotel.

Storytelling Formula: Use Past Continuous to set the background scene, Past Simple for the main action events, and Past Perfect for earlier background context.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

In natural connected speech, '-ing' ends in the velar nasal /ɪŋ/. The auxiliary 'be' is unstressed and blends into the subject.

Examples
  • While the sun was setting, Sarah walked into the restaurant where her friends had reserved a table.
