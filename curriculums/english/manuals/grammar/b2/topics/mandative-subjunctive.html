Mandative & Formulaic Subjunctive

Expressing urgency, formal demands, and fixed subjunctive expressions

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express formal demands, recommendations, necessity, and set expressions with precision in academic and professional English.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

Core Rule: The mandative subjunctive uses the base form of the verb (bare infinitive: be, go, attend) after formal verbs of demand/suggestion (suggest, demand, insist, propose, recommend) or adjectives of necessity (essential, vital, crucial, imperative), regardless of subject or tense.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Mandative & Formulaic Subjunctive, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

Examples
  • The manager suggested that he be present at the meeting.
  • It is vital that every employee submit the report on time.
  • If I were you, I would accept the offer immediately.
  • Be that as it may, we must proceed according to plan.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌The doctor recommended that she goes to the hospital.
tap to see the correct form
✅The doctor recommended that she go to the hospital.
(Mandative subjunctive uses base verb 'go', no -s ending)
❌It is essential that he is informed immediately.
tap to see the correct form
✅It is essential that he be informed immediately.
(Use 'be' for the passive subjunctive, not 'is' or 'was')
❌She insisted that he doesn't leave the room.
tap to see the correct form
✅She insisted that he not leave the room.
(Negative subjunctive uses 'not + base verb', without 'does' or 'did')
