Expressing Purpose: To vs For

Explaining why you do something or what an item is used for

Purpose PatternStructureExample
Action purpose (Why you do something)to + infinitiveI went to the shop to buy bread.
Noun purpose (For a thing / person)for + nounI went to the shop for some bread.
General function of a toolfor + verb-ingThis knife is for cutting bread.

Rule: Use to + verb when stating the reason for an action (I came here to study, NOT for study or for to study).

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Expressing Purpose: To vs For, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

Examples
  • She is saving money to buy a laptop.
  • This key is for opening the garage door.
