TenseFormCore ConceptExample
Present Simpleverb / verb-sHabits & general truthsI work every day.
Present Continuousam/is/are + verb-ingActions right now / arrangementsI am working right now.
Past Simplepast form (-ed / irregular)Finished past actions at specific timeI worked yesterday.
Past Continuouswas/were + verb-ingAction in progress in the pastI was working at 4 PM.
Present Perfecthave/has + past participlePast connected to present / experienceI have worked here for years.
Future (Going to / Will)going to / will + verbIntentions vs instant decisionsI am going to work tomorrow. / I'll work late.

Mastery Checklist: Match the time adverbial to the correct tense: yesterday ➔ Past Simple; now ➔ Present Continuous; since 2020 ➔ Present Perfect; tomorrow ➔ Future.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Review of Verb Tenses, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

Examples
  • Yesterday I went shopping, but today I am resting at home.
  • I have known Mark since 2018, and we are meeting tomorrow.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I live here since 2015 and I am working here yesterday.
