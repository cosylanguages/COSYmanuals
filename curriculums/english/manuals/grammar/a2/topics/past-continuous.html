SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / He / She / ItHe was sleeping.He wasn't sleeping.Was he sleeping?
You / We / TheyThey were playing.They weren't playing.Were they playing?

Form: was/were + verb -ing. Use it for an action that was already in progress at a specific time in the past (e.g., At 8 PM yesterday, I was studying).

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

In natural connected speech, '-ing' ends in the velar nasal /ɪŋ/. The auxiliary 'be' is unstressed and blends into the subject.

Examples
  • What were you doing at midnight? I was watching a film.
  • It was raining heavily when we woke up.
