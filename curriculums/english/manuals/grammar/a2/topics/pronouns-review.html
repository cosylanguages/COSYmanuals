Rule: Subject pronouns do the action (Subject + Verb). Object pronouns receive the action (Verb + Object). Possessive adjectives need a noun (my book). Possessive pronouns stand alone (The book is mine).

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Pronouns Review: Subject, Object, Possessive, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

Examples
  • She called him yesterday, but he didn't answer her.
  • Is this pen yours or mine? It's my pen, so it's mine.
