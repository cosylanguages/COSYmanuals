Past Simple Review

Regular -ed endings, irregular past forms, and ago

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can talk clearly about finished actions at specific completed times in the past.

1. "I visited Paris in 2019." — Is the trip to Paris completely finished?
Correct · Past simple refers to finished actions at a specific time in the past.
2. "She lived in Rome for two years." — Does she live in Rome now?
Correct · Past simple indicates the period of living in Rome is completely over.
TypeAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
Regular Verbs (+ed)I worked yesterday.I didn't work yesterday.Did you work yesterday?
Irregular VerbsShe went to Paris.She didn't go to Paris.Did she go to Paris?

Rule: In negative sentences and questions, use did / didn't + the base verb infinitive (NOT the past form). I didn't see him (NOT: I didn't saw him).

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Regular '-ed' endings have 3 distinct sounds: /t/ after voiceless consonants (stopped), /d/ after voiced sounds (played), and /ɪd/ after /t/ or /d/ (needed).

Examples
  • They bought a new car two weeks ago.
  • What time did you arrive last night?
