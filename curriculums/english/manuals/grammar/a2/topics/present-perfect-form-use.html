Present Perfect: Form & Use

A past action connected to now

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss life experiences and past actions whose timing is unstated or connected to the present.

SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / You / We / TheyI've worked.I haven't worked.Have you worked?
He / She / ItShe's worked.She hasn't worked.Has she worked?

Form: have/has + past participle. Use: a past action connected to now — we don't know or don't care exactly when. I've visited Paris. (at some point in my life, up to now)

👀 Notice: Is the time finished, or not mentioned?

Past simple = a finished time (I visited Paris in 2019). Present perfect = no finished time, just the experience or result matters now (I have visited Paris, at some point).

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Present Perfect: Form & Use, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples
  • They've travelled to many countries.
  • Has she finished her homework?
