PatternCommon Trigger VerbsExample
Verb + Infinitive (to + verb)want, hope, decide, plan, offer, promise, needI want to learn Spanish. She decided to buy the car.
Verb + Gerund (verb-ing)enjoy, mind, avoid, finish, suggest, keepI enjoy swimming. Would you mind closing the door?

Rule: Memorize which trigger verbs take to + infinitive and which take -ing. After prepositions (about, in, of, for), ALWAYS use the gerund: interested in learning.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Infinitives & Gerunds, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

Examples
  • We decided to stay at home.
  • They finished eating dinner at 8 PM.
