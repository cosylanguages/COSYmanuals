Past Perfect: Had + Past Participle

The action that happened before another past action

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can clarify the chronological sequence of two connected past events.

Core Rule: Master the usage, nuance, and structural patterns of Past Perfect (had + past participle) to show an action completed before another point in the past.

1st: Train had left (Past Perfect)
2nd: I arrived (Past Simple)
Now
pastfuture

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Regular '-ed' endings have 3 distinct sounds: /t/ after voiceless consonants (stopped), /d/ after voiced sounds (played), and /ɪd/ after /t/ or /d/ (needed).

Examples
  • When I arrived at the station, the train had already left.
  • She had studied French before she moved to Paris.
⚠️ Common mistakes
