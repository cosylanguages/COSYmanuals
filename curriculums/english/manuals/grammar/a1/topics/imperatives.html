Form: base verb (no subject) for instructions, directions, rules and recipes. Add don't for the negative. Sit down. Don't touch that. Please wait here.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Dropping the subject entirely is unusual for English, which normally insists on a stated subject in every sentence · imperatives are one of the few exceptions.
