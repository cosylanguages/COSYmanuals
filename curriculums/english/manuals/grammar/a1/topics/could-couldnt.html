💡 Could is more polite than can

Can you help me? is fine between friends. Could you help me? sounds a little more polite · good for shops, strangers, or your teacher.

∞ Connects to what you already know

Can / Can’t · could is simply can moved into the past; same meaning, further back in time.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Could / Couldn't, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples

  • He couldn't come to the party last week.
