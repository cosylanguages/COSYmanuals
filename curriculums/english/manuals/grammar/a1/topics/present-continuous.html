💡 Two parts, one job each

Two parts: am/is/are (matches the subject) + verb -ing (always the same). Forget the -ing, and it's not 'happening now' anymore.

Now
I'm working now
pastfuture

Spelling of -ing: drop silent -e (make → making); double the last consonant after one vowel (run → running); -ie → y (lie → lying).

∞ Connects to what you already know

The Verb ‘To Be’ · am/is/are here are the same forms, now paired with -ing.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

In natural connected speech, '-ing' ends in the velar nasal /ɪŋ/. The auxiliary 'be' is unstressed and blends into the subject.

More examples
  • I'm reading a book now.
  • She's cooking dinner at the moment.
  • Is it raining?
