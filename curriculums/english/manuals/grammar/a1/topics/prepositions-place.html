💭 Think like a native speaker

English prepositions of place are notoriously inconsistent with other languages · 'in the photo' but 'on the wall', 'at the door' but 'in the doorway'. There's often no logical shortcut; native speakers learned these one phrase at a time too.

2. Fix: 'The book is in the shelf.'
Books sit 'on' a shelf's surface, not 'in' it.

Between describes a position in the middle of two things.
