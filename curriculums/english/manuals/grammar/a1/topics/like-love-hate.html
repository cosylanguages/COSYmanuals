Rule: after like, love, hate, don't mind, enjoy, the next verb takes the -ing form. I like swimming.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Treating a verb as a noun (swimming, reading) by adding -ing is a very English trick · many languages use a completely different infinitive-style construction here instead.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Like / Love / Hate + -ing, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples
  • I love watching football.
  • She likes cooking for her family.
  • We hate waiting in long queues.
  • Do you enjoy travelling?
