🗣️ Pronunciation Note

In spoken English, 'going to' reduces to 'gonna' /ˈɡənə/ in informal speech, while 'will' contracts smoothly to /l/ (she'll, they'll).

More examples
  • I'm going to visit my grandmother this weekend.
  • They're going to buy a new car.
  • Is she going to study tonight?

∞ Connects to what you already know

Present Continuous · going to uses the exact same am/is/are + -ing pattern you already know, just add ‘going to’ after it.
