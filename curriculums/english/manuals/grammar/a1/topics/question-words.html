★ Why this matters

Question words are how every conversation starts · asking someone's name, where they're from, or what time it is all begin here.

Asks forWordExample
thingWHATWhat is your name?
placeWHEREWhere do you live?
timeWHENWhen is your lesson?
personWHOWho is your teacher?
reasonWHYWhy are you learning English?
mannerHOWHow do you go to work?
quantity, countableHOW MANYHow many students are there?
quantity, uncountableHOW MUCHHow much does it cost?
ageHOW OLDHow old are you?
clock timeWHAT TIMEWhat time is it?

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Question Words, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.
