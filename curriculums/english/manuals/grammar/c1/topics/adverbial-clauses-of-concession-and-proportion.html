Adverbial Clauses of Concession & Proportion

Formulating complex concession (Notwithstanding, Albeit) and proportion (In proportion as, Inasmuch as) in formal prose.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can state proportional relationships and complex concessions in formal essays and academic treatises.

Core Rule: Use formal concessive subordinators (albeit, notwithstanding that) and proportional subordinators (in proportion as, in proportion to, inasmuch as) to link dependent clauses.

SubordinatorTypeExample Sentence
AlbeitConcessiveThe policy was effective, albeit costly.
Notwithstanding thatConcessiveNotwithstanding that revenues dropped, dividends remained fixed.
In proportion asProportionalRisks escalate in proportion as leverage increases.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Pronounce 'albeit' as /ɔːlˈbiː.ɪt/ and 'notwithstanding' as /ˌnɒt.wɪðˈstæn.dɪŋ/.

Examples
  • The expansion continued, albeit at a reduced pace.
  • Inasmuch as the audit revealed no fraud, the file is closed.
