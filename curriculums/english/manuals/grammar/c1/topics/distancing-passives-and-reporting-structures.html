Distancing Passives & Reporting Structures

Impersonal passive structures and reporting verbs (It is reported to have been..., Allegedly...) for academic stance and objective distance.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can present claims objectively and maintain professional stance distance in corporate policy reports and academic research.

Core Rule: Use impersonal passive constructions: It + Passive Reporting Verb + That-Clause (It is claimed that...) or Subject + Passive Reporting Verb + Perfect Infinitive (The asset is reported to have appreciated...).

Structure TypeGrammar PatternExample Sentence
Impersonal It-PassiveIt + Passive Verb + that...It is widely reported that legislative amendments will pass.
Subject + Infinitive PassiveSubject + Passive Verb + to have V3The enterprise is understood to have acquired the subsidiary.
Adverbial DistancingAllegedly / Reportedly + ClauseReportedly, negotiations reached consensus late last night.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Keep stress on the reporting verb stem (rePORted, un-der-STOOD) and unstress 'to have been'.

Examples
  • The manuscript is believed to have been authored in the early fourteenth century.
  • It has been suggested that tariff adjustments may disincentivize cross-border investment.
