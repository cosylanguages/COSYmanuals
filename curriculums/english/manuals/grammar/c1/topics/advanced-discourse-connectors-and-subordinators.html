ConnectorSyntactic ClassExample Sentence
Insofar asSubordinating ConjunctionThe policy succeeds insofar as it protects low-income households.
In light ofPrepositional PhraseIn light of recent findings, the guidelines were revised.
NotwithstandingPreposition / AdverbNotwithstanding market headwinds, revenues grew.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Ensure clear articulation of 'insofar as' /ɪn.səʊˈfɑːr æz/.

Examples
  • In light of macroeconomic shifts, strategic reallocation is essential.
  • The protocol is effective insofar as compliance is enforced.
