Mixed Conditionals for Past-Present Hypothesizing

Expressing hypothetical connections across different time frames (Past cause -> Present result, or Permanent state -> Past result).

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can articulate complex hypothetical scenarios linking past events to present realities and enduring states to past outcomes.

Core Rule: Use If + Past Perfect for past condition with Would + Base Verb for present result (Past -> Present), or If + Past Simple for permanent state with Would have + Past Participle for past result (Permanent -> Past).

TypeIf-Clause (Condition)Main Clause (Result)Example Sentence
Past -> PresentIf + Past Perfect (If central banks had acted)Would + Base Verb (the economy would be stable now)If central banks had acted earlier, market liquidity would be healthier today.
Permanent -> PastIf + Past Simple (If she spoke fluent French)Would have + V3 (she would have led the summit)If she spoke fluent French, she would have been appointed to lead the delegation.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Contract auxiliaries naturally in spoken discourse (had -> 'd, would have -> would've).

Examples
  • If we had invested in renewable infrastructure five years ago, our grid would be zero-carbon today.
  • If the country were not an island nation, trade disputes would have escalated much faster last year.
