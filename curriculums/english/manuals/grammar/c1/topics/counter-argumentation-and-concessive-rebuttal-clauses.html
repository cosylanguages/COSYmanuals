Counter-Argumentation & Concessive Rebuttals

Framing concession-rebuttal pairs (Granted that X, it nevertheless follows that Y) in formal debate.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can acknowledge opposing premises while maintaining a firm thesis counter-argument.

Core Rule: Pair an introductory concessive clause (Granted that, Conceding that, Admitting that) with an emphatic main rebuttal clause (it nevertheless follows that, nonetheles, it remains true that).

Concession PhaseRebuttal TransitionExample Sentence
Granted that + Clauseit nevertheless follows that...Granted that costs rose, it nevertheless follows that productivity doubled.
Conceding that + Clausethe fact remains that...Conceding that errors occurred, the fact remains that the core hypothesis is sound.
Admittedly + Sentencehowever / yet...Admittedly, risks exist; however, delay carries greater peril.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Pause after the concessive clause and stress the rebuttal transition marker (nevertheless, however).

Examples
  • Granted that short-term volatility persists, long-term fundamentals remain robust.
  • Admittedly, the sample size was small; nevertheless, the correlation is statistically significant.
