Cleft Sentences for Emphasis

It-clefts and Wh-clefts (pseudo-clefts) to restructure information and highlight specific sentence elements.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can foreground specific elements, contrast facts, and structure emphasis in debate and formal writing.

Core Rule: It-Cleft: It + Be + Highlighted Element + That/Who + Rest of Sentence.
Wh-Cleft: What / All + Clause + Be + Highlighted Element.

Cleft TypeGrammar PatternExample Sentence
It-Cleft (Subject)It is/was + Subject + that/who...It was the financial director who identified the accounting discrepancy.
It-Cleft (Adverbial)It is/was + Adverbial + that...It was only after the audit that structural vulnerabilities emerged.
Wh-Cleft (Pseudo-cleft)What + S + V + is/was + ClauseWhat concerns the board most is long-term fiscal sustainability.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Apply focal stress to the highlighted element immediately following 'It was...' or 'What... is'.

Examples
  • It is not immediate profit that drives our research, but long-term societal impact.
  • What we require above all else is institutional transparency.
