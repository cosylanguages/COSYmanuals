Conditionals of Concession & Hypothesis

Concessive conditional structures (Even if, Even though, Whether... or) to express unyielding premises.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can maintain firm thesis stances and evaluate scenarios where outcomes hold regardless of conditions.

Core Rule: Use Even if + Clause to express that an outcome remains unchanged regardless of a hypothetical condition, or Whether + S + V + or + Clause for alternative conditions.

Concessive StructureGrammar PatternExample Sentence
Even if (Hypothetical)Even if + Present/Past + ClauseEven if fiscal policy were relaxed, inflation would remain sticky.
Even though (Factual)Even though + Real Tense + ClauseEven though interest rates rose, housing demand surged.
Whether... or (Alternative)Whether + S + V + or + S + VWhether the board agrees or dissents, the merger will proceed.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Emphasize 'Even' and 'Whether' to mark the concessive nature of the premise.

Examples
  • Even if the project exceeds its original budget, its strategic value justifies completion.
  • Whether we deploy capital locally or abroad, strict risk parameters apply.
