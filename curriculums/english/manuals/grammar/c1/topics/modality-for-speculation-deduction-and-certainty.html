Modality for Speculation, Deduction & Certainty

Must have, cannot have, might conceivably, open to question, and past modal deductions

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express fine-grained degrees of past and present certainty, impossibility, and speculation.

Core Rule: Apply must have + V3 for logical necessity, cannot/couldn't have + V3 for negative certainty, and might conceivably for theoretical possibilities.

Deduction LevelStructureExample
Past Certainty (+)must have + V3The system must have encountered an unhandled exception.
Past Certainty (-)cannot / couldn't have + V3The security breach couldn't have originated from the internal server.
Theoretical Possibilitymight conceivably + V3 / BaseThe board might conceivably approve the merger.
Open CertaintyIt remains to be seen / open to questionWhether this strategy will succeed remains to be seen.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Modality for Speculation, Deduction & Certainty, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

Examples

  • Whether the legislation will achieve its intended goals remains open to question.
