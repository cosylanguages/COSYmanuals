Formulaic PhraseFunction / MeaningExample Sentence
Be that as it mayConcessive transition ('nevertheless')Be that as it may, we must proceed with the vote.
Suffice it to saySummarizing assertion ('it is enough to say')Suffice it to say, the experiment exceeded expectations.
Far be it from me to...Polite disclaimer before criticismFar be it from me to criticize, but errors exist.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Pronounce formulaic expressions as single unhurried rhythmic blocks.

Examples
  • Suffice it to say, the quarterly figures reflect unprecedented growth.
  • Be that as it may, the board must evaluate alternative proposals.
