Cohesion, Ellipsis & Lexical Substitution

Cohesive connectors, substitution devices (do so, such, the former / the latter), and ellipsis

Core Rule: Use cohesive connectors (notwithstanding, subsequently, accordingly) and substitution markers (do so, the former / the latter) for elegant discourse continuity.

DeviceCohesive FunctionExample
Cohesive Connectorsnotwithstanding / subsequently / accordinglyNotwithstanding initial setbacks, the venture secured funding.
Substitution (Nominal)the former / the latterThe report analyzed solar and wind energy; the former showed higher growth.
Substitution (Verbal)do so / doing soIf members wish to vote, they must do so in writing.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Cohesion, Ellipsis & Lexical Substitution, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

Examples
  • The board evaluated two proposals; the former prioritized cost efficiency, while the latter emphasized rapid expansion.
  • Employees were asked to sign the revised protocol, and all senior managers did so promptly.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌ The company wanted to expand, but to expand was too risky.
