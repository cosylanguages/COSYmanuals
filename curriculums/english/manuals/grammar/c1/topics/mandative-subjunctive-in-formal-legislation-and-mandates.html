Mandative Subjunctive in Formal Registers

Using the base form of the verb after expressions of demand, requirement, recommendation, and necessity in formal legislation.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can draft formal resolutions, legal mandates, and institutional requirements with normative subjunctive precision.

Core Rule: After verbs and adjectives of urgency/demand (demand, insist, require, imperative, essential, mandatory), use that + Subject + Base Verb (no -s for third person, no past tense, and be for passive/copula).

Trigger ExpressionSubjunctive PatternExample Sentence
Verb of Requirementdemand / insist + that + S + BaseThe committee demanded that the minister resign immediately.
Adjective of UrgencyIt is imperative that + S + BaseIt is imperative that the audit be completed by Friday.
Negative Subjunctiverecommend + that + S + not + BaseWe recommended that the board not approve the merger without review.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Maintain uninflected base verb pronunciation regardless of third-person subjects.

Examples
  • It is essential that every delegate submit credentials prior to voting.
  • The statute requires that safety standards be maintained across all facilities.
