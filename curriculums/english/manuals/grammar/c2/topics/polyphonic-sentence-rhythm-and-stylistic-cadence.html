Rhetorical DeviceSyntactic PatternExample Sentence
TricolonClause A, Clause B, and Clause CWe must plan with foresight, execute with precision, and lead with integrity.
ChiasmusSubject A + Verb B, Verb B + Subject AAsk not what your country can do for you; ask what you can do for your country.
Periodic CadenceDependent clauses -> Final main clauseWhen the economy faltered, when trade stalled, and when uncertainty mounted, the institution held firm.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Use rhythmic pitch modulation and deliberate pauses before final climactic clauses.

Examples
  • Not by rhetoric alone, nor by policy in isolation, but through collective action shall reform be achieved.
  • They came to observe, to analyze, and to conquer the market.
