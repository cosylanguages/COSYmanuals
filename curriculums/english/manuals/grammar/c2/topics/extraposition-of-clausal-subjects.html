Extraposition of Clausal Subjects

Extraposition of subject that-clauses, infinitive clauses, and wh-clauses using dummy 'It'.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can maintain objective academic stance and fluid sentence rhythm using extraposed clausal subject constructions.

Core Rule: Move heavy clausal subjects to the end of the sentence and insert dummy It in subject position: It + Passive / Adjectival Predicate + That-Clause / Infinitive Clause.

Extraposition TypeDummy It ConstructionExample Sentence
That-Clause ExtrapositionIt is + Adj/V3 + that...It is widely maintained that cybernetic governance requires ethical oversight.
Infinitive ExtrapositionIt is + Adj + to + BaseIt is essential to verify raw telemetry prior to algorithmic training.
Wh-Clause ExtrapositionIt remains + Adj + Wh-clauseIt remains unclear whether market liquidity will recover in Q4.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Keep 'It is' unstressed and emphasize the predicate adjective or passive verb (widely main-TAINED, es-SEN-tial).

Examples
  • It was acknowledged by all panel members that additional research is required.
  • It is imperative to address systemic inequalities before enacting policy.
