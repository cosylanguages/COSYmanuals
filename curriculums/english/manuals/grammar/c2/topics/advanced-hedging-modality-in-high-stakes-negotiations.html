Advanced Hedging Modality in Negotiations

Ultra-diplomatic modal combinations (would seem to suggest, might conceivably be construed) in executive deadlocks.

Core Rule: Stack modal auxiliaries with distance verbs and passive infinitives: would + seem / appear + to + be + V3 / Adjective.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Deliver stacked hedging modals with a smooth, unhurried, measured cadence.

Examples
  • The proposed valuation would appear to reflect optimistic market projections.
  • Any abrupt change might conceivably trigger regulatory scrutiny.
