Statutory ProvisoFunctionExample Sentence
Subject toSubordination to overriding ruleSubject to Section 4, the licensee may transfer rights.
Provided always thatBinding exception / qualificationProvided always that written notice is delivered within ten days.
Save as provided inExclusive exceptionSave as provided in Article 2, no refunds shall be issued.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Stress the legal connective phrase ('Sub-ject to', 'Pro-vi-ded al-ways that').

Examples
  • Subject to shareholder approval, the dividend distribution shall proceed.
  • Provided always that the indemnity clause remains in full force.
