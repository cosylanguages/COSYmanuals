Infinitive & Gerund Reduced Clauses

Condensing complex relative and adverbial clauses into infinitive and gerund reduced clause structures in executive agreements.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can write high-density executive contracts, policy frameworks, and legal briefs with condensed clause mechanics.

Core Rule: Reduce relative or adverbial subordinate clauses into non-finite clauses: Infinitive Clauses (the first state to ratify...) or Preposition + Gerund Clauses (with a view to establishing...).

Clause Reduction TypeReduced ConstructionExample Sentence
Infinitive Reductionthe first/only + Noun + to + BaseThe country was the first to ratify the global climate accord.
Gerund Prepositionalwith a view to + Verb-ingThe merger was executed with a view to expanding market share.
Perfect Gerundupon / after + having + V3Upon having evaluated the risks, the board authorized the expenditure.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Group reduced infinitive/gerund clauses into smooth breath units.

Examples
  • The committee met with the intention of resolving outstanding governance disputes.
  • He was the sole executive to object to the proposed compensation scheme.
