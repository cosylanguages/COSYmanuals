Legal Syntax Mechanics & Archaic Adverbs

Pre-positional modifiers, archaic legal adverbs (herein, thereof, notwithstanding, hereinafter), and statutory syntax mechanics.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can read, interpret, and draft statutory provisions and commercial contracts using canonical legal adverbs and pre-positional syntax.

Core Rule: Use compound pronominal adverbs formed from here / there / where + preposition (herein = in this document; thereof = of that; statutory = pursuant to).

Legal AdverbMeaning / EquivalentExample Sentence
Herein / HereofIn this document / Of this documentThe terms specified herein shall take effect immediately.
Thereof / ThereinOf that / In that matterThe board reviewed the contract and all amendments thereof.
NotwithstandingIn spite of / DespiteNotwithstanding any prior agreement, this covenant remains binding.
HereinafterIn the rest of this documentAcme Corp, hereinafter referred to as 'the Licensee'.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Stress the prepositional suffix in compound legal adverbs (here-IN, there-OF, here-in-AF-ter).

Examples
  • The liabilities described therein shall be borne exclusively by the indemnifying party.
  • Notwithstanding paragraph 4, either party may terminate upon written notice.
