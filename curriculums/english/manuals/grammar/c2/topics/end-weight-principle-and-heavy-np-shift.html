End-Weight Principle & Heavy-NP Shift

Shift of heavy, complex noun phrases and clausal complements to sentence-final position.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can optimize syntactic cadence and avoid awkward top-heavy sentences by shifting heavy noun phrases.

Core Rule: Postpose complex, heavily modified noun phrases to the end of the sentence (Heavy-NP Shift), especially after short verbs or prepositional phrases.

Sentence StyleStructureExample Sentence
Standard (Top-Heavy)Heavy Object + Short VerbA multi-billion dollar fund aimed at green transition was established. (Awkward)
Heavy-NP ShiftedIntroductory Verb/It + Heavy ObjectThe council established a multi-billion dollar fund aimed at green transition. (Balanced)
Extraposed ShiftIt + Verb + Heavy ClauseIt was made clear to all delegates that compliance is non-negotiable.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Deliver the heavy postposed NP with steady, sustained pitch resolution.

Examples
  • The board attributed to systemic oversight errors the sudden collapse of the overseas venture.
  • We brought to the attention of the executive committee several critical regulatory discrepancies.
