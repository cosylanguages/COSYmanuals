CEFR C1 Conversation Control

Subtle Floor Management & Strategic Steering

Interpreting non-verbal cues, reclaiming floor, interrupting without offense, and controlling conversation pace.

💬 How do I manage conversational flow, prevent interruptions, and take control quietly?

Advanced speakers use subtle floor-holding devices (fillers like "if I may just finish this thought", prosodic shifts, bodily posture) to control dialogue momentum.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can hold turn against interjections, yield turn gracefully, and steer discussions back on course.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Strategic Steering Chips

If I could just finish this point, I'll hand right over to you. To pick up on what Sarah was saying earlier... Before we move on, I'd love to hear Michael's perspective on this. That brings us neatly back to our core question...

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "Stop talking, I am not finished."
✅ Natural: "Allow me to just tie off this thought, and then I'm all ears."
