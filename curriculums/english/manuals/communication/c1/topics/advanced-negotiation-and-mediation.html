CEFR C1 Strategic Negotiation

Advanced Negotiation & Mediation Language

Bargaining positions, creating win-win trade-offs, and mediating third-party disputes.

💬 How do I negotiate high-stakes deals and mediate deadlock between opposing parties?

Strategic negotiation moves beyond simple price haggling into value creation, conditional concessions ("If... then..."), and mediating entrenched standoffs.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can pitch complex conditional concessions, unpack hidden party motives, and broker durable win-win compromises.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Negotiation & Mediation Chips

We're prepared to flex on price, provided that payment terms are expedited. Subject to board approval, we could offer extended warranty terms. Where do you see room for compromise on both sides? That represents a significant concession on our part. We have a deal! Let me summarize our binding parameters.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "Give me 10% discount or I leave."
✅ Natural: "If you can adjust your margin by 10%, we'd be willing to lock in a two-year contract today."
