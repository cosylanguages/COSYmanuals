CEFR C1 Public Speaking

Giving Eloquent Extended Presentations

Structuring keynotes, handling tough Q&A, and maintaining audience engagement over long turns.

💬 How do I command the stage during a 30-minute keynote or executive presentation?

Extended speaking requires internal signposting, vocal pacing, narrative hooks, and handling unexpected audience interruptions with poise.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can structure long-form talks, transition between complex themes smoothly, and answer hostile Q&A questions confidently.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Keynote Signposting Chips

To set the stage for today's discussion... Turning now to the second pillar of our analysis... This brings us to a crucial nuance that is often overlooked. That is an insightful question—let me break my answer into two parts. I'll leave you with this final thought... Thank you for your attention.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "Now I talk about slide 3. Look at chart."
✅ Natural: "If we turn our attention to the chart on the screen, a striking trend emerges."
