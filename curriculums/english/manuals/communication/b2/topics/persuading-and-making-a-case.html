Persuading & Making a Case

Structuring a persuasive argument for a real decision (proposal, pitch).

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can deliver persuasive business pitches, proposal speeches, and compelling cases.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.

🧩 Persuasive Pitch Chunks

Consider the impact of... What makes this proposal unique is... The evidence overwhelmingly supports... I strongly urge you to consider...

🗺️ Proposal Pitch Map

1
Hook problem: "Current onboarding procedures result in 20% employee drop-off."
2
Propose solution: "What makes our digital mentorship app unique is immediate automated feedback."
3
Call to action: "The pilot evidence overwhelmingly supports adoption. I strongly urge you to approve trials."
More Examples
  • Consider the impact of delaying this initiative for another quarter.
  • The data overwhelmingly supports our recommendation.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌You must agree with me because my proposal is good.
tap to see the correct form
✅The evidence overwhelmingly supports this proposal. I urge you to consider...
