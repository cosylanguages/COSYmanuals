CEFR B2 Modal Precision

Speculating About Past, Present & Future

Modal precision: might have, could be, is likely to, was probably.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express precise degrees of certainty across past, present, and future timeframes.

🧩 Modal Speculation Chunks

He must have missed the turn. They can't have finished already! She could well be delayed in traffic. It is bound to happen sooner or later.

🗺️ Modal Deduction Matrix

1
Past Deduction (100% Sure Positive): "The door was locked from inside; he must have gone out through the garden."
2
Past Deduction (100% Sure Negative): "She can't have received my message; she would have replied immediately."
3
Present/Future Deduction: "Given the current momentum, the policy is bound to pass."
More Examples
  • They might have misinformed us about the opening hours.
  • He could well be the best candidate for the position.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌He mustn't have seen me. (Use 'can't have' for negative past deduction!)
