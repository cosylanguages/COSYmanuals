Negotiating Towards a Decision

Collaborating under some disagreement, making concessions, and reaching compromise.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can manage nuanced group negotiations, grant concessions, and forge compromise decisions.

🧩 Negotiation & Concession Chunks

I take your point, but... Could we meet in the middle? I'm willing to concede that... if we... That seems like a fair compromise.

🗺️ Negotiation Dialogue Map

1
Stalemate: "I strongly favor allocating budget to marketing, while you prefer R&D."
2
Concession offer: "I'm willing to concede 30% of the surplus to R&D if we secure the marketing campaign."
3
Agreement seal: "That seems like a fair compromise. Let's draft the agreement."
More Examples
  • I take your point regarding the deadline, but quality cannot be compromised.
  • Shall we meet halfway on the price?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I accept nothing from your proposal. (Too rigid for B2 collaborative tasks!)
