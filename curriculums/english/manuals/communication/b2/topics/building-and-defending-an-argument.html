Building & Defending an Argument

Structuring opinion + evidence + pre-empting counter-arguments.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can build persuasive spoken and written arguments, acknowledge objections, and defend your position.

🧩 Argumentation Chunks

The fundamental argument for X is... Evidence clearly demonstrates that... Critics may argue that... however,... Consequently, it stands to reason that...

🗺️ Argumentation Architecture

1
Thesis statement: "Renewable energy investment is essential for economic stability."
2
Pre-empt counter-argument: "Skeptics often point to high initial installation costs."
3
Rebuttal & Conclusion: "However, long-term operational savings far outweigh setup expenditure. Consequently, transition is necessary."
More Examples
  • While some claim that AI threatens jobs, evidence suggests it creates new technical sectors.
  • Consequently, we must prioritize digital literacy in schools.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌My argument is true because I am right. (Always provide empirical or logical evidence!)
