Hedging & Diplomatic Language

Softening claims, disagreeing without confrontation, and controlling register.

🧩 Diplomatic Hedging Chunks

I might be wrong, but... It could be argued that... I'm not entirely convinced that... To some extent I agree, although...

🗺️ Diplomatic Map: Soften ➔ State ➔ Leave Room

1
Soften opening: "I might be wrong, but I'm not entirely convinced that this strategy will work."
2
State point: "It could be argued that our target audience prefers digital channels."
3
Leave open qualifier: "...though I could be missing something in the data."
More Examples
  • I see where you're coming from, but I'm not entirely convinced.
  • That's one way of looking at it, although another perspective exists.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌You are wrong about that. (Too blunt for professional/academic contexts!)
tap to see the correct form
✅I see where you're coming from, but I'm not entirely convinced.
