CEFR B1 Future Certainty

Talking About Future Plans & Predictions

Expressing degrees of certainty (sure to, might, there's a chance) in natural conversation.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express varying degrees of likelihood and certainty regarding future ambitions.

🧩 Building Blocks: Certainty Chips

I'm bound / sure to... It's highly likely that... There's a good chance that... I doubt that...

🗺️ Degree of Certainty Spectrum

1
100% Certainty: "He is bound to win the award; he performed brilliantly."
2
70% Likelihood: "There's a good chance that prices will drop next month."
3
Low Likelihood: "I doubt that the flight will be on time given the weather."
More Examples
  • It's highly likely that we'll move house next year.
  • I doubt that he will accept the offer.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I am sure 100% will rain.
tap to see the correct form
✅I'm sure it will rain. / It's bound to rain.
