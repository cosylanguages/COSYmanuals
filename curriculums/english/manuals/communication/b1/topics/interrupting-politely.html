Interrupting Politely

Interjecting smoothly in meetings to seek clarification or add key points.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can interrupt ongoing discussions respectfully without appearing aggressive or rude.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

Sorry to interrupt, but could I just clarify...? May I quickly jump in here for a second? Before we move on, could I add something? If I could just interject for a moment...

🗺️ Interrupting Politely Dialogue Flow

1
Polite Interjection: "Sorry to interrupt, but could I just clarify the deadline for Phase 1?"
2
Acknowledge Speaker: "Thank you, John. I just wanted to highlight that the client requested an update."
3
Yielding Back: "Thanks for letting me clarify that. Please go ahead with your presentation."
More Authentic Examples
  • May I quickly jump in here? We actually addressed that point in yesterday's meeting.
  • Before we move on to the next topic, I'd like to share a quick update.
