Giving Presentations

Structuring clear presentations with opening hooks, transitions, and conclusions.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can open, structure, and conclude a formal presentation using professional signposting language.

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

Today I'd like to talk to you about... First, I will outline..., followed by... Turning now to our next point... To sum up, the key takeaway is...

🗺️ Giving Presentations Dialogue Flow

1
Opening & Purpose: "Good morning everyone. Today I'd like to talk to you about our new project timeline."
2
Signposting Main Body: "First, I will outline our current progress, followed by a review of our budget."
3
Concluding: "To sum up, our project is on track, and the key takeaway is that early testing saved time."
More Authentic Examples
  • As you can see on this chart, our performance has improved significantly.
  • Thank you for your time; I'd be happy to take any questions now.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Today I talk about the report. Also another thing is money.
tap to see structured signposting language
✅Today I'd like to talk to you about our quarterly performance.
