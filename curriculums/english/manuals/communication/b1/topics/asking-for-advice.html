Asking for Advice

Seeking guidance, recommendations, and expert opinions on choices.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can ask for guidance and recommendations effectively when facing decisions.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

What would you recommend I do in this situation? I'd really appreciate your advice on how to handle... If you were in my shoes, what steps would you take? Do you think it would be wise to...?

🗺️ Asking for Advice Dialogue Flow

1
Present Dilemma: "I'm trying to decide whether to accept the new transfer offer or stay in my current department."
2
Direct Advice Request: "What would you recommend I do in this situation?"
3
Specific Perspective: "If you were in my shoes, would you speak with HR first?"
More Authentic Examples
  • I'd really appreciate your advice on how to negotiate this contract extension.
  • Do you think it would be wise to consult a financial advisor before investing?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Tell me what to do right now.
tap to see advice-seeking frames
✅What would you recommend I do in this situation? I'd appreciate your advice.
