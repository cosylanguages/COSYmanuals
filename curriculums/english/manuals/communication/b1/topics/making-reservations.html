Making Reservations

Booking venues, confirming availability, and specifying requirements.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can make formal reservations for venues, hotels, or restaurants and specify special requests.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

I'd like to inquire about booking a conference hall... Do you have any availability for a party of six on...? We have a few dietary requirements, specifically... Could you confirm the cancellation policy for this booking?

🗺️ Making Reservations Dialogue Flow

1
Initial Inquiry: "Good afternoon. I'd like to inquire about booking a conference hall for October 15th."
2
Check Details: "Do you have availability for a party of thirty, including catering services?"
3
Special Request: "We also have a few dietary requirements, specifically two vegetarian meals."
More Authentic Examples
  • Could you send through a written confirmation of our reservation via email?
  • What is your cancellation policy in case our schedule changes?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Give me a room on Friday.
tap to see formal reservation phrases
✅I'd like to inquire about booking a room. Do you have availability for Friday?
