Describing & Comparing a Photo (Long Turn)

Sustaining a 1-minute visual description, speculating about context, and structuring long turns.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can sustain a continuous 1-minute description of visual scenes and speculate about hidden details.

🧩 Building Blocks: Photo Description Chips

In the foreground / background,... On the left / right-hand side,... They seem to be... Judging by their clothes, I'd say...

🗺️ 1-Minute Photo Description Map

1
Overall Scene: "This photo shows a group of colleagues working outdoors in a park."
2
Foreground & Details: "In the foreground, a woman with laptop is smiling, while two men behind her examine plans."
3
Speculation & Atmosphere: "Judging by the sunny weather, it looks like summer, and they seem very relaxed."
More Examples
  • It looks as if they're celebrating a successful launch.
  • In the background, I can see tall glass office buildings.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌In the photo people sits on chairs.
tap to see the correct form
✅In the photo, people are sitting on chairs. (Always use Present Continuous for photo actions!)
