Giving Instructions

Sequencing step-by-step guidance, procedures, and clear directions.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can deliver clear, sequential instructions for technical or administrative procedures.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

First, ensure X is connected; then, proceed to Y. Once you have completed step one, make sure to... Before continuing, verify that... Finally, don't forget to save your changes.

🗺️ Giving Instructions Dialogue Flow

1
Step One: "First, ensure the printer is connected to the Wi-Fi network; then, open the settings panel."
2
Step Two: "Once you have opened settings, verify that the IP address matches your network."
3
Final Step: "Finally, don't forget to run a test print to confirm the setup."
More Authentic Examples
  • Before continuing, verify that all safety switches are turned off.
  • Once you've filled out the form, submit it directly to the portal.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Do this then that then do another thing.
