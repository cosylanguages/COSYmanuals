Negotiating a Joint Decision

🧩 Building Blocks: Collaborative Negotiation Chips

What do you think about option X? That's a valid point, however... Wouldn't it be better to...? Shall we make a final choice?

🗺️ PET Collaborative Speaking Task Flow

1
Open evaluation: "Let's look at the ideas on the card. What do you think about getting a gift card?"
2
Trade-off exploration: "A gift card is useful, but it feels a bit impersonal. Wouldn't a personalized book be better?"
3
Consensus building: "I see what you mean. The book feels much more thoughtful. So shall we go with the book?"
More Examples
  • That's a strong point, but we need to consider the cost.
  • So we're agreed on the weekend trip then!
