🧩 Presentation Signposting Chips

Today I'd like to talk about... To begin with,... Moving on to my next point,... To summarize main points,...

🗺️ Mini-Presentation Structure

1
Hook & Overview: "Today I'd like to talk about why public parks matter in modern cities."
2
Signpost Points: "To begin with, parks boost mental health. Moving on to my second point, they encourage community events."
3
Conclude & Q&A: "To summarize, urban green spaces are essential. Thank you, and I'd be happy to answer any questions."
