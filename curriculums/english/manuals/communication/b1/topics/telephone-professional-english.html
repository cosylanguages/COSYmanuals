Telephone Professional English

Managing phone calls, transferring lines, and handling audio issues politely.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can conduct professional telephone interactions, connect callers, and manage call flow smoothly.

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

Could you put me through to the accounts department? May I ask who is calling, please? Hold the line, please, while I transfer your call. I'm afraid the line is bad; could you repeat that?

🗺️ Telephone Professional English Dialogue Flow

1
Call Request: "Good morning. Could you put me through to the accounts department, please?"
2
Identify Caller: "Certainly! May I ask who is calling so I can inform Mr. Davis?"
3
Transfer Call: "Hold the line, please, while I connect you to his extension."
More Authentic Examples
  • I'm afraid her line is currently busy. Would you like to leave a message?
  • Thank you for calling. Have a great rest of your day!
