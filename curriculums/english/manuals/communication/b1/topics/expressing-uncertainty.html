Expressing Uncertainty

Hesitating politely and qualifying statements when details are unconfirmed.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express doubt or lack of full conviction politely without sounding unprepared.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

I'm not entirely convinced that... As far as I'm aware, but I'd need to check... It's difficult to say for certain, but... I have some reservations about...

🗺️ Expressing Uncertainty Dialogue Flow

1
Polite Hesitation: "I'm not entirely convinced that this timeline is realistic for the team."
2
Qualifying Knowledge: "As far as I'm aware, the budget was approved, but I'd need to double-check."
3
Balanced Doubt: "It's difficult to say for certain until we receive the final report."
More Authentic Examples
  • I have some reservations about launching before testing is complete.
  • Off the top of my head, I'd estimate around 50 attendees, though I'm not 100% sure.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I don't know anything about this topic.
