Handling Complaints

De-escalating issues professionally and offering immediate solutions.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can acknowledge customer complaints empathetically and offer clear, professional resolutions.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused... Thank you for bringing this matter to our attention. I will personally look into this issue right away. To make things right, we would like to offer you...

🗺️ Handling Complaints Dialogue Flow

1
Empathy & Apology: "I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused with your order delay."
2
Acknowledge Issue: "Thank you for bringing this matter to our attention. I understand how frustrating this must be."
3
Propose Solution: "I will personally make sure your replacement is dispatched today free of charge."
More Authentic Examples
  • I take full responsibility for this misunderstanding and will resolve it immediately.
  • Please rest assured that we are taking steps to prevent this error from happening again.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌It's not my fault, you should have checked earlier.
tap to see the professional resolution phrase
✅I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused, let me look into this right away.
