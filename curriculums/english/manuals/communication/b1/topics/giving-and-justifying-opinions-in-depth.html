Giving & Justifying Opinions in Depth

Structuring opinion + reason + concrete example beyond basic one-line views.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can present well-supported opinions backed by logical justifications and real-life examples.

🧩 Building Blocks: Opinion Support Chunks

From my perspective,... The main reason I say this is... For instance, last month... That's why I strongly believe...

🗺️ Opinion Justification Pyramid

1
State view: "From my perspective, working remotely is much more productive."
2
Justify reason: "The main reason I say this is that you eliminate commuting stress."
3
Illustrate example: "For instance, I save two hours every day that I can spend on exercise."
More Examples
  • As far as I'm concerned, public transport should be free in cities.
  • For example, many European capitals have already implemented low-emission zones.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I think X because yes. (Always elaborate with a reason!)
tap to see the correct form
✅I think X because it saves time, for instance...
