Narrating Complex Past Experiences

🧩 Building Blocks: Complex Narrative Chunks

I was just about to... when... It turned out that... By the time we arrived,... What made it memorable was...

🗺️ Complex Storytelling Architecture

1
Background setup: "I was travelling through northern Italy two summers ago."
2
Prior background (Past Perfect): "I had booked a small hotel in Verona, but my train was severely delayed."
3
Inciting action (Past Continuous + Past Simple): "While I was waiting at the platform, a stranger offered to share a taxi."
4
Resolution & reflection: "It turned out we were staying in the same street! We ended up having dinner together."
More Examples
  • By the time the rescue team arrived, the storm had passed.
  • What made the trip memorable was how friendly everyone was.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌When I arrived, the train already left.
tap to see the correct form
✅When I arrived, the train had already left.
