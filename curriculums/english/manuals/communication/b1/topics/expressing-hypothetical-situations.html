Expressing Hypothetical Situations

Speaking hypothetically in natural conversation (if I were you, imagine if, what if).

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss imaginary scenarios, hypothetical choices, and conditional outcomes.

🧩 Building Blocks: Hypothetical Chunks

Imagine if we could... What if you had the chance to...? If that happened, I'd probably... Supposing we took a year off,...

🗺️ Hypothetical Scenario Map

1
Prompt Scenario: "Imagine if you won the lottery tomorrow!"
2
Hypothetical Response: "If that happened, I'd probably buy a house in Cornwall and travel."
3
Follow-up query: "Would you quit your job straight away?" — "I might reduce my hours!"
More Examples
  • Supposing we moved to Spain, how would we handle the language?
  • What if the flight gets cancelled? We'd take a train instead.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌If I win the lottery yesterday, I would buy a car.
tap to see the correct form
✅If I won the lottery, I would buy a car. (Second conditional: past verb + would!)
