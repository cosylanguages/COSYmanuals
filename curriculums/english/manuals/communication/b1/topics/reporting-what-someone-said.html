CEFR B1 Conversational Reporting

Reporting What Someone Said

Natural reported speech in conversation (he mentioned that..., she claimed that...).

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can relay news, conversations, and instructions from other people accurately in speech.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.

🧩 Building Blocks: Reporting Chunks

He mentioned that... She mentioned she was going to... According to the manager,... They warned us that...

🗺️ Reported Speech Flow

1
Original Statement: Mark says: "I am taking the train tomorrow morning."
2
Spoken Report: "Mark mentioned that he was taking the morning train."
3
Pass on detail: "He said he had already bought his ticket online."
More Examples
  • According to the news, the storm will hit at midnight.
  • She warned us that traffic was heavy on the motorway.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌He told that he was busy.
tap to see the correct form
✅He said that he was busy. / He told me that he was busy. ('Tell' needs a personal object!)
