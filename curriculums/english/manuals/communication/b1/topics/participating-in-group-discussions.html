Participating in Group Discussions

Building on colleagues' ideas, bringing in quiet participants, and guiding discussion.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can participate actively in group discussions by piggybacking on ideas and encouraging team input.

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

I'd like to echo what Sarah just said... Building on John's point, we could also... I'd love to hear what David thinks about this. That brings us back to our main topic...

🗺️ Participating in Group Discussions Dialogue Flow

1
Build on Idea: "I'd like to echo what Sarah just said about improving our onboarding workflow."
2
Add Contribution: "Building on her point, we could also record short video tutorials for new hires."
3
Include Others: "I'd love to hear what David thinks about this from a technical standpoint."
More Authentic Examples
  • That's a fascinating point, and it aligns closely with our customer feedback.
  • That brings us back to our main question regarding budget allocation.
