Making Suggestions

Proposing creative ideas, alternatives, and collaborative action plans.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can offer constructive suggestions and propose new approaches during discussions.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

Why don't we try approaching this from a different angle? How about we conduct a quick survey among clients? It might be a good idea to set up a shared document. I suggest we schedule a brief catch-up meeting tomorrow.

🗺️ Making Suggestions Dialogue Flow

1
Propose New Angle: "Why don't we try approaching this problem from a different angle?"
2
Concrete Idea: "How about we conduct a quick survey among existing clients to gather feedback?"
3
Action Step: "It might be a good idea to draft the questions by this afternoon."
More Authentic Examples
  • I suggest we schedule a brief catch-up meeting tomorrow to finalize the details.
  • What if we divide the research tasks across the three team members?
