CEFR B1 Opinions & Discussions

Expressing Agreement

Validating others' opinions enthusiastically or with nuanced agreement.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can agree strongly or partially with colleagues' views during discussions.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

I couldn't agree more with your assessment. You've hit the nail on the head regarding... That's a very valid point; I share your view. I am completely on the same page as you.

🗺️ Expressing Agreement Dialogue Flow

1
Strong Agreement: "I couldn't agree more with your assessment of our market strategy."
2
Highlight Shared View: "You've hit the nail on the head regarding our customer retention challenges."
3
Reaffirm Alignment: "I am completely on the same page as you; let's move forward with this plan."
More Authentic Examples
  • That's a very valid point; I share your view on simplifying our product line.
  • I second that proposal wholeheartedly!
