Networking at Conferences

Initiating conversations, exchanging contacts, and establishing business rapport.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can break the ice at professional events, discuss professional background, and exchange contact details.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

What line of work are you in? Are you attending any particular sessions today? Here's my business card; let's connect on LinkedIn! It was a pleasure meeting you; enjoy the conference!

🗺️ Networking at Conferences Dialogue Flow

1
Icebreaker Question: "Hello! Is this your first time at this conference? What line of work are you in?"
2
Sharing Background: "I'm in software product design. Are you attending any particular keynote sessions today?"
3
Contact Exchange: "That sounds fascinating! Here's my business card; let me connect with you on LinkedIn."
More Authentic Examples
  • What brings you to this year's summit?
  • It was a pleasure meeting you; I look forward to keeping in touch!
⚠️ Common mistakes
