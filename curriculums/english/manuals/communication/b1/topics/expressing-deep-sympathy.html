Expressing Deep Sympathy

Offering genuine condolences and support during difficult moments.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can offer sincere sympathy and condolences during times of grief or personal hardship.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

Please accept my deepest condolences... My thoughts and prayers are with you and your family. I was deeply saddened to hear about your loss. Please know that I am here for you if you need anything.

🗺️ Expressing Deep Sympathy Dialogue Flow

1
Sincere Condolence: "Please accept my deepest condolences during this difficult time."
2
Personal Support: "I was deeply saddened to hear about your loss; she was a wonderful person."
3
Offer Assistance: "Please know that I am here for you if you need anything at all."
More Authentic Examples
  • My thoughts are with you and your family as you go through this sad loss.
  • Words cannot express how sorry I am for your loss.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌That's life, don't worry about it.
