Asking for Permission

Requesting authorization politely in formal or professional environments.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can seek permission politely using formal modal verb structures.
🎯 Ready to test your skills? Reinforce this topic with interactive practice drills.
🎯 Practice this →

🧩 Building Blocks: Key Functional Chips

Would there be any objection if I...? May I have your permission to...? Would it be alright if I took tomorrow morning off? Do you mind if I record this video call?

🗺️ Asking for Permission Dialogue Flow

1
Formal Request: "Would there be any objection if I left slightly early today for a personal appointment?"
2
Explain Reason: "I have completed all my assigned tasks for the day."
3
Confirm Approval: "Thank you! I will ensure I am reachable by email if anything urgent arises."
More Authentic Examples
  • May I have your permission to access the archived financial records?
  • Do you mind if I record this webinar for absent team members?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I am leaving early today, bye.
tap to see polite permission structures
✅Would there be any objection if I left slightly early today?
