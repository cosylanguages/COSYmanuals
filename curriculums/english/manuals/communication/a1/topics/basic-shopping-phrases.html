CEFR A1 Retail Interactions

Basic Shopping Phrases

Asking about sizes, colors, prices, and paying at retail stores.

💬 How do I ask for help and buy things in a store?

Retail exchanges require simple questions about prices, sizes, and payment methods.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can ask for assistance, check prices/sizes, try on clothing, and pay by cash or card.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Shopping Chips

I'm just looking, thanks! Do you have this in medium / blue? Can I try this on? How much is this shirt? I'll take it! Can I pay by card? Thank you, bye!

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "I wish to acquire this garment with money."
✅ Natural: "I'll take this! Can I pay by card?"
