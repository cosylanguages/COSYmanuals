CEFR A1 Question Systems

Asking Simple Questions

Using Who, What, Where, When, Why and How to gather information.

💬 How do I ask basic questions to find out what I need?

Questions are the key to keeping conversations active and gathering vital information in daily life.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can ask open-ended WH- questions and Yes/No questions about people, places, times, and prices.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Question Chips

What is this? / What's your job? Where is the bus station? When does the store open? Who is that? How much is this? Do you speak English? Ah, I see! / Thank you!

🗺️ Question Flow Map

1
Polite Opener: "Excuse me, could you help me?"
2
Ask Question: "Where is the nearest supermarket?"
3
Information Given: "It's just down the street on the left."
4
Acknowledge & Thank: "Great, thank you so much!"

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "I desire to know where the station is located."
✅ Natural: "Excuse me, where's the station?"
