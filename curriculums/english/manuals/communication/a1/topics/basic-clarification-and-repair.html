CEFR A1 Repair Strategies

Basic Clarification & Repair

Asking people to repeat, speak slower, or spell words when you don't understand.

💬 What do I do when I don't catch what someone said?

Don't panic when you miss a word! Every learner needs immediate tools to request repetition and repair dialogue.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can ask someone to speak slower, repeat a phrase, spell a word, or confirm meaning.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Repair Chips

Sorry, could you repeat that, please? Could you speak a bit slower, please? What does [word] mean? How do you spell that? Ah, I understand now! Thank you.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "What? I am deaf to your words!"
✅ Natural: "Sorry, I didn't catch that. Could you say that again?"
