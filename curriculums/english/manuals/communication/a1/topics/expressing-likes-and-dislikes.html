CEFR A1 Personal Expression

Expressing Preferences, Likes & Dislikes

Talking about favorite things, food, hobbies, and things you don't enjoy.

💬 How do I share what I like and dislike?

Sharing personal taste helps build friendships and find common interests with others.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express strong likes ("I love..."), mild preferences ("I prefer..."), and mild dislikes ("I don't really like...").

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Preference Chips

I love playing football / reading. I really like Italian food. I prefer coffee over tea. Me too! / Really? I don't really like cold weather. What's your favorite movie / food?

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "I possess hatred towards rain."
✅ Natural: "I don't really like rainy days."

1. How do you politely say you don't enjoy spicy food?
"I don't really like..." cushions dislikes politely.
