Asking for & Giving Directions

Navigating towns and cities, asking where things are, and giving basic spatial instructions.

💬 How do I ask for directions when I'm lost in a new city?

Simple navigation phrases ensure you can find places confidently and help visitors find their way.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can ask where landmarks are, understand simple directions (straight on, left, right, next to), and thank passersby.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Direction Chips

Excuse me, where is the hotel? Is it far from here? Go straight ahead. Turn left / Turn right. It's next to the bank / opposite the park. Ah, okay! Got it! Thank you so much! Have a nice day!

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "Indicate to me the path to the museum."
✅ Natural: "How do I get to the museum?"
