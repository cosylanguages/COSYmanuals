CEFR A1 Telephony

Simple Phone & Messaging Phrases

Answering simple calls, checking who is speaking, and sending quick chat messages.

💬 How do I answer the phone and send basic text messages?

Handling phone calls without video requires clear identity markers and simple confirmation phrases.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can answer calls ("Hello, this is..."), identify callers, leave short messages, and text arrival times.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Phone Chips

Hello, this is [Name] speaking. Who is speaking, please? Can I call you back in 5 minutes? I'm on my way! / I'm running late. No problem! See you soon. Talk to you later! Bye!

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "I am Alex on the smartphone line."
✅ Natural: "Hi, this is Alex."
