CEFR A1 Speech Acts

Greetings & Introductions

Starting a conversation, introducing yourself, and greeting others warmly.

💬 How do I greet someone and introduce myself naturally?

Greeting people smoothly creates immediate social connection and sets a friendly, confident tone for any exchange.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can open everyday exchanges, give your name, ask someone's name, and say where you are from.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Turn-Taking Chips

Hello! / Hi there! Good morning / Good afternoon! My name is [Name]. I'm from [City / Country]. What's your name? Where are you from? Nice to meet you! Have a great day! / Bye!

🗺️ Interaction Flow Map

1
Opening: "Hi there! I'm Sarah."
2
Acknowledge & React: "Hello Sarah! Nice to meet you. I'm Marco."
3
Follow-up Ask: "Nice to meet you too! Where are you from, Marco?"
4
Closing Warmth: "I'm from Italy! Well, have a nice day!" — "Thanks, you too!"

👀 Notice: Spoken Politeness

Notice how native English speakers pair a greeting ("Hi") directly with a personal name or "Nice to meet you". Saying only "My name is X" without a smile or greeting feels distant.

🧠 Think in Conversation

Focus on social warmth over perfect grammar. A short "Hi! I'm John, nice to meet you!" is 100% effective in English-speaking environments.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "How do you do? I am called Alexander."
✅ Natural: "Hi! I'm Alex. Great to meet you!"

💡 Pragmatic Memory Trick

Remember the 3-Step Handshake: Hi ➔ Name ➔ Nice to meet you!

💬 Sample Exchange

A: Hi there! I'm Emma. What's your name?
B: Hello Emma! I'm Kenji. Nice to meet you!
A: Nice to meet you too, Kenji! Where are you from?
B: I'm from Japan! And you?
