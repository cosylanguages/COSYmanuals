Ordering Food & Drinks

Requesting items at cafes and restaurants politely using 'I'd like...' and 'Can I have...'.

💬 How do I order food and drinks without sounding rude?

Ordering at cafes and restaurants is one of the most frequent real-world communicative tasks.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can order coffee, meals, ask for the bill, and express dietary preferences.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Ordering Chips

Hi, can I have a coffee, please? I'd like a sandwich, please. How much is that? Could we have the bill, please? That's delicious! / Thank you! Keep the change! / Bye!

🗺️ Cafe Ordering Flow Map

1
Staff Ask: "Hi! What can I get for you today?"
2
Order Request: "Can I have a cappuccino and a croissant, please?"
3
Clarify & Pay: "Have in or take away?" — "Take away, please. How much is it?"
4
Transaction Close: "That's $6.50." — "Here you go. Thanks!"

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "I want one coffee."
✅ Natural: "Can I get a coffee, please?"
