Telling a Simple Story

Narrating past events in clear sequence (first, then, after that, finally, in the end).

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can recount past events in chronological order using clear transition markers.

🧩 Building Blocks: Time Sequence Chips

First of all,... Then / After that,... Suddenly,... Finally / In the end,...

🗺️ Story Map Architecture

1
Set the scene: "Last weekend, I went to London with my friend."
2
First event: "First, we checked into our hotel near the station."
3
Development: "Then, we walked around the market and bought lunch."
4
Resolution: "In the end, we took an evening train back home."
More Examples
  • First, we booked the tickets online. After that, we met at the station.
  • Suddenly, the train stopped in the middle of a tunnel.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌At the end we came home. (Use 'In the end' for story outcomes!)
tap to see the correct form
✅In the end, we came home.
