🧩 Building Blocks: Transaction Chips

I'd like a ticket to..., please. Could I have the bill, please? Do you accept credit cards? Keep the change!

🗺️ Restaurant Transaction Flow

1
Order: "Could I get a sparkling water and the chicken salad?"
2
Confirmation / Request: "Sure! Anything else?" — "No, that's all, thank you."
3
Payment: "Could we have the bill, please?" — "Card or cash?"
More Examples
  • Two return tickets to Oxford, please.
  • Could I try this jacket on in a medium?
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Give me a coffee. (Too blunt!)
tap to see the correct form
