Making Plans Together

Suggesting, negotiating time/place, accepting/declining, and changing plans.

🧩 Building Blocks: Planning Phrases

Shall we meet at...? How about Saturday afternoon? That sounds great! I'm afraid I'm busy then.

🗺️ Planning Dialogue Flow

1
Proposal: "Shall we get coffee this Friday?"
2
Constraint / Alternative: "I'm busy on Friday. How about Saturday morning instead?"
3
Agreement & Details: "Saturday morning works for me. Let's meet at 10 AM at Costa."
More Examples
  • Why don't we grab dinner after work?
  • That suits me perfectly. See you then!
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Shall we to meet at 5?
tap to see the correct form
✅Shall we meet at 5? (Shall + bare infinitive!)
