Giving Simple Advice

Should/could-based suggestions, and responding empathetically to problems.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can offer helpful advice and suggest practical solutions to common daily issues.

🧩 Building Blocks: Advice Chips

If I were you, I'd... You should definitely try... Why don't you talk to...? That's a good idea!

🗺️ Advice Exchange Map

1
Stating Problem: "I'm really tired and I can't focus on my work."
2
Offering Advice: "You should take a short 15-minute walk outside."
3
Reaction: "That's a good idea. I'll do that right now!"
More Examples
  • You could ask your manager for extra time.
  • If I were you, I'd go to bed early tonight.
