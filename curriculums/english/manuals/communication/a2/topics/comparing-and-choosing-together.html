Comparing & Choosing Together

Comparing options, evaluating pros and cons, and reaching a joint decision.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can evaluate options with a conversation partner and reach a mutual agreement.

🧩 Building Blocks: Decision Chips

Which one is better? This option is cheaper than... On the one hand... but... So, let's go for option B!

🗺️ Joint Decision Flow

1
Open Comparison: "Which option do you prefer: train or bus?"
2
Evaluate: "The train is faster, but the bus is much cheaper."
3
Conclude: "Since we have time, let's take the bus then!"
More Examples
  • I think the hotel is more comfortable than the hostel.
  • So we agree on taking the afternoon session!
