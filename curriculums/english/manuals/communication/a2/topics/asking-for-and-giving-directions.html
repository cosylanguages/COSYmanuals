Asking For & Giving Directions

Wayfinding dialogue, clarifying a route, and checking understanding.

🧩 Building Blocks: Direction Chips

Excuse me, is there a bank near here? Go straight ahead for two blocks. Turn left at the traffic lights. It's on your right, opposite the pharmacy.

🗺️ Wayfinding Exchange

1
Opening: "Excuse me, how do I get to the central station?"
2
Instructions: "Go straight down this road, take the second left, and keep going until the roundabout."
3
Confirmation: "So, second left and then straight to the roundabout?" — "Exact!"
More Examples
  • The museum is between the library and the park.
  • Take the first turning on your right.
