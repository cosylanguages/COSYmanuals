🧩 Building Blocks: Descriptive Phrase Chips

He looks like... She's wearing... It's a cozy, quiet place with... What strikes me about it is...

🗺️ Description Structure

1
Overview: "My hometown is a small coastal town in western Wales."
2
Specific Features: "It has narrow streets, old stone houses, and a busy harbor."
3
Personal Feeling: "What I love about it is how friendly the local people are."
More Examples
  • He looks like his older brother, tall with short brown hair.
  • The café was cozy and filled with light.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌He looks tall and thin. (Use 'looks like' for nouns, 'looks' for adjectives!)
tap to see the correct form
✅He looks tall and thin. / He looks like an actor.
