15.2 Sports & Physical Exercise (10 terms)

basketball noun

A game where two teams try to throw a ball through a high net.

"He plays basketball every Wednesday."

football noun

A game played by two teams of eleven players who kick a ball.

"They are playing football in the park."

gym noun

A room or building with equipment for doing physical exercise.

"I go to the gym three times a week."

running noun

Moving fast on foot as a sport or exercise.

"He goes running every morning in the park."

"Swimming is an excellent full-body exercise."

tennis noun

A game played by two or four players with rackets and a ball.

"Do you want to play tennis?"

volleyball noun

A game in which two teams hit a ball over a high net.

"We played beach volleyball in summer."

yoga noun

A system of exercises for body and breath control.

"Yoga helps reduce stress."
