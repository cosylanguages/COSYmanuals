ConceptDefinition
nudge theoryBehavioural interventions altering choices without prohibiting options.
systemic riskPossibility of total financial collapse across an entire sector.
price elasticitySensitivity of demand relative to changes in price.
Examples
  • Central banks intervened with monetary policy to mitigate systemic risk.
  • Behavioural economists design nudges to encourage timely tax payments.
