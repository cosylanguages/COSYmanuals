Multilateral diplomacy, international sanctions, territorial integrity, and exercising soft power

Diplomatic TermStrategic Meaning
soft powerAbility to co-opt and attract through culture and values rather than coercion.
territorial integrityPrinciple that nation-states should not promote border alterations in others.
multilateral treatyBinding agreement negotiated among three or more sovereign states.
Examples
  • Diplomats exercise soft power and negotiate multilateral treaties.
  • International sanctions were imposed following violations of territorial integrity.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌ Soft power refers to military naval bombardments.
