Cognitive Biases, Mental Disorders & Therapy

Cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), confirmation bias, cognitive dissonance, and resilience

Psychological ConceptMechanism
confirmation biasTendency to search for and favor information reinforcing pre-existing beliefs.
cognitive dissonanceMental discomfort experienced when holding contradictory beliefs simultaneously.
cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)Psychotherapy restructuring maladaptive thought patterns.
Examples
  • Cognitive behavioral therapy helps patients restructure maladaptive thought patterns.
  • Confirmation bias leads individuals to ignore contradictory evidence.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌ Cognitive dissonance occurs when everyone agrees perfectly.
tap to see the correct form
✅ Cognitive dissonance arises from internal tension between conflicting beliefs.
