TermDomain
ratifyFormal adoption and legal enactment of a multilateral treaty.
sovereigntySupreme authority of a state to govern its territory.
unconstitutionalViolating fundamental constitutional principles.
Examples
  • Supreme Court ruled that the executive decree was unconstitutional.
  • Parliament debated whether treaty terms infringe upon national sovereignty.
