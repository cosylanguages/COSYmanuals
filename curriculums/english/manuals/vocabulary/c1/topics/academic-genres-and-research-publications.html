Academic Genres & Research Publications

Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, white papers, monographs, and peer review terminology

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can categorize academic publication genres and discuss peer-reviewed research findings.

Core TermCategoryDefinition / Context
systematic reviewGenreExhaustive synthesis of literature addressing a specific research question.
meta-analysisMethodStatistical combination of results from multiple empirical studies.
monographPublicationDetailed scholarly treatise on a single specialized subject.
substantiateVerbProvide empirical evidence to support or prove a claim.
Examples
  • The peer review panel commended the exhaustive literature synthesis.
  • Researchers must substantiate their hypotheses with empirical data.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌ The paper substantiated that no evidence was found.
