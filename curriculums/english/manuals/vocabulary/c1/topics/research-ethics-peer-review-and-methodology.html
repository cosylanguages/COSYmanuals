TermRoleContext
Institutional Review Board (IRB)EthicsCommittee approving research involving human subjects.
replication crisisMethodologyMethodological challenge where studies fail to produce identical results upon re-testing.
double-blind studyDesignExperimental setup where neither participants nor researchers know who receives treatment.
Examples
  • Prior to initiating the clinical trial, researchers obtained informed consent.
  • The journal retracted the paper due to undisclosed conflicts of interest.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌ The double-blind trial informed patients of their dosage.
tap to see the correct form
✅ In a double-blind study, neither patients nor administrators know treatment assignments.
