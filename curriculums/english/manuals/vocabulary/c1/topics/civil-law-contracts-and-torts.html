TermMeaning
tortCivil wrong resulting in legal liability.
indemnification clauseContractual agreement transferring financial risk/liability.
compensatory damagesMonetary award compensating plaintiff for losses.
Examples
  • Both parties agreed to an out-of-court settlement including an indemnification clause.
  • The plaintiff filed a lawsuit seeking compensatory damages for gross negligence.
