TermMarket Impact
financial contagionSpread of economic shock from one market across interconnected sectors.
inflation hedgeAsset protecting purchasing power during currency devaluation.
liquidity crisisAcute shortage of cash or easily convertible assets across institutions.
Examples
  • To guard against financial contagion, firms prioritized portfolio diversification.
  • Investors shifted capital into commodities as an inflation hedge.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌ Liquidity crisis means having too much spare cash.
tap to see the correct form
✅ Liquidity crisis implies a severe shortage of available liquid capital.
