BranchFocus
epistemologyStudy of the nature, origin, and limits of human knowledge.
normative ethicsBranch of ethics investigating set standards of right and wrong conduct.
determinismPhilosophical view that all events are completely determined by previously existing causes.
Examples
  • Philosophers engage in epistemological inquiry to determine the limits of rational belief.
  • If actions are causally predetermined, free will becomes subject to metaphysical debate.
