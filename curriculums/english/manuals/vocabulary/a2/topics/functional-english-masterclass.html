SituationKey Functional Set PhrasesExample
Small Talk & Greetings"Lovely to see you!", "How was your weekend?", "Nice weather today!"Lovely to see you! How have you been?
Booking Appointments"I'd like to make an appointment...", "Would Tuesday suit you?"Would Tuesday at 3 PM suit you?
Phone & Video Calls"Hello, this is... speaking", "Could I speak to...", "You're on mute!"Hello, this is Alex speaking.
Customer Complaints"I'm calling to complain about...", "We will offer a full refund."I'm calling to complain about a delayed package.
Clarification & Repetition"Could you repeat that?", "What does this mean?", "Could you speak slower?"Could you speak a bit more slowly, please?
Polite Opinions & Agreement"In my opinion...", "I completely agree!", "I see your point, but..."I completely agree with your suggestion.
