VerbEssential A2 CollocationsExample
MAKEdecision, mistake, friends, progress, money, call, reservationHe made a reservation for two at 8 PM.
DOhomework, washing-up, housework, exercise, favor, your bestPlease do me a favor and hold this door.
TAKEmedicine, break, photo, bus, shower, exam, notes, care ofRemember to take notes during the lecture.
GETmarried, lost, ready, better, home, job, tired, degreeWe got lost in the city center.
HAVEmeeting, shower, party, rest, fun, problem, time, lookLet's have a look at the new catalog.
GIVEadvice, permission, lift, presentation, call, hand, giftCan you give me a hand with these boxes?
KEEPcalm, in touch, promise, secret, fit, going, track ofMake sure to keep in touch while abroad!
PAYattention, bill, by card, in cash, fine, visitYou can pay by card or in cash.
BREAKpromise, law, news, record, habit, down, boneThe car broke down on the highway.
