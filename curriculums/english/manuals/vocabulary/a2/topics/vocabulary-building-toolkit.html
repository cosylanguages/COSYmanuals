Toolkit Core: Front-loaded toolset for A2 Key. Phrasal verbs (*get up, look for, turn on/off, give up*), prefixes (*un-, re-, dis-*), suffixes (*-ful, -less, -able*), and degree adverbs (*fairly, quite, extremely, a bit*).

CategoryWords / CollocationsExample
Phrasal Verbsget up, look for, turn on/off, give upI need to look for my keys before we leave.
