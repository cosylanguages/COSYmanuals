Core Monolingual Prompt

🔥 Today's Fluency Mission: Native reaction speech.
🗣️ 3. Useful Target Sentence Frames

Automated Sentence Starters

  • Frame 1: "That's an intriguing perspective, but I have to respectfully disagree because..."
  • Frame 2: "I see your point regarding digital convenience; however, physical books offer..."
  • Frame 3: "What's particularly interesting about paper books is the tactile experience they provide..."
  • Frame 4: "I've never thought about digital transition as absolute; rather, I see a co-existence..."
  • Frame 5: "To wrap up my reaction, print media possesses an enduring value that screens cannot replace..."
📌 4. Self-Recording & Audit Task

Voice Recording Task

Record a voice note on your phone. Listen back once and check: Did you pause to translate? Re-phrase any hesitation points directly in your target language!
