🎙️ 30-Day Speaking Marathon

Daily Oral Fluency & Confidence Builder

Duration 30 Days
Daily Time 10-15 Minutes
Target Skill Spontaneous Speaking & Pitch
Practice Integration COSYgames Speaking Challenges

🏆 Marathon Goals & Milestones

Transform your speaking confidence by completing one 2-minute oral response or dialogue challenge each day for 30 consecutive days.

  • Days 1-10: Personal Stories & Daily Routines (Overcoming hesitancy)
  • Days 11-20: Expressing Opinions & Debates (Structuring arguments)
  • Days 21-30: Advanced Problem Solving & Storytelling (Mastering flow and intonation)

📅 30-Day Speaking Schedule

  • Day 1: Introduce Yourself Like a Pro

    Record a 60-second elevator pitch about who you are, your hobbies, and your language goals.

    Practice Voice Warmup on COSYgames 🎮
  • Day 2: Describe Your Favorite Place

    Use sensory adjectives (vibrant, serene, bustling) to describe a location you love visiting.

    Play Descriptive Word Challenge on COSYgames 🎮
  • Day 3: Unexpected Situations

    Tell a story about a time when plans changed at the last minute and how you handled it.

    Play Story Spinner on COSYgames 🎮
  • Day 4: Tech in Everyday Life

    Speak for 90 seconds on whether social media brings people closer together or further apart.

    Practice Debates on COSYgames 🎮
  • Day 5: Recommending a Film or Book

    Convince a friend to watch your favorite movie without spoiling the ending.

    Play Pitch Master on COSYgames 🎮
  • Days 6 to 30: Daily Micro-Prompts

    Unlock new daily prompts each morning in your student portal or teacher feedback sessions.
