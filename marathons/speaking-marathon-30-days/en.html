🇬🇧 English Speaking Marathon

30-DAY SPEAK & THINK IN ENGLISH

Eliminate internal mental translation, master circumlocution, and build spontaneous continuous speech.

Phase 1: Direct Concept Association & Inner Monologue (Days 1–10)

Day 1

Live Sensory Stream

Look around your room. Speak aloud for 90 seconds naming everything you see, feel, and hear directly in English without translating in your head.

Day 2

Continuous Self-Commentary

Describe your exact physical actions in real-time for 2 minutes ("I am opening the fridge, picking up a glass of water...").

Day 3

Emotion & Mood Naming

Explain how you feel right now in 3 different ways using English emotional adjectives without consulting a bilingual dictionary.

Day 4

Morning Routine Walkthrough

Narrate your entire morning routine step-by-step in continuous present simple/continuous.

Day 5

Object Description Without Names

Describe 3 household objects without using their actual names (e.g. describe a toaster using shape, function, material).

Phase 2: Rapid Circumlocution & Synonym Chains (Days 11–20)

Day 11

The "Forgotten Word" Rescue Drill

Practice explaining 5 complex items (e.g. microwave, passport, skyscraper) if you forgot the main word using simple English explanations.

Day 12

Fast Synonym Swap

Take 3 basic sentences ("I am happy", "The food was good") and rephrase them instantenously using 3 higher-level synonyms each.

Day 15

Immediate Argument Rephrasing

State an opinion on remote work, then immediately restate the exact same opinion using completely different vocabulary.

Phase 3: Uninterrupted Flow & Spontaneous Monologues (Days 21–30)

Day 21

3-Minute Uninterrupted Monologue

Speak continuously for 180 seconds on a random topic (e.g. space exploration) with ZERO pauses longer than 2 seconds.

Day 30

The Master Unscripted Keynote Speech

Deliver a 5-minute continuous speech on your 1-year personal vision completely spontaneously without any native language thoughts.
