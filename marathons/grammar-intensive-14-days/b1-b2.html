ENGLISH GRAMMAR CONFUSIONS (B1 → B2)

Master intermediate and upper-intermediate grammar choices with precision logic.

Level B1

1. Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Core Difference: Movie background action vs. Sudden interrupting event.

Past Continuous

Background action in progress at a past moment.

Example: I was cooking dinner...

Past Simple

Short completed action that interrupts or happens in sequence.

Example: ...when the phone rang.

💡 Memory Trick: Past Continuous is the movie background scene; Past Simple is the sudden action!
Level B1

2. Used to vs Would (Past Habits & States)

Core Difference: States & Actions vs. Repeated Actions Only.

Used to

Works for both past states (be, live, have) AND repeated past actions.

Example: I used to live in Paris.

Would

Works ONLY for repeated past actions (never for past states!).

Example: Every summer, we would go camping.

💡 Quick Logic: State verb (live, be, have)? → MUST use Used to.
Level B1–B2

3. Conditionals (1st vs 2nd vs 3rd)

Core Difference: Real future vs. Unreal present vs. Unreal past regret.

1st Conditional (Real Future): If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home.

2nd Conditional (Unreal Present): If I won the lottery today, I would buy an island.

3rd Conditional (Unreal Past Regret): If I had studied harder, I would have passed.

💡 Memory Trick: Step back in time for higher irreality: Present → Past → Past Perfect.
Level B2

4. Defining vs Non-Defining Relative Clauses

Core Difference: Essential identity info (no commas, 'that' allowed) vs Extra commentary (commas required, no 'that').

Defining Clause

Identifies which specific person or thing. Essential info, no commas.

Example: The car that I bought is fast.

Non-Defining Clause

Adds extra detail that can be dropped. Hugged by commas!

Example: My brother, who lives in Sydney, is a doctor.

💡 Memory Trick: Commas hug extra information. 'That' hates commas!
