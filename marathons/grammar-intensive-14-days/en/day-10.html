DAY 10 / 14 · GRAMMAR INTENSIVE

Relative Clauses (Defining vs Non-Defining)

Master essential identity info without commas vs. extra detail with commas.

Stage 1 · Elicitation

Compare these two sentences:

1. "My brother who lives in Sydney is a doctor."
2. "My brother, who lives in Sydney, is a doctor."

Notice the commas: In sentence 1, I have multiple brothers, so "who lives in Sydney" specifies which brother. In sentence 2, I have only one brother, and "who lives in Sydney" is just extra detail wrapped in commas!

Stage 2 · The Rule

Core Rule: Essential Identity vs. Extra Detail

Defining Relative Clause

Gives essential information to identify who or what we are talking about. Cannot be removed.

  • NO COMMAS used!
  • 'That' can replace who/which: The car that I bought is fast.

Non-Defining Relative Clause

Gives extra non-essential information. The sentence makes complete sense without it.

  • HUGGED BY COMMAS!
  • NEVER use 'that': Paris, which is the capital of France, is beautiful.
💡 Memory Trick: Essential info = No commas, 'that' allowed! Extra bonus info = Commas hug it, 'that' is FORBIDDEN!
Stage 3 · Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: In "My sister, who is a lawyer, visited me," can we remove "who is a lawyer" without changing who visited me?
    Answer: Yes! It is extra information surrounded by commas.
  2. Question: In "The students who cheated failed the test," did ALL the students fail?
    Answer: No! Only the specific students who cheated failed.
  3. Question: Can we write "London, that is on the River Thames, is big"?
    Answer: No! Non-defining clauses with commas CANNOT use 'that' (must use 'which').
  4. Question: When can relative pronouns (who/which/that) be omitted?
    Answer: In defining clauses when the pronoun is the OBJECT of the verb (e.g., "The movie [that] we watched").
Stage 4 · Guided & Free Practice

Complete the Sentences (who / which / whose / where / that) & add commas if needed

1. The woman _____ lives next door is a professional musician.
2. Amazon _____ was founded by Jeff Bezos _____ is now a global giant.
3. This is the restaurant _____ we had our first date.
4. The boy _____ bike was stolen reported it to the police.
5. My smartphone _____ I bought last week _____ takes amazing photos.
🔍 Click to Reveal Answer Key & Explanations
  1. who / that — Defining clause (essential info identifying the woman); no commas.
  2. , which ... , — Non-defining clause (Amazon is a proper noun, extra info needs commas and 'which').
  3. where — Relative adverb denoting location.
  4. whose — Relative possessive pronoun ("his bike was stolen").
  5. , which ... , — Non-defining extra info wrapped in commas (or defining if specifying among multiple phones).
