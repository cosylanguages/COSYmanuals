Reported Speech & Backshifting Rules

Master converting direct quotes into indirect reported statements with tense shifts, pronoun adjustments, and time reference updates.

Stage 1 · Elicitation

Compare Direct Speech with Reported Speech:

Direct Quote: Mark said, "I am working late today."
Reported: Mark said that he was working late that day.

Notice the 3 shifts: 1) Tense shifts back into the past (am working → was working). 2) Pronoun changes (I → he). 3) Time word updates (today → that day)!

Stage 2 · The Rule

Core Rule: One Step Back in Time (Backshifting)

Tense Backshift Table

  • Present Simple → Past Simple
  • Present Continuous → Past Continuous
  • Past Simple / Present Perfect → Past Perfect
  • Will → Would / Can → Could

Time & Place Adjustments

  • now → then / at that moment
  • today → that day
  • yesterday → the day before / previous day
  • tomorrow → the next day / following day
  • here → there
💡 Memory Trick: Say vs Tell! "He SAID THAT..." (no object person) vs "He TOLD ME THAT..." (requires object person)!
Stage 3 · Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: Can we say "He told that he was tired"?
    Answer: No! "Tell" must have a person object ("He told me that..."). For no object, use "said" ("He said that...").
  2. Question: In reported questions ("Where do you live?"), do we keep question word order or use normal statement word order?
    Answer: Normal statement word order! ("He asked where I lived," NOT "where did I live").
  3. Question: How do we report a Yes/No question ("Are you hungry?")?
    Answer: Using if or whether ("He asked if I was hungry").
  4. Question: If a fact is still true right now (e.g., "The Earth revolves around the Sun"), must we backshift?
    Answer: Backshifting is optional when reporting permanent scientific truths.
Stage 4 · Guided & Free Practice

Convert Direct Speech into Reported Speech

1. Direct: "I have already seen this movie," said Emma.
Reported: Emma said that she _____ that movie.
2. Direct: "We will call you tomorrow," they told us.
Reported: They told us that they _____ us the following day.
3. Direct: "Where are you going?" asked my friend.
Reported: My friend asked me where I _____.
4. Direct: "Do you like sushi?" asked David.
Reported: David asked me _____ I liked sushi.
5. Direct: "Don't open the window," the teacher said to the student.
Reported: The teacher told the student _____ open the window.
🔍 Click to Reveal Answer Key & Explanations
  1. had already seen — Present Perfect backshifts to Past Perfect.
  2. would call — Will backshifts to Would.
  3. was going — Question backshifts to statement word order with Past Continuous.
  4. if / whether — Yes/No question connector in reported speech.
  5. not to — Imperative command converted to negative infinitive (`not to + V1`).
