DAY 5 / 14 · GRAMMAR INTENSIVE

Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Master background actions in progress vs. interrupting completed events.

Stage 1 · Elicitation

Visualize this scene in a film:

"I was cooking dinner when the phone suddenly rang."

Identify the roles: Which action was the long, ongoing background scene? Which action was the sudden single moment that interrupted it?

Stage 2 · The Rule

Core Rule: Movie Scene Background vs. Sudden Action

Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing)

Background action that was actively in progress at a specific moment in the past.

  • Often introduced by WHILE: While I was walking in the park...
  • Example: I was cooking dinner at 7 PM yesterday.

Past Simple (V2)

Short completed action that interrupts an ongoing action, or sequential past actions.

  • Often introduced by WHEN: ...when the lights went out.
  • Example: He woke up, brushed his teeth, and left. (Sequence)
💡 Memory Trick: Past Continuous = Movie Background Music (long, playing in progress). Past Simple = Sudden Lightning Flash (short, completed event)!
Stage 3 · Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: In "While I was studying, my brother entered," which action started first?
    Answer: "Studying" started first and was in progress.
  2. Question: Did "studying" finish before my brother entered, or was it interrupted?
    Answer: It was ongoing in the background when the brother entered.
  3. Question: In "She dropped her purse and broke her glass," are these overlapping actions or a sequence of finished events?
    Answer: A sequence of finished events (one after another in Past Simple).
  4. Question: Which word usually connects Past Continuous (while / when)?
    Answer: While usually introduces Past Continuous; when usually precedes the interrupting Past Simple event.
Stage 4 · Guided & Free Practice

Complete the Sentences (Past Simple vs Past Continuous)

1. They _____ (play) tennis when it suddenly started to rain.
2. While I _____ (walk) home, I found a lost wallet.
3. At 8 o'clock yesterday evening, she _____ (read) a book in her room.
4. He _____ (hear) a loud crash while he was preparing breakfast.
5. The driver _____ (not / notice) the red light because he was talking on his phone.
🔍 Click to Reveal Answer Key & Explanations
  1. were playing — Ongoing background activity interrupted by rain.
  2. was walking — Continuous background action preceded by "While".
  3. was reading — Action in progress at a specific past moment (8 o'clock).
  4. heard — Sudden interrupting perception verb in Past Simple.
  5. didn't notice — Short completed state in Past Simple alongside continuous phone call.
