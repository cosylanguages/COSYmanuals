DAY 2 / 14 · GRAMMAR INTENSIVE

Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Master permanent routines, facts, and habits vs. actions happening right now or temporary situations.

Stage 1 · Elicitation

Compare these two statements from Sarah:

A: "I live in Madrid and I work as a teacher."
B: "I am living in London this month and working at a summer camp."

Think about it: Which sentence describes Sarah's permanent life baseline? Which sentence describes a temporary project happening around now?

Stage 2 · The Rule

Core Rule: Permanent Habit vs. Temporary / Right Now

Present Simple (V1 / -s)

Use for permanent facts, general truths, and regular habits/routines.

  • Key Keywords: always, usually, often, every day, on Mondays.
  • Example: She works in London. (Permanent job)
  • Example: The sun rises in the east. (Fact)

Present Continuous (am/is/are + V-ing)

Use for actions happening right now at the moment of speech or temporary situations around now.

  • Key Keywords: now, right now, at the moment, this week, currently.
  • Example: She is working from home today. (Temporary)
  • Example: Look! It is raining outside. (Right now)
💡 Quick Logic: Always/Usually/Permanent baseline = Simple. Right now/This week/Temporary change = Continuous! Stative verbs (know, want, like, believe) stay in Simple!
Stage 3 · Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: If I say "I drink coffee," am I holding a cup of coffee right now?
    Answer: Not necessarily! It means drinking coffee is a general habit or preference in your life.
  2. Question: If I say "I am drinking coffee," when is this action happening?
    Answer: Right now, at this exact moment of speaking.
  3. Question: Can we say "I am knowing the answer right now"?
    Answer: No! "Know" is a stative verb (mental state), so we must say "I know the answer."
  4. Question: "He usually drives to work, but this week he is taking the bus." Why is "taking" continuous?
    Answer: Because taking the bus is a temporary change for "this week", not his permanent routine.
Stage 4 · Guided & Free Practice

Complete the Sentences (Present Simple vs Present Continuous)

1. Keep quiet! The baby _____ (sleep) in the bedroom right now.
2. Mark usually _____ (go) to the gym on Tuesdays, but today he is sick.
3. Water _____ (boil) at 100 degrees Celsius.
4. I _____ (not / understand) this grammar rule. Can you explain it again?
5. Why _____ (you / wear) a winter coat indoors today?
🔍 Click to Reveal Answer Key & Explanations
  1. is sleeping — Action happening right now ("right now").
  2. goes — Habitual routine signaled by "usually".
  3. boils — Scientific fact / general truth.
  4. don't understand — "Understand" is a stative verb; use Present Simple.
  5. are you wearing — Temporary action occurring right now.
