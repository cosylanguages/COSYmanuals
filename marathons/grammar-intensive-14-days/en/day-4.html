DAY 4 / 14 · GRAMMAR INTENSIVE

Will vs Be Going To vs Present Continuous for Future

Master instant decisions and promises vs. pre-planned intentions vs. fixed calendar arrangements.

Stage 1 · Elicitation

Analyze these three future forms:

1. Phone rings: "I'll answer it!"
2. "I'm going to study computer science next year."
3. "I'm flying to Paris tomorrow at 9 AM (I have my ticket)."

Notice the timeline of decision: Sentence 1 is decided on the spot. Sentence 2 is an intention made before now. Sentence 3 is a fixed arrangement with external details (time/ticket)!

Stage 2 · The Rule

Core Rule: When Was the Decision Made?

WILL (Modal Verb)

Instant decisions (spontaneous), offers, promises, and predictions based on opinion/belief.

  • Instant: "I'll have the steak, please."
  • Promise: "I'll call you as soon as I arrive."

BE GOING TO vs PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Be going to: Intentions/plans made prior to speaking OR predictions based on clear visual evidence.

Present Continuous: Fixed future arrangements with a specific time/place/person already booked.

  • Evidence: Look at those dark clouds! It's going to rain.
  • Fixed: I am meeting the doctor at 4 PM on Tuesday.
💡 Memory Trick: Decided NOW? → WILL. Decided BEFORE / Visual evidence? → GOING TO. Booked in your calendar with another person? → PRESENT CONTINUOUS!
Stage 3 · Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: "I will help you with your bags." Did I plan this 10 minutes ago, or am I deciding right now?
    Answer: Deciding right now on the spot (spontaneous offer).
  2. Question: "Look at that boy on the edge of the wall! He's going to fall!" Why do we use "going to"?
    Answer: Because there is present visual evidence right now showing the future result.
  3. Question: What is the difference between "I'm going to visit my dentist" and "I'm seeing my dentist tomorrow at 10 AM"?
    Answer: "Going to" expresses intention; "seeing... at 10 AM" is a confirmed appointment/arrangement.
  4. Question: Can we use "will" for a pre-booked flight ticket?
    Answer: No, fixed arrangements with tickets/schedules use Present Continuous ("I am flying tomorrow").
Stage 4 · Guided & Free Practice

Complete the Sentences (Will vs Be Going To vs Present Continuous)

1. A: "We don't have any milk." B: "Oh, really? I _____ (go) and buy some right now."
2. A: "We don't have any milk." B: "I know. I _____ (buy) some later. It's on my shopping list."
3. Be careful with that heavy box! You _____ (drop) it!
4. She _____ (meet) her manager at 3 PM tomorrow. It's on her official calendar.
5. Don't worry, I promise I _____ (not / tell) anyone your secret.
🔍 Click to Reveal Answer Key & Explanations
  1. will go ('ll go) — Spontaneous decision made at the moment of speaking.
  2. am going to buy — Pre-planned intention ("It's on my shopping list").
  3. are going to drop — Prediction based on immediate physical evidence.
  4. is meeting — Fixed calendar arrangement with a specified time/place.
  5. won't tell (will not tell) — Promise.
