DAY 7 / 14 · GRAMMAR INTENSIVE

Used To vs Would & Be Used To vs Get Used To

Master past habits, past states, familiarity, and the process of becoming accustomed.

Stage 1 · Elicitation

Examine these past memory statements:

1. "I used to live in Paris." (Correct)
2. "I would live in Paris." (Incorrect ❌)
3. "Every summer, we would visit my grandmother." (Correct)

Spot the difference: Why is sentence 2 ungrammatical, while sentence 1 and sentence 3 are both perfectly correct?

Stage 2 · The Rule

Core Rule: Action vs. State & Familiarity Structures

Past Habits & States (Used to vs Would)

Used to + V1: Discontinued past habits AND past states (live, be, have, know, love).

Would + V1: Repeated past actions ONLY (never for past states!).

  • State (Used to only): I used to be shy.
  • Action (Both OK): We used to go camping / We would go camping.

Familiarity (Be/Get used to + -ing / noun)

Be used to + V-ing/noun: To be comfortable/accustomed to something.

Get used to + V-ing/noun: The transition process of becoming accustomed over time.

  • Familiar state: I am used to waking up early.
  • Transition process: I am slowly getting used to the cold weather.
💡 Quick Logic: State verb (live, be, have)? → MUST use "Used to". Repeated nostalgia action? → "Would" or "Used to". Already accustomed? → "Be used to + -ING". Process of learning? → "Get used to + -ING"!
Stage 3 · Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: Can I say "When I was a child, I would have a dog"?
    Answer: No! "Have" is a state verb. You must say "I used to have a dog."
  2. Question: "She is used to driving on the left." Does she find driving on the left difficult or strange now?
    Answer: No, it is familiar and normal for her now.
  3. Question: What form of the verb follows "be used to" (infinitive or gerund)?
    Answer: Gerund (V-ing) or noun! (e.g., "be used to working", NOT "be used to work").
  4. Question: What is the difference between "I get used to noise" and "I am used to noise"?
    Answer: "Get used to" is the active process/adaptation; "Am used to" is the finished state of familiarity.
Stage 4 · Guided & Free Practice

Complete the Sentences (Used to / Would / Be used to / Get used to)

1. When I was young, my father _____ (read) me bedtime stories every night.
2. My brother _____ (live) in Tokyo, but now he lives in Sydney.
3. It was hard at first, but now I _____ (wake) up at 6 AM every morning.
4. Don't worry, you will soon _____ (get / speak) English all day.
5. She _____ (not / like) spinach when she was a kid.
🔍 Click to Reveal Answer Key & Explanations
  1. would read / used to read — Repeated past action; both are correct.
  2. used to live — Past state verb (live), so "would" cannot be used.
  3. am used to waking — Current state of familiarity; requires `am used to + V-ing`.
  4. get used to speaking — Transition process over time; requires `get used to + V-ing`.
  5. didn't use to like — Past state verb negative (`didn't use to like`).
