DAY 9 / 14 · GRAMMAR INTENSIVE

Conditionals (Zero, 1st, 2nd, 3rd & Mixed)

Master real future possibilities vs. unreal present hypotheticals vs. past regrets.

Stage 1 · Elicitation

Compare these conditional situations:

1. "If it rains tomorrow, we will stay home."
2. "If I won a million dollars today, I would buy a yacht."
3. "If I had studied harder back in school, I would have passed."

Notice the reality level: Sentence 1 is a realistic future possibility. Sentence 2 is an imaginary present dream. Sentence 3 is an unchangeable past regret!

Stage 2 · The Rule

Core Rule: Step Back in Tense for Irreality

Zero & 1st Conditional (Real)

Zero (Scientific fact): If + Present Simple, Present Simple. (If you heat ice, it melts.)

1st (Real Future): If + Present Simple, WILL + V1. (If she calls, I will answer.)

2nd & 3rd Conditional (Unreal)

2nd (Unreal Present): If + Past Simple, WOULD + V1. (If I had time, I would travel.)

3rd (Unreal Past Regret): If + Past Perfect, WOULD HAVE + V3. (If I had known, I would have come.)

💡 Memory Trick: Real future = Present + Will. Imaginary present = Past + Would. Past regret = Past Perfect + Would Have!
Stage 3 · Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: In "If I were you, I would quit," am I actually you?
    Answer: No! It is an unreal hypothetical present situation (2nd conditional).
  2. Question: In "If we had left earlier, we wouldn't have missed the train," did we leave early? Did we miss the train?
    Answer: We did NOT leave early, and we DID miss the train (3rd conditional past regret).
  3. Question: Is "If it rains tomorrow, I will stay inside" certain or possible?
    Answer: It is a real future possibility dependent on the weather (1st conditional).
  4. Question: In the 'if' clause of a 1st conditional, can we put 'will'?
    Answer: No! The 'if' clause takes Present Simple ("If it rains...", NOT "If it will rain...").
Stage 4 · Guided & Free Practice

Complete the Sentences (Zero, 1st, 2nd, or 3rd Conditional)

1. If you _____ (mix) red and blue, you get purple.
2. If she works hard this week, she _____ (pass) her exam.
3. If I _____ (be) a bird, I would fly around the world.
4. If we _____ (set) our alarm, we wouldn't have overslept this morning.
5. What _____ (you / do) if you saw a bear in the woods?
🔍 Click to Reveal Answer Key & Explanations
  1. mix — Zero conditional (general scientific rule).
  2. will pass — 1st conditional (real future outcome).
  3. were / was — 2nd conditional (unreal present hypothetical; "were" preferred in formal contexts).
  4. had set — 3rd conditional (unreal past action).
  5. would you do — 2nd conditional question form for hypothetical situation.
