ENGLISH GRAMMAR CONFUSIONS (A0 → A2)

Master confusing grammar pairs (A vs B) with instant logic and visual memory tricks.

Level A0–A1

1. A vs AN (Articles Before Nouns)

Core Difference: Sound determines the choice—not the letter!

Use A when...

The next word begins with a consonant sound.

Examples: a car, a dog, a university (/j-sound/)

Use AN when...

The next word begins with a vowel sound.

Examples: an apple, an elephant, an hour (/silent h/)

💡 Memory Trick: Listen to the first sound! Vowel sound = AN, Consonant sound = A.
Level A0–A1

2. Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Core Difference: Permanent routine vs. Right now / Temporary action.

Present Simple

Use for habits, routines, and permanent facts (always, usually, every day).

Example: She works in London.

Present Continuous

Use for actions happening right now or temporary situations (this week, currently).

Example: She is working from home today.

💡 Quick Logic: Always/Usually → Simple. Right now / Temporary → Continuous.
Level A2

3. Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Core Difference: Finished time vs. Present connection/experience.

Past Simple

Finished action in a finished time (yesterday, last year, in 2020, ago).

Example: I visited Rome in 2019.

Present Perfect

Life experience, unfinished time (today, this week), or present result.

Example: I have visited Rome three times.

💡 Memory Trick: Yesterday closes the door (Past Simple). Present Perfect keeps the door open!
Level A2

4. Will vs Be Going To

Core Difference: Instant decision vs. Pre-planned intention.

Will

On-the-spot decisions, offers, promises, opinion predictions.

Example: The phone is ringing. I'll get it!

Be Going To

Prior plans made before speaking or predictions with present evidence.

Example: Look at those dark clouds! It's going to rain.

💡 Quick Logic: Decided now? → WILL. Decided before / Evidence now? → GOING TO.
