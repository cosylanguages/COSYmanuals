💡 Key English Spelling Logic & Mnemonics

💡 The 3 Vowel Length Strategies

English uses 3 distinct visual strategies to signal vowel length to readers:

  • 1. Silent E (Magic E): hop (short) → hope (long).
  • 2. Doubled Consonant: hoping (long o) vs. hopping (short o).
  • 3. Vowel Teams: rain, boat, see (long vowel team).

💡 Etymology Origin Markers

  • Greek Origin: CH = /k/ (school, chrome), PH = /f/ (phone), PS/PN silent initial P (psychology).
  • French Origin: CH = /ʃ/ (chef, machine), -QUE = /k/ (unique), -ETTE stressed.
  • Latin Origin: Soft C/G, -TION/-SION, TI/CI/SI = /ʃ, ʒ/.
  • Germanic Origin: Hard G (get, give, girl), silent GH (night, light), initial KN/WR silent (know, write).
