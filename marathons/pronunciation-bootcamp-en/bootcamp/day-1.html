Stage 2 🤔 Elicitation

Compare these pairs:

  • Group A (Closed Syllable / Short Vowel): cat, pen, sit, hot, cup

Notice the pattern: In Group A, the vowel is trapped by a consonant at the end (Closed Syllable). In Group B, the silent -e at the end pushes the middle vowel to say its name (Alphabet Sound)!

Stage 3 📖 Rule Explanation

1. The 5 English Vowels: Short vs. Long

Vowel Short Sound (Closed Syllable CVC) Mouth Position Long Sound (Magic E / Name) Mouth Position
A /æ/ (cat, map) Jaw dropped wide, flat tongue. /eɪ/ (cake, tape) Lips stretch wide into a smile.
E /ɛ/ (bed, pen) Mid-open jaw, relaxed lips. /iː/ (Pete, these) High tight smile, tongue close to roof.
I /ɪ/ (sit, big) Relaxed mouth, quick short burst. /aɪ/ (time, kite) Open wide then glide up into smile.
O /ɒ/ (hot, dog) Open rounded jaw. /oʊ/ (home, note) Round lips pulling into a small 'u'.
U /ʌ/ (cup, run) Relaxed throat, quick short sound. /juː/ or /uː/ (cute, tune) Push lips out into tight circle ('yoo' or 'oo').

2. Essential Vowel Teams (Two Vowels = One Sound)

When two vowels go walking, the first one does the talking (says its long name)!

  • EE / EA → /iː/ : see, tree, meat, eat (Exceptions: head, bread = /ɛ/).
  • AI / AY → /eɪ/ : rain, wait (mid-word) vs. day, play (end of word).
  • OA / OW → /oʊ/ : boat, coat (mid-word) vs. show, slow (end of word).
💡 Position Principle: Use AI and OA inside a word (rain, boat). Use AY and OW at the end of a word (day, show).
Stage 4 ❓ Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: Is the vowel in "mat" short or long?
    Answer: Short (/æ/), because it is a closed CVC syllable without silent -e.
  2. Question: What happens when we add silent -e to "mat" → "mate"?
    Answer: Vowel A becomes long (/eɪ/), pronouncing its alphabet name!
  3. Question: Where do we use AI vs AY for the /eɪ/ sound?
    Answer: AI in the middle of words (rain); AY at the end of words (day).

Stage 7 🔑 Day 1 Takeaway

Golden Rule of Day 1:

CVC = Short Vowel (cat, sit). CVC + silent E = Long Vowel (cake, time). Two vowels walking = First vowel talks (rain, boat)!
