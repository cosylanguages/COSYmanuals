Day 3 — Bossy R & Silent Letters

Master R-controlled vowels (ER, IR, UR cover 21.5% of English) and unlock silent consonant patterns.

Stage 2 🤔 Elicitation

Compare these silent letter pairs:

  • know, knee, knife → K is completely silent!

History Hint: In Old English and Germanic origins, these letters were once pronounced centuries ago. Today they remain in writing as historical markers!

Stage 3 📖 Rule Explanation

1. R-Controlled Vowels ("Bossy R")

Stage 4 ❓ Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: Do ER, IR, and UR sound different in "her", "bird", and "fur"?
    Answer: No! They all share the exact same R-controlled vowel sound /ɜːr/.
  2. Question: Is the 'b' pronounced in "climbing"?
    Answer: No, the root word "climb" ends in -mb, keeping 'b' silent even with suffixes.
  3. Question: Why is 'k' silent in "knife"?
    Answer: Initial KN- cluster always drops the K sound in Modern English.

Stage 7 🔑 Day 3 Takeaway

Golden Rule of Day 3:

ER, IR, and UR match in sound (/ɜːr/). Initial KN, WR, GN and final MB, MN silence their extra consonant!
