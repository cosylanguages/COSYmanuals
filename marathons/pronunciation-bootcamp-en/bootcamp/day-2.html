Day 2 — Consonants, Digraphs & Soft C / Soft G

Learn why C and G flip sounds before E, I, Y and master 9 core consonant digraphs.

Stage 1 🎯 Warmer

Read these words out loud:

« CAT » vs. « CITY »   |   « GO » vs. « GEM »

Why is C pronounced /k/ in cat but /s/ in city? Why is G pronounced /g/ in go but /dʒ/ in gem?

Stage 2 🤔 Elicitation

Observe what comes right after C and G:

  • Hard C / G (before A, O, U or Consonant): cat, cob, cup, go, game, gun
  • Soft C / G (before E, I, Y): cent, city, cycle, gem, giant, gym

Pattern: Whenever C or G is followed by E, I, or Y, it turns into its "soft" sound!

Stage 3 📖 Rule Explanation

1. Soft C and Soft G Rules

LetterHard Sound (Default)Soft Sound (Before E, I, Y)Exceptions & Notes
C /k/ (cat, cold) /s/ (cent, city, face) 100% reliable rule!
G /g/ (go, game) /dʒ/ (gem, giant, page) Germanic exceptions stay hard: get, give, girl. Latin/French = soft.

2. Core Consonant Digraphs (Two Letters = One Sound)

DigraphSoundExamplesPosition / Rule
SH/ʃ/ship, fish, push100% reliable sound.
CH/tʃ/child, much, churchDefault /tʃ/; Greek origin = /k/ (school); French = /ʃ/ (chef).
TH/θ/ (voiceless) or /ð/ (voiced)think, thick / the, thisContent words = voiceless /θ/; function words = voiced /ð/.
PH/f/phone, photoGreek origin words. 100% reliable.
WH/w/what, where, whenPronounced /h/ before 'o' (who, whom).
NG/ŋ/sing, long, kingNasal sound in back of throat.
CK/k/back, kick, rockUsed after short vowels at word end.
TCH/tʃ/match, watch, catchUsed after short vowels (protects vowel).
DGE/dʒ/bridge, edge, judgeUsed after short vowels for soft G.
💡 Short Vowel Consonant Blockers: Short vowels use CK (back), TCH (match), and DGE (bridge) to lock in their short vowel sound!
Stage 4 ❓ Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: How is C pronounced in "pencil"?
    Answer: /s/ (Soft C), because it is directly followed by 'i'.
  2. Question: Why do we write "match" with TCH instead of CH?
    Answer: Because 'a' is a short vowel, and TCH follows short vowels.
  3. Question: Is TH in "think" voiced or voiceless?
    Answer: Voiceless /θ/ (air only, no vocal cord vibration).

Stage 7 🔑 Day 2 Takeaway

Golden Rule of Day 2:

C and G turn soft (/s/, /dʒ/) before E, I, Y. Short vowels call for CK, TCH, and DGE!
