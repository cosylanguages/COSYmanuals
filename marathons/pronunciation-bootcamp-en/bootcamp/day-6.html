Day 6 — Word Stress & Stress-Shifting Rules

Master 2-syllable noun vs. verb stress pairs and multi-syllable suffix stress position rules.

Stage 1 🎯 Warmer

Read these two identical words with different stress:

« RE-cord » (Noun)   vs.   « re-CORD » (Verb)

Changing the stressed syllable completely changes the grammatical role of over 27 common English words!

Stage 2 🤔 Elicitation

Observe 2-Syllable Stress Patterns:

  • 2-Syllable Nouns / Adjectives → Stress First Syllable: TAble, HAPpy, DOCtor, PREsent, OBject
  • 2-Syllable Verbs / Prepositions → Stress Second Syllable: reLAX, deCIDE, beTWEEN, preSENT, obJECT

Pattern: First syllable = Noun/Adjective; Second syllable = Verb!

Stage 3 📖 Rule Explanation

1. The Noun vs. Verb Stress Shift

2. Multi-Syllable Suffix Stress Position Rules

Suffix PatternStress RuleExamples
-tion, -sion, -ic, -ical Stress syllable right before suffix inforMAtion, fanTAStic, poLItical
-ty, -cy, -phy, -gy, -al Stress 3rd syllable from end (antepenultimate) uniVERsity, deMOCracy, phoTOGraphy
-ee, -eer, -ese, -ette, -ade Stress directly ON the suffix guaranTEE, volunTEER, JapanESE, cassETTE
💡 Compound Stress Rule: Compound Nouns stress the FIRST word (SEAfood, TOOTHpaste). Compound Adjectives stress the SECOND word (old-FASHioned, rock-SOLid).
Stage 4 ❓ Concept Check Questions (CCQs)
  1. Question: Where is stress placed in the verb "to export"?
    Answer: Second syllable (ex-PORT). The noun is EX-port.
  2. Question: Where does stress fall in "education"?
    Answer: Syllable right before -tion → ed-u-CA-tion.
  3. Question: Which word is stressed in compound noun "airport"?
    Answer: First word → AIR-port.

Stage 7 🔑 Day 6 Takeaway

Golden Rule of Day 6:

2-Syllables: Nouns stress 1st, Verbs stress 2nd. -TION/-IC stress syllable right before; -EE/-EER stress suffix itself!
