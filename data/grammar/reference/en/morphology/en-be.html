English — The Verb 'To Be'

We use 'To Be' to describe names, jobs, feelings, and nationalities.

Why is this important? This verb connects subjects to who or what they are. You will use it in almost every conversation to identify people and objects.
⚠ Why learners struggle: Many languages omit the verb "is" or "are" in the present tense (e.g., saying "I student" or "She happy"), but English always demands a clear verb!

👀 Notice

Which form of the verb 'To Be' do we use when talking about multiple people (We, They)?

🧠 Think like English

English likes every sentence to have a clear verb. While some languages can skip the verb to say "He doctor", English always demands a verb to hold the statement together!

⚠ Common Mistakes
✗ Incorrect Sentence ✓ Correct Standard Sentence
I is a student. I am a student.
She are happy. She is happy.

💡 Memory Trick

Think of 'To Be' as a bridge. A bridge links two pieces of land; 'To Be' links a person to their role or feelings. Never leave the bridge out!

🔗 This prepares you for the Present Continuous (BE + Verb-ing) and the Past Simple (was/were).
