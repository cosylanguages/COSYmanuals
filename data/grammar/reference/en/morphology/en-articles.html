English — Articles: A, AN, THE

We use articles before nouns to show whether an object is new or already known.

Why is this important? Articles are part of how English organizes information. They let the listener know whether you are introducing something new or referring to something specific.
⚠ Why learners struggle: Many languages don't use articles at all, making it difficult to remember when to choose 'a', 'an', or 'the'.

⭐ Fully Coloured Examples
Article usage (Between 2 and 5 examples maximum)
General / Consonant This is a car. Used before a consonant sound.
General / Vowel I have an apple. Used before a vowel sound.
Specific / Known Where is the book? The speaker and listener know which book.
General Plural I love books. No article is used for general plurals.

👀 Notice

Which article do we choose when the next word begins with a vowel sound (a, e, i, o, u)?

🧠 Think like English

English speakers like to tell listeners whether something is already known (the) or brand new (a/an). Articles are part of how English organizes information flow!

⚠ Common Mistakes
✗ Incorrect Sentence ✓ Correct Standard Sentence
I bought the car (first mention). I bought a car.
This is a apple. This is an apple.

💡 Memory Trick

"a" opens the story ➔ "the" continues the story!

🔗 This prepares you for Countable and Uncountable nouns and Plural nouns.
