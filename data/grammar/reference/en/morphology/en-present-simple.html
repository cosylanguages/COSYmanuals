Vocabulary theme for practice: Family
English — Present Simple

We use the Present Simple for routines, habits, and facts.

Why is this important? You'll hear this every day when talking about daily life. It forms the bedrock of basic conversations, allowing you to tell people about yourself and your family.
⚠ Why learners struggle: Many languages do not use helper verbs (like do/does) to ask questions or build negatives, which can feel repetitive at first.

⭐ Fully Coloured Examples
Standard sentence patterns (Between 2 and 5 examples maximum)
I / You / We / They I like pizza. Native speakers choose the plain form.
He / She / It She reads books. Add -s or -es to the action.
They They play football. This grammar appears without any extra ending.
He He drinks tea. Add -s when talking about single persons.

👀 Notice

Which letters are added to the verb when we change "I play" into "She plays"?

🧠 Think like English

English usually shows relationships between words through word order, not endings. That's why changing the order of words often changes the meaning completely. Keep the Subject-Verb-Object order firm!

⚠ Common Mistakes
✗ Incorrect Sentence ✓ Correct Standard Sentence
He work in London. He works in London.
She doesn't likes tea. She doesn't like tea. (no double -s!)

💡 Memory Trick

Think of -s as the "singular supervisor". He, She, and It are solo players, so they always need the singular supervisor (-s) to help the verb.
