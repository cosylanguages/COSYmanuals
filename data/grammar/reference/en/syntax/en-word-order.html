English — Word Order
A1 / Beginner

English relies on a strict word order to show who does what.

Why is this important? You will use this grammar almost every time you speak. English doesn't use endings on nouns to show who did what; the position of the words does all the heavy lifting.
⚠ Why learners struggle: If your native language has a flexible word order, it is easy to place words in the wrong spot, which can change the meaning completely.

👀 Notice

Which word position tells us who is performing the action?

🧠 Think like English

English cares about word order. English nouns don't have endings to show if they are doing the action or receiving it. The only way to know is by their position in the sentence.

⚠ Common Mistakes
✗ Incorrect Sentence ✓ Correct Standard Sentence
Pizza likes my brother. My brother likes pizza.
They yesterday drank water. They drank water yesterday.

💡 Memory Trick

Think of a train: Subject is the Engine, Verb is the Coal Car, Object is the Passenger Car. They must be connected in this exact order to move forward!

🔗 This prepares you for the Present Simple and asking questions (ASI/QUASI).
