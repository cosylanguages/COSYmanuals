Master Founder Portal. Click on any language below to open the dedicated Teacher Access Portal for that language.

🔐 Workflow Architecture Summary:

  1. Founder Level (This Page): Contains direct links to each language's Teacher Portal.
  2. Teacher Level: Send the language teacher link (e.g. manuals/en/teacher-access.html) to your contracted instructor. Teachers see all course tracks and levels for their language.
  3. Student Level: When a teacher clicks a specific level (e.g. general/a1), it opens the student manual landing page (e.g. manuals/en/general/a1/index.html) containing grammar.md, vocabulary.md, and communication.md.
