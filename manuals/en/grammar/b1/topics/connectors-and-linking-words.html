Level B2 Grammar

Connectors and Linking Words

Link sentences and paragraphs, not only clauses.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use connectors that open sentences and hold a longer text together.
Examples in context
  • The bid was late. Moreover, it was over budget. Adds a second point. — Moreover opens a sentence.
  • The delay was avoidable. Therefore, we are claiming costs. Draws a conclusion. — Therefore marks result.
  • Three risks stand out. Firstly, the supplier. Orders the points. — Firstly marks sequence.

👀 What do you observe?

Sentence connectors hold a text together across sentence boundaries: addition (moreover, furthermore, in addition, similarly), contrast (however, nevertheless, on the other hand, by contrast), result (therefore, consequently, as a result), sequence (firstly, secondly, finally), and summary (in conclusion, overall, to sum up). They are adverbs, so they take a comma after them and a full stop or semicolon before them.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
addition moreover, furthermore, in addition moreover, it was over budget
contrast however, nevertheless, on the other hand however, the timetable is not
result therefore, consequently, as a result therefore, we are claiming costs
sequence firstly, secondly, finally firstly, the supplier
summary in conclusion, overall overall, the plan is sound
punctuation full stop or semicolon before, comma after late. Moreover, it was over budget.

Formula / rule: Decide the relation, then place the connector at the start of the sentence with a comma after it. Use a full stop or a semicolon before it, never a comma alone. Keep one connector per relation per paragraph, since two addition connectors in a row read as padding.

★ Why it matters

At B1 the clause-level links are already known, and the step up is managing a paragraph: a reader follows an argument because however signals a reversal and therefore signals a conclusion. Without them a report is a list of true statements with no claim about how they relate.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use these connectors far more in writing than in speech, where and, but and so do the work. Overusing moreover and furthermore in speech sounds like a memo being read aloud.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The connector is stressed and followed by a pause: the bid was late. moreOVER, it was over budget.

Additional example bank
  • Contrast: The trial succeeded. Nevertheless, the cost is prohibitive.
  • Result: Two suppliers withdrew. As a result, the tender reopened.
  • Summary: Overall, the audit found no material error.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: The bid was late, moreover it was over budget (comma splice before a sentence connector)

✅ Right: The bid was late. Moreover, it was over budget

❌ Wrong: However the timetable is not (missing comma after the connector)

✅ Right: However, the timetable is not
