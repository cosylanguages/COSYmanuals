Level B2 Grammar

Articles: Deeper Rules

Choose a, the or nothing in the harder cases.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can handle generic reference, unique things and the zero article.
Examples in context
  • I need a chair. Any one chair. — A for first mention.
  • The chair is broken. The one we both mean. — The for shared knowledge.
  • Water boils at 100. General, uncountable. — No article for general reference.

👀 What do you observe?

A and an mark one of many and first mention. The marks shared knowledge: something already mentioned, unique (the sun), or identified by context (the desk in this room). No article is used for general plurals and uncountables (water boils, dogs bark), for most proper nouns, and in fixed phrases (at home, by train, in bed). Generic reference also takes the with singular countables and musical instruments: the piano, the whale.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
a / an first mention, one of many I need a chair
the known, unique or identified the sun, the desk, the report
no article, plural general reference dogs bark
no article, uncountable general reference water boils
the + singular countable generic reference to a species or class the whale, the piano
no article in fixed phrases set expressions at home, by train, in bed

Formula / rule: Ask whether the reader can identify the thing. If not and it is countable singular, use a or an. If yes, use the. If the reference is general, use no article with plurals and uncountables, and the with a singular that stands for the class.

★ Why it matters

The article is where a language without articles shows most clearly: a text that says I need chair or the water boils at 100 reads as translated. The generic the (the piano, the whale) is also a marker of educated English that learners rarely produce.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use the generic the with instruments, inventions and species, and no article with abstract nouns and institutions in their general sense (go to school, in prison). The difference between go to school and go to the school is a real one.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Articles are unstressed, and the is /ðə/ before a consonant and /ði/ before a vowel: the office, the audit.

Additional example bank
  • Generic: The bicycle remains the most efficient city vehicle.
  • Identified: The figures in the annex are provisional.
  • Institutional: She is still in hospital.
