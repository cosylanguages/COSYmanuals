Wh-Questions in the Past

Ask about the past with the right auxiliary.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can form past wh-questions with did and with be.
Examples in context
  • Where did you put the file? Wh-word, did, subject, base verb. — Did carries the past.
  • Why was the meeting cancelled? Be takes no did. — Was plus subject.
  • Who told the client? Who as subject takes no auxiliary. — Who + past verb.

👀 What do you observe?

Past wh-questions use did plus the base verb, and the past is carried by did alone: where did you put it, not where did you putted it. With be, the past form moves to the front and no did is used: why was the meeting cancelled. When who or what is the subject of the question, there is no auxiliary and the verb stays in the past: who told the client.

wh + did + subject + base most past questions where did you put the file
be: wh + was / were + subject no did with be why was the meeting cancelled
who as subject no auxiliary, past verb who told the client
who as object did plus base who did you tell
negative didn't plus base why didn't you call?
short answer did or didn't alone yes, I did

Formula / rule: Decide whether the question is about an action or a state. An action takes did plus the base verb. A state with be takes was or were plus the subject. If who or what is the subject, drop the auxiliary and keep the past verb.

★ Why it matters

Past question forms are where learners leave the tense twice: what did you did is the classic error, because the past is already carried by did. Getting it right also matters for reported questions, where the auxiliary disappears entirely.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use who did you tell and who told you side by side without confusion, because the subject role decides. In informal speech, did reduces to /dɪd/ or nearly vanishes.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Did carries the stress in a real question: WHERE did you PUT it? In a follow-up, the wh-word is stressed instead.

Additional example bank
  • Action: When did the panel publish the minutes?
  • State: Where were the files before the audit?
  • Subject: Who signed the contract?
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: Where did you putted the file (past form after did)

✅ Right: Where did you put the file

❌ Wrong: Why the meeting was cancelled? (missing auxiliary and inversion)

✅ Right: Why was the meeting cancelled?
