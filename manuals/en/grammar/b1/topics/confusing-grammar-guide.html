B1 Intermediate Confusing Grammar Master Guide

A vs B (vs C) structural contrasts, logic maps, and usage guides for intermediate English learners

🎯 Master Goal: Express subtle differences in past experience, continuous duration, necessity, preference, and degree with intermediate fluency.

👀 What do you notice?

Compare these two intermediate sentences:

  • "I have painted the kitchen." (Result / finished action → kitchen is ready now)
  • "I have been painting the kitchen." (Duration / activity focus → my hands are covered in paint!)

💡 Check your understanding (CCQs)

Score: 0 / 2
1. Function check: If someone says "I've been waiting for two hours", are they emphasizing the duration of the activity or the completed result?
Correct · Present Perfect Continuous emphasizes the duration and continuous activity up to now.
2. Form check: What is the difference between 'so beautiful' and 'such a beautiful day'?
Correct · 'So' modifies adjectives alone (so warm); 'Such' modifies noun phrases (such a warm day).

🧠 Logic Map: Core B1 Grammar Contrasts

Structure AStructure B (vs C)Core Logic DifferenceKey Examples
Past Simple Present Perfect Simple Finished specific past time vs. Unspecified past time connected to now I lived in Rome in 2018. vs. I have lived in Rome for 3 years.
Present Perfect Simple Present Perfect Continuous Completed result / quantity vs. Duration of activity / ongoing action I have written 5 emails. vs. I have been writing emails all morning.
For Since / From Duration length vs. Specific starting point in time for 3 months | since March | from 9 AM to 5 PM
Didn't need to Needn't have Action was not necessary, so maybe not done vs. Action was done, but was unnecessary I didn't need to buy milk (so I didn't). vs. I needn't have bought milk (I bought it, but we had plenty).
So Such (a/an) So + adjective/adverb vs. Such + (a/an) + adjective + noun She is so smart. vs. She is such a smart person.
Another Other / The other / Others Another + singular countable vs. Other + plural vs. The other = remaining one another cup | other places | the other shoe | others agree
Wish + Past Simple Wish + Would Desire to change a present state vs. Desire for someone else to change annoying behavior I wish I was taller. vs. I wish you would stop tapping your pen!
