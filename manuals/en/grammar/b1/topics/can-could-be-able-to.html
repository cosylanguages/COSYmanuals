Level B2 Grammar

Can, Could, Be Able To

Express ability across tenses and situations.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use can, could and be able to for present, past and future ability.
Examples in context
  • She can run the audit alone. Present ability. — Can has no other forms.
  • He could read at four. General past ability. — Could for past skill.
  • We will be able to start on Monday. Future ability. — Be able to carries the tense.

👀 What do you observe?

Can covers present ability and possibility but has no infinitive, perfect or future forms. Could covers general past ability; for a single achievement in the past, use was able to or managed to. Be able to supplies every tense can lacks: will be able to, have been able to, would like to be able to.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
can present ability and possibility she can run the audit
could general past ability he could read at four
was able to / managed to single past achievement we were able to finish
will be able to future ability we will be able to start on Monday
have been able to ability up to now they have been able to cope
couldn't / wasn't able to past inability they weren't able to finish

Formula / rule: Decide the time first. Present: can. General past skill: could. One specific past success: was able to. Future or perfect: be able to with the tense. Negative forms follow the same logic.

★ Why it matters

The distinction between could and was able to is the difference between a capability and an achievement: he could swim as a child, but he was able to swim across the lake last summer. In performance reports, that is the difference between potential and delivery.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use can and could for permission and requests far more than for ability (can I borrow this?), and use be able to when the tense demands it without thinking.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Can is unstressed and reduced to /kən/; the negative can't is stressed and distinguishes British /kɑːnt/ from American /kænt/.

Additional example bank
  • Future: The team will be able to report by June.
  • Achievement: They managed to close the gap in one quarter.
  • Perfect: We have been able to hold the price.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: We will can start on Monday (can has no future form)

✅ Right: We will be able to start on Monday

❌ Wrong: We could finish the audit yesterday (could for a single achievement)

✅ Right: We were able to finish the audit yesterday
