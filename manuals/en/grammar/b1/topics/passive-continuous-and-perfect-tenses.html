Level B2 Grammar

Passive: Continuous and Perfect

Put an ongoing or completed action in the passive.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use is being, was being, has been and had been in the passive.
Examples in context
  • The office is being repainted. Present continuous passive. — Is being + participle.
  • The report has been published. Present perfect passive. — Has been + participle.
  • The room had been cleaned. Past perfect passive. — Had been + participle.

👀 What do you observe?

The continuous passive uses being plus the participle: is being repainted, was being checked. The perfect passive uses been plus the participle: has been published, had been cleaned, will have been finished. Both keep the tense on the auxiliary, and both keep the participle unchanged.

is / are being + participle present continuous passive the office is being repainted
was / were being + participle past continuous passive the file was being checked
has / have been + participle present perfect passive the report has been published
had been + participle past perfect passive the room had been cleaned
will have been + participle future perfect passive it will have been finished by June
being versus been in progress versus completed is being checked / has been checked

Formula / rule: Choose the time and the aspect first, then build: progressive takes being, perfect takes been, and the participle never changes. The single most common error is writing been where being belongs.

★ Why it matters

The continuous passive is the tense of work in progress (the system is being migrated), and the perfect passive is the tense of results (the system has been migrated). In status reporting those two sentences describe completely different states.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use the continuous passive mainly in writing and formal speech; in conversation they prefer the active (they're repainting the office). The perfect passive is common everywhere.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Being is stressed when the progress is the point: the office is BEING repainted; been is weak and the participle carries the stress.

Additional example bank
  • In progress: The accounts are being audited this week.
  • Completed: The accounts have been audited.
  • Earlier: The site had been cleared before the survey.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: The office is been repainted (been where being belongs)

✅ Right: The office is being repainted

❌ Wrong: The report has being published (being where been belongs)

✅ Right: The report has been published
