Reported Speech: Statements

Report what someone said and shift the tense back.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can report a statement with the tense, pronouns and time words adjusted.
Examples in context
  • I am busy → He said he was busy. Present to past. — Am becomes was.
  • I have finished → She said she had finished. Perfect to past perfect. — Have becomes had.
  • I will call → He said he would call. Will becomes would. — Future to conditional.

👀 What do you observe?

Reported statements shift the tense one step back: present simple to past simple, present continuous to past continuous, present perfect to past perfect, will to would, can to could, must to had to. Pronouns and time words shift too: now becomes then, today becomes that day, tomorrow becomes the next day. Say takes no object, tell takes a person: he said that, he told me that.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
present → past tense shift I am busy → he said he was busy
present perfect → past perfect earlier action I have finished → she had finished
will → would future to conditional I will call → he would call
can → could, must → had to modal shift I can't come → he couldn't come
say vs tell tell takes a person he said that / he told me that
time and place words shift too now → then, here → there, tomorrow → the next day

Formula / rule: Move every tense back one step, move the pronouns to the reporter's point of view, and move the time words with them. Keep the tense if the statement is still true and the reporting verb is in the present: she says she is busy.

★ Why it matters

Reported speech is the grammar of minutes, journalism and summaries, and the tense shift is what keeps the timeline honest: a report that says he is busy has not reported anything, it has repeated it. The shift is also where pronouns go wrong, which is why a projected report must be checked for who said what.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers often skip the backshift in conversation when the fact is still true (she said she is coming). In writing, the shift is expected, and the time words are changed consistently or not at all.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The reporting verb is unstressed (he said that /hi ˈsed ðət/) and the reported clause carries the information stress.

Additional example bank
  • Simple: I am reviewing the file → She said she was reviewing the file.
  • Perfect: We have signed → They said they had signed.
  • Modal: I must leave → He said he had to leave.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: He said me that he was busy (say with a person object)

✅ Right: He told me that he was busy

❌ Wrong: She said she will send it (future not shifted)

✅ Right: She said she would send it
