For, Since, From

Mark a length, a starting point and a range.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use for with a length, since with a starting point and from with a range.
Examples in context
  • for three weeks a length of time — we have waited for three weeks
  • since 2019 a starting point, up to now — she has worked here since 2019
  • from nine to five a range with a start and end — open from nine to five

👀 What do you observe?

For marks a length of time and works with any tense: for three weeks, for two hours. Since marks a starting point and requires a perfect tense when the period runs to now: since 2019, since Monday. From marks the start of a range and looks forward: from nine to five, from June onwards. From does not run to the present on its own.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
for + length any tense we waited for two hours
since + point perfect tense to now she has worked here since 2019
from + point range, forwards the office is open from nine
from ... to start and end from nine to five
since + clause point in time as a clause since the merger closed
for + period noun also used with perfects for three years

Formula / rule: Ask whether you are measuring a length, naming a starting point, or marking a range. Length takes for with any tense. A start that runs to now takes since with a perfect. A range takes from, usually paired with to or until.

★ Why it matters

For and since track the two things a schedule needs: how long something lasts and when it began. From is the only one that describes a future range, which is why it appears on every notice and timetable.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use since with a clause freely (since we moved) and use from in speech mainly for ranges. Since with a past simple is a common error that natives never make.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

For is weak /fə/ before a length, since is unstressed /sɪns/, and from reduces to /frəm/ in the range pattern.

Additional example bank
  • Length: The panel has been sitting for six hours.
  • Start: Nothing has changed since the audit.
  • Range: The window is open from March to June.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: She has worked here for 2019 (for with a starting point)

✅ Right: She has worked here since 2019

❌ Wrong: I have known him since three years (since with a length)

✅ Right: I have known him for three years
