Level B1 Grammar

During, For, While

Separate a period, a length and a background action.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use during with a noun, for with a length and while with a clause.
Examples in context
  • during the meeting a period, with a noun — the alarm rang during the meeting
  • for two hours a length of time — we waited for two hours
  • while we were talking two actions at once, with a clause — the phone rang while we were talking

👀 What do you observe?

During takes a noun and means inside a period: during the meeting, during the audit. For takes a length of time: for two hours, for three weeks. While takes a clause and marks two actions happening at the same time: while we were talking. During and for answer different questions, and while cannot take a noun.

Formula / rule: Look at what follows the word. A noun means during or for, and the choice is period versus length. A subject and verb means while. Then check the verb in the while-clause: it is usually continuous when the action is background.

★ Why it matters

These three are the most frequent time-word confusion at B1 because many languages use one word for all of them. In writing, the difference between during the audit and for the audit is the difference between an event inside a period and a period itself.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use while and during freely, and they often drop the subject and verb after while in informal speech (while eating, while on the phone), which is standard in reduced clauses.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

During is unstressed (/ˈdjʊərɪŋ/), for reduces to /fə/ before a length, and while is /waɪl/ with a full vowel.

Additional example bank
  • Period: Nothing was recorded during the hearing.
  • Length: The panel deliberated for three hours.
  • Simultaneous: The line dropped while I was presenting.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: during we talked (clause after during)

✅ Right: while we talked

❌ Wrong: we waited during two hours (during with a length)

✅ Right: we waited for two hours
