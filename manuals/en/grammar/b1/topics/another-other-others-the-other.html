Level B2 Grammar

Another, Other, Others, The Other

Choose the form that fits the noun that follows.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use another, other, others and the other without confusing countable and uncountable nouns.
Examples in context
  • another week one more, singular countable — Another before a singular noun.
  • other bidders different ones, plural — Other before a plural noun.
  • others a pronoun, no noun after it — Some accepted; others did not.

👀 What do you observe?

Another means one more or a different one and takes a singular countable noun. Other is an adjective and needs a noun after it: other options, other people. Others is a pronoun and stands alone. The other refers to the second of two or the remaining one, and the others refers to the rest of a known group.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
another + singular one more or different another week
other + plural different ones, adjective other bidders
others (pronoun) no noun after it some accepted; others did not
the other the second of two, or the remaining we chose the other option
the others (pronoun) the rest of a known group two passed; the others failed
uncountable other + uncountable, no another other information

Formula / rule: Look at what follows. A singular countable noun takes another. A plural takes other. Nothing after it means the pronoun others, or the others for a known group. Uncountable nouns take other, never another.

★ Why it matters

These forms are the most frequent determiner error at B1 because all four are translatable by one word in many languages. They also drive the difference between an additional item and a different one, which is exactly what a schedule or a shortlist needs.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use another one in speech where writing has another, and they use others loosely for the rest. The distinction between each other's and one another is separate and softer.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Another is stressed on the second syllable (/əˈnʌðə/), and the other on the first (/ˈʌðə/), which is the audible difference between the two patterns.

Additional example bank
  • Singular: We need another meeting before signing.
  • Plural: Other suppliers quoted less.
  • Pronoun: Two bids were compliant; the others were not.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: another bidders (another with a plural)

✅ Right: other bidders

❌ Wrong: other week (other where another is needed with a singular)

✅ Right: another week
