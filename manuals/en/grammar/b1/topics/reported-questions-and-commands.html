Level B2 Grammar

Reported Questions and Commands

Report what was asked or ordered, without the question form.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can report questions with statement order and commands with the infinitive.
Examples in context
  • She asked where the file was. Reported question, statement order. — No inversion, no question mark.
  • He asked if we had signed. Yes/no question with if. — If introduces the clause.
  • They told us to wait. Reported command. — Tell + object + infinitive.

👀 What do you observe?

Reported questions keep the question word but lose the inversion and the question mark: where is the file becomes she asked where the file was. Yes/no questions take if or whether. Reported commands use tell or ask plus an object plus the infinitive, and negatives put not before the infinitive. Say cannot take an object, so commands need tell, ask, order or advise.

ask versus tell tell needs an object she asked me / she told me
tense backshift one step back after a past verb asked where the file was

Formula / rule: Decide whether it was a question or an order. A question keeps its question word, takes statement order and gets if when there is no question word. An order takes tell or ask, an object, and the infinitive. Then backshift the tense if the reporting verb is past.

★ Why it matters

These two patterns are the grammar of minutes and instructions, and the errors are the ones that make a report ambiguous: asked us to wait and asked if we would wait describe different events, and only the second leaves the decision open.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers keep the inversion in very informal reported questions (she asked where's the file), and they use ask for both questions and requests, relying on the pattern to disambiguate.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The reporting verb is unstressed and the reported clause carries the information stress: she asked where the FILE was.

Additional example bank
  • Wh-question: He asked when the panel would report.
  • Yes/no: They asked whether the deadline had moved.
  • Command: The auditor told the team to keep the drafts.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: She asked where was the file (inversion kept)

✅ Right: She asked where the file was

❌ Wrong: They said us to wait (say with an object)

✅ Right: They told us to wait
