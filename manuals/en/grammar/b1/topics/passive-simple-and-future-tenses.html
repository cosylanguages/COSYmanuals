Passive: Simple and Future Tenses

Put the object first in the present, past and future.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can form the passive in present simple, past simple and future.
Examples in context
  • The file is checked daily. Present simple passive. — Is + participle.
  • The contract was signed in June. Past simple passive. — Was + participle.
  • The results will be published. Future passive. — Will be + participle.

👀 What do you observe?

The passive takes the tense on be and leaves the main verb as a past participle. Present simple: is checked. Past simple: was signed. Future: will be published. Present continuous: is being repainted. Past continuous: was being checked. The agent appears in a by-phrase only when it matters: the contract was signed by both directors.

Formula / rule: Find the tense the sentence needs, put it on be, and leave the participle alone. Then decide whether the agent earns a by-phrase: it does when the reader needs to know who acted, and it does not when the actor is obvious, unknown or irrelevant.

★ Why it matters

The passive lets a writer keep the topic in front: the file is checked daily puts the file first because the file is what the paragraph is about. It also hides the agent, which is a service in a process description and a problem in an accountability report.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use the passive freely in process talk (the forms are sent out automatically) and avoid it in personal statements. In speech, the agent is usually dropped: it was cancelled, nobody knows by whom.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The auxiliary is unstressed and the participle carries the information stress: the file is CHECKED every morning; the results will be PUBlished.

Additional example bank
  • Present: Each invoice is verified against the order.
  • Past: The minutes were circulated after the meeting.
  • Future: The panels will be replaced in April.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: The contract was sign in June (base verb instead of a participle)

✅ Right: The contract was signed in June

❌ Wrong: The results will published on Friday (missing be)

✅ Right: The results will be published on Friday
