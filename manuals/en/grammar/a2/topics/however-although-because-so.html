However, Although, Because and So

Link a reason, a contrast and a result.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can join two ideas with because for reason, so for result, although for contrast and however for a separate sentence.
Examples in context
  • I stayed in because it was raining. The reason follows. — Because introduces the cause.
  • It was raining, so I stayed in. The result follows. — So introduces the effect.
  • Although it was raining, I went out. A contrast inside one sentence. — Although plus a clause.
  • It was raining. However, we went out. A contrast across two sentences. — However needs a full stop.

👀 What do you observe?

Because gives the reason and so gives the result; they are opposites and never appear in the same sentence. Although introduces a contrast inside one sentence and takes a subject and verb. However does the same job across a sentence boundary, with a comma after it. Because of and despite take a noun instead of a clause.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
because reason, plus a clause because it was raining
so result, plus a clause so I stayed in
although contrast, plus a clause although it was raining
however contrast, new sentence However, we went out.
because of / despite plus a noun because of the rain
so that purpose, plus a clause so that we could leave

Formula / rule: Ask what the second idea does: if it explains why, use because; if it tells you what happened next, use so; if it pushes against the first idea, use although inside the sentence or however at the start of a new one.

★ Why it matters

These four words carry the logic of a paragraph. Mixing because and so in one sentence, or using however like a conjunction, makes the argument hard to follow even when every word is correct.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers start sentences with so constantly, and with but, though less in writing. However is mostly written; in speech people say but.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Because and although are unstressed and the clause they introduce takes the stress: I stayed in because it was RAINing.

Additional example bank
  • Reason: She left because the meeting had finished.
  • Result: The meeting had finished, so she left.
  • Contrast: Although she was tired, she stayed.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: Because it was raining, so I stayed in (because and so in one sentence)

✅ Right: Because it was raining, I stayed in

❌ Wrong: Although it was raining. I went out (although needs one sentence)

✅ Right: Although it was raining, I went out
