Level A2 Grammar

Uses of Go

Use go for movement, activities and change.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use go for movement, for activities with -ing and for changes of state.
Examples in context
  • We go to work by train. Movement to a place. — Go to plus a place.
  • They went swimming after lunch. An activity. — Go plus -ing.
  • The milk has gone bad. A change of state. — Go plus an adjective.
  • How did the interview go? Progress or outcome. — Go alone, no object.

👀 What do you observe?

Go describes movement towards a place, usually with to, but home and abroad take no preposition. Go plus -ing names an activity: go swimming, go shopping, go running. Go plus an adjective describes a change for the worse: go bad, go wrong, go grey. Go alone means turn out or progress: the meeting went well. The past is went and the participle is gone.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
go to + place movement go to work
go + home / abroad no preposition go home, go abroad
go + -ing an activity go swimming, go shopping
go + adjective a change, usually for the worse go bad, go wrong
go alone progress or outcome the interview went well
went / gone irregular forms we went, it has gone

Formula / rule: Decide what follows go: a place with to, an activity with -ing, an adjective for a change of state, or nothing at all for progress. Remember home and abroad take no to.

★ Why it matters

Go covers movement, leisure and change, which is most of what a day contains, and the -ing pattern is how English names going out to do something rather than simply travelling.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers say go and get and go and see, joining two verbs with and, and use go for in the sense of choose: I'll go for the cheaper one.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Go is unstressed in the phrase and the activity is stressed: go SWIMming. In went the vowel is long.

Additional example bank
  • Movement: She goes to school by bike.
  • Activity: We went hiking in the hills.
  • Change: The plan went wrong at the last minute.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: We go to home by train (to before home)

✅ Right: We go home by train

❌ Wrong: They went to swim after lunch (for the activity)

✅ Right: They went swimming after lunch
