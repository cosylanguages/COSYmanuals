Level A2 Grammar

Most, Most of, The Most

Talk about the majority, and about the maximum.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use most for a general majority, most of for a known group and the most for a superlative.
Examples in context
  • Most people prefer the short route. A general majority. — No the before most.
  • Most of the students passed the exam. A known group. — Of the after most.
  • She is the most experienced member. Superlative of a long adjective. — The most plus adjective.

👀 What do you observe?

Most without the and without of states a general majority: most people, most water. Most of needs a determiner after it: most of the students, most of my time. The most forms the superlative of a long adjective: the most expensive, and of an adverb: most quickly. Most alone can also mean very in formal writing: a most unusual case.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
most + plural or uncountable noun general majority most people, most water
most of + the / my + noun a known group most of the students
the most + adjective superlative the most expensive
the most + adverb superlative of manner most quickly
a most + adjective formal, means very a most unusual case
almost all near-total almost all the students

Formula / rule: Ask whether the group is general or already known: general takes most plus noun, known takes most of plus the or a possessive. Add the when you rank one thing above the rest.

★ Why it matters

The presence or absence of of and the marks whether you talk about people in general or a group the listener already knows, and getting it wrong changes what the sentence refers to rather than just sounding foreign.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers say most of them and most of us with pronouns, never most them. In speech the most is often replaced by the -est form even with long adjectives: the prettiest rather than the most pretty.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Most is stressed in the majority sense: MOST people. In the superlative the adjective is stressed: the most exPENsive.

Additional example bank
  • Majority: Most cities have a bus service.
  • Known group: Most of the cities in the north have trams.
  • Superlative: That was the most useful lesson.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: Most of people prefer the short route (of without a determiner)

✅ Right: Most people prefer the short route

❌ Wrong: She is most experienced member (missing the)

✅ Right: She is the most experienced member
