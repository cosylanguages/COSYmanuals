A vs B contrast pairs, decision rules, and usage guides for elementary English learners

👀 What do you notice?

🧠 Logic Map: Core A2 Elementary Contrast Systems

Structure AStructure BCore Logic DifferenceKey Examples
Past Simple Present Perfect Finished time (yesterday, in 2020) vs. Unfinished time / Life experience (ever, never, so far) I visited Paris in 2022. vs. I have visited Paris twice.
Will Going to Instant decision or prediction without evidence vs. Prior plan, intention, or clear evidence I'll carry that bag for you! vs. Look at those dark clouds, it's going to rain.
Going to (Future) Present Continuous (Future) Intention/plan vs. Fixed arrangement with time, date, or other person confirmed I am going to study tonight. vs. I am meeting my boss at 3 PM.
Must / Have to Don't have to vs Mustn't Obligation vs. Optional (no necessity) vs. Strictly forbidden rule You must wear a seatbelt. | You don't have to pay. | You mustn't smoke here.
Too Enough Excessive (negative problem, before adj) vs. Sufficient (positive/neutral, after adj) This tea is too hot. vs. He is tall enough to play basketball.
Another Other / Others / The other An + other + singular noun vs. Other + plural noun vs. Pronoun vs. Final remaining option another apple | other books | some like tea, others like coffee | the other shoe
In / On (Place) Into / Onto (Movement) Static position inside/on surface vs. Dynamic directional movement into/onto surface He is in the room. vs. He walked into the room.

🗣️ Pronunciation & Spoken Contrast Note

Notice how modal contractions change spoken force: mustn't drops the 't' sound (/ˈmʌsnt/), while don't have to reduces to /doʊnt hæv tə/ in rapid natural speech.

❌You mustn't pay for the ticket, it is free.

✅You don't have to pay for the ticket, it is free.
('Don't have to' = optional/unnecessary; 'mustn't' = illegal/forbidden)
❌I have seen him yesterday.
tap to see the correct form
✅I saw him yesterday.
('Yesterday' is a finished time marker, requiring Past Simple)

tap to see the correct form
✅This jacket is big enough for me.
