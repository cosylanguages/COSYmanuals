Present Perfect: Ever, Never, For, Since

Life experiences and how long something has lasted

WordMeaning / UseExample
EVERquestions: at any timeHave you ever been to Japan?
NEVERnegative experienceI've never tried sushi.
FORa period of time (how long)I've lived here for three years.
SINCEa starting point in timeI've lived here since 2021.

👀 Notice: A length of time, or a starting point?

Can you count it (three years, two weeks, five minutes)? Use for. Is it a fixed point you could circle on a calendar (2021, last Monday, my birthday)? Use since.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Present Perfect: Ever, Never, For, Since, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples
  • They've been married for ten years.
  • He's worked here since January.
