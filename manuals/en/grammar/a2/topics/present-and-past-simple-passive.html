Level A2 Grammar

Present and Past Simple Passive

Say what happens to something, not who does it.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can describe processes and past events with be plus the past participle when the doer is unknown or unimportant.
Examples in context
  • Bread is baked every morning. Present passive, routine. — The baker is not named.
  • The letter was sent yesterday. Past passive, single event. — Agent unimportant.
  • These cars are made in Japan. Present passive, origin. — Where matters, not who.

👀 What do you observe?

The passive puts the receiver of the action first. Form it with be in the right tense plus the past participle: is baked, are made, was sent, were broken. Use by plus the agent only when the agent matters. The present passive describes routines, facts and processes; the past passive describes finished events.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
am / is / are + past participle present passive bread is baked
was / were + past participle past passive the letter was sent
by + agent only when it matters the novel was written by Orwell
negative not before the participle the shop is not opened on Sundays
questions invert be and the subject was the letter sent?
irregular participles third column of the verb broken, sent, made, written

Formula / rule: Choose the passive when the action matters more than the doer. Be in the tense you need plus the past participle, and by plus the agent only if the reader needs to know who did it.

★ Why it matters

Instructions, news reports, science and process descriptions all use the passive because the object of the action is the subject of the sentence. Getting was sent and is sent right is what separates a clear report from an ambiguous one.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers drop the agent almost every time: my car was stolen is normal, my car was stolen by someone is odd. The by phrase survives mainly in formal writing and in names of works.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The participle is stressed: the letter was SENT. Be is weak and contracts after a pronoun: it's cleaned, they're made.

Additional example bank
  • Present: The forms are checked twice.
  • Past: The bridge was built in 1890.
  • With an agent: The report was written by the finance team.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: The letter was send yesterday (base verb instead of participle)

✅ Right: The letter was sent yesterday

❌ Wrong: The bread is bake every morning (missing participle)

✅ Right: The bread is baked every morning
