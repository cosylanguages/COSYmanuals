Level A2 Grammar

On Time, In Time, At the End, In the End

Choose the right time phrase.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can say on time for a schedule, in time for a deadline, at the end for a point and in the end for a conclusion.
Examples in context
  • The train left on time. Exactly to the timetable. — No early, no late.
  • We arrived just in time for the show. Before it was too late. — In time for something.
  • There is a quiz at the end of each unit. A position, the final part. — At the end of.
  • In the end, we took the cheaper flat. After everything, finally. — A conclusion, not a position.

👀 What do you observe?

On time means at the scheduled moment. In time means before a deadline, often followed by for or to. At the end refers to the final point of something, followed by of. In the end means finally, after a process of deciding or waiting, and takes no of.

Formula / rule: Decide whether you mean schedule (on time), deadline (in time), position in something (at the end of) or conclusion after a process (in the end). Of follows the ones that name a thing, not the one that summarises.

★ Why it matters

These phrases look alike and are used constantly in work and travel talk, and on time against in time is the difference between a punctual arrival and a narrow escape.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use just in time for a close call and right on time for exact punctuality. In the end is often replaced by eventually in writing.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The preposition carries little stress; the key word does: on TIME, in TIME, at the END, in the END.

Additional example bank
  • To schedule: The meeting started on time.
  • Before a deadline: We finished in time to catch the train.
  • Finally: In the end, nobody complained.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: We arrived on time for the show (schedule where a deadline is meant)

✅ Right: We arrived in time for the show

❌ Wrong: In the end of the film, she leaves (conclusion where a position is meant)

✅ Right: At the end of the film, she leaves
