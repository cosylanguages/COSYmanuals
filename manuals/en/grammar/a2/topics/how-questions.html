Level A2 Grammar

How Questions

Ask about manner, quantity, age and frequency.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can ask with how, how much, how many, how long, how often and how far.
Examples in context
  • How do you get to work? Manner. — How plus do plus subject.
  • How much does it cost? Price, uncountable. — Much for an amount.
  • How many people came? Count, countable. — Many with a plural noun.
  • How long does the journey take? Duration. — Long for time.

👀 What do you observe?

How alone asks about manner or method. Combined with an adjective or adverb it asks about a quantity or a measurement: how much for uncountable amounts and price, how many for countable things, how long for duration, how often for frequency, how far for distance and how old for age. The auxiliary comes straight after the how phrase.

Formula / rule: Choose the how phrase that matches the kind of answer you want, then keep the normal question order: how phrase, auxiliary, subject, verb.

★ Why it matters

The how family is how you ask for numbers and measurements without guessing the wrong noun, and much against many is the pair learners get wrong most because some languages use one word for both.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers answer how questions with a bare phrase and no verb: about twenty minutes, twice a week, ten kilometres. Asking how is also the standard way to open a service conversation.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The how phrase takes the stress and the rest of the question falls: HOW MUCH does it COST?

Additional example bank
  • Manner: How did you fix it?
  • Frequency: How often does the bus run?
  • Distance: How far is the airport from here?
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: How much people came (much with a countable noun)

✅ Right: How many people came

❌ Wrong: How long do you go to the gym (long for frequency)

✅ Right: How often do you go to the gym
