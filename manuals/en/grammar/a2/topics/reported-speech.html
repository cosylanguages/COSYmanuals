Reported Speech

Repeat what somebody said.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can report a statement by moving the tense back one step and changing the pronouns.
Examples in context
  • "I'm tired." She said she was tired. Present becomes past. — Am becomes was.
  • "I'll call." He told me he would call. Will becomes would. — Tell needs the person.
  • "We have finished." They said they had finished. Present perfect becomes past perfect. — Have becomes had.

👀 What do you observe?

In reported speech the tense usually moves back one step: present to past, past to past perfect, will to would, can to could, may to might. Pronouns and time words change to fit the new speaker and moment: today becomes that day, tomorrow the next day, here there. Say needs no person, tell needs one: she said she was tired, she told me she was tired.

past to past perfect tense shift we finished, they had finished
will to would modal shift I'll call, he would call
can to could modal shift I can't come, she couldn't come
say / tell tell takes a person she told me
time words today, tomorrow, here that day, the next day, there

Formula / rule: Move the tense one step back, change the pronouns to the reporter's point of view and adjust the time and place words. Choose say when no person is mentioned and tell when there is one.

★ Why it matters

It is how news, messages and meetings are passed on, and the tense shift is what shows the listener that the words were spoken earlier rather than now.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers often keep the original tense when the situation is still true: she said she is tired. The backshift is the careful version, and the one written exams expect.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The reporting verb is unstressed and the reported clause carries the beat: she said she was TIRED.

Additional example bank
  • Statement: He said he had lost his keys.
  • Promise: She told me she would help.
  • Time shift: They said they would arrive the next day.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: She said me she was tired (say with a person)

✅ Right: She told me she was tired

❌ Wrong: He said he will call (no backshift in careful reporting)

✅ Right: He said he would call
