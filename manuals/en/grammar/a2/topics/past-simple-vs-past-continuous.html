Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Finished past actions vs past background activities in progress (Points 67 & 68)

🎯 Communicative Goal: Tell stories about past events, set background scenes, and describe interruptions using Past Continuous and Past Simple together.

👀 What do you notice?

Compare these two sentences:

  • "I cooked dinner yesterday." (Completed past action)
  • "I was cooking dinner when the phone rang." (Background activity in progress interrupted by a short event)

💡 Check your understanding (CCQs)

Score: 0 / 2
1. Which action started first in 'I was sleeping when the phone rang'?
Correct · Past Continuous sets the ongoing background scene; Past Simple interrupts it.
2. What structure is used for Past Continuous?
Correct · Form: Subject + was/were + verb-ing.

1. Past Simple vs Past Continuous Comparison

FeaturePast SimplePast Continuous
Core IdeaCompleted, finished action at a specific time in the pastAction in progress at a specific moment in the past
StructureVerb + -ed / irregular form (e.g. watched, went)was / were + verb-ing (e.g. was watching, were going)
ExamplesI watched a film yesterday.I was watching a film at 8 PM last night.

2. Combining Both: Background Action + Interruption (Point 68)

Rule: Use Past Continuous for the long background activity, and Past Simple for the short interrupting event.

  • ✅ I was sleeping (long) when the phone rang (short).
  • ✅ She was walking home when she saw an accident.
  • ✅ While I was studying, my friend called me.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
