Level A2 Grammar

Uses of Get

Use one verb for obtaining, becoming and arriving.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use get for obtaining, receiving, becoming, arriving and understanding.
Examples in context
  • I need to get some milk. Obtain. — Get plus a thing.
  • She got tired after the walk. Become. — Get plus an adjective.
  • We got home at nine. Arrive. — Get plus a place.
  • I didn't get the joke. Understand. — Get plus an idea.

👀 What do you observe?

Get is the most flexible verb in English. With a noun it means obtain or receive: get a ticket, get an email. With an adjective it means become: get tired, get cold, get better. With a place it means arrive: get home, get to London. With an idea it means understand: get the point. It also forms phrasal verbs: get up, get on, get over, get rid of.

get + idea understand get the point
get + past participle have something done get my hair cut
phrasal verbs with up, on, over, rid of get up, get on, get over it

Formula / rule: Read the word after get: a thing means obtain, an adjective means become, a place means arrive, an idea means understand. Add to before a named place, but not before home.

★ Why it matters

Get replaces a dozen more formal verbs in everyday speech, so recognising which meaning is in play is what allows you to follow a conversation at natural speed.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers use get for the passive in speech: he got fired, it got broken. That form is informal but very common, and it always implies something happened to the subject.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Get is unstressed unless it carries the meaning: I need to GET some milk. In get up the stress falls on up.

Additional example bank
  • Obtain: Where did you get that jacket?
  • Become: It gets dark early in winter.
  • Arrive: What time did you get to the airport?
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: We got to home at nine (to before home)

✅ Right: We got home at nine

❌ Wrong: She got tire after the walk (noun instead of adjective)

✅ Right: She got tired after the walk
