FunctionWordExample
addingANDI have a brother and a sister.
contrastBUTShe is small but strong.
choiceORDo you want tea or coffee?
reasonBECAUSEI'm happy because it's my birthday.
resultSOIt was raining, so we stayed home.

Because gives a reason (why?); so gives a result (what happened next).

★ Why this matters

These five tiny words let you build longer, more natural sentences instead of speaking only in short, disconnected ones · a big step toward sounding fluent.

⚠️ Common mistakes

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Conjunctions, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples
  • We like pizza and pasta.
  • He is tired, but he is still working.
  • I stayed home because I was ill.
  • She studied hard, so she passed the exam.
