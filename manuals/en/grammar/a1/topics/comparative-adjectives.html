Comparative Adjectives & Equality

Comparing two things (More, Less, As...as)

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can compare two items using -er/more...than (superiority), less...than (inferiority), as...as (equality), and handle irregular forms like good ➔ better.

👀 What do you notice?

Observe how comparisons change depending on direction and adjective length:

  • London is bigger than Oxford. (Short adjective: +er than)
  • A car is more expensive than a bike. (Long adjective: more...than)
  • This tea is less hot than before. (Inferiority: less...than)
  • She is as tall as her brother. (Equality: as...as)
  • This restaurant is better than the other one. (Irregular: good ➔ better, NOT more good!)

💡 -er or more?

Short word (big, small, nice)? Add -er. Long word (expensive, beautiful)? Don't change it · add more before it instead.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Notice how 'than' reduces to /ðən/ in natural speech: taller than me /tɔːlə ðən miː/.

More examples
  • She is taller than her brother.
  • This book is less interesting than the movie.
  • He speaks English better than last year.
