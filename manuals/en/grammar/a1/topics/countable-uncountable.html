CountableUncountable
MeaningYou can count them: 1, 2, 3…You cannot count them separately
Singular/Plurala book, two booksno plural: water, not waters
Examplesapple, chair, dog, friend, eggwater, milk, rice, money, music, time, information

Tip: uncountable nouns are always singular and never follow a/an or a number. To count them, use a container word: a glass of water, a piece of information.
