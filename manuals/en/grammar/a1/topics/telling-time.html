Ask: What time is it? / What's the time? Answer: It's 3 o'clock. It's half past three. It's quarter to three. Other minutes follow the same pattern: five past three, twenty to three.

💡 Writing dates in numbers

Writing the date 5th July 2026 in numbers: UKAU 05/07/2026 (day/month/year) · US 07/05/2026 (month/day/year). CA both are used. Spelling the month out (5 July / July 5) always avoids confusion.
