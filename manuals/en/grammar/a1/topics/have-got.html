🌐 Around the English-speaking world

UK Have got is the everyday form for possession: I've got a bike.

USCA Both have got and plain have are used and understood: I've got a bike = I have a bike.

AU Follows British usage · have got is very common.

Everywhere: for the past, use had only · have got has no separate past form.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing Have Got, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples
  • We've got a big house.
  • Has he got blue eyes?
  • They haven't got any children.
  • I've got two brothers.
