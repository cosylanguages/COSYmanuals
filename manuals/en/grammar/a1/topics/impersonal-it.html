It’s Cold, It’s Five O’Clock: Impersonal It

Weather, time and days with ‘it’

Rule: Use it’s (= it is) as the subject to talk about weather, time, days and dates. It has no meaning here, but English needs a subject. Do not mix it up with there is / there are, which says that something exists.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Many languages leave the subject out when they talk about the weather: Spanish Llueve, Italian Piove, Russian Идёт дождь. English cannot: it always puts it first.
