★ Why this matters

This is the phrase you'll use in every café, shop, and restaurant · it's often the very first 'polite' structure learners get right.

SUBJECTI
=
MODALwould
FunctionExample
Polite requestI'd like a coffee, please. (= I want, but more polite)
OfferWould you like some tea?
Positive answerYes, please. I'd love some.
Negative answerNo, thank you. I'm fine.

I'd = I would. Use I'd like (not I want) to sound polite in shops, cafés and restaurants.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing I'd Like / Would You Like…?, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples
  • I'd like a sandwich, please.
  • Would you like to come to my house?
  • I'd like two tickets, please.
