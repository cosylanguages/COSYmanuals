Work Collocations: Have, Make, Do, Take, Send

Verbs that go together with work words

Rule: A collocation is a pair of words that usually go together. Learn verb + noun as one piece: have a meeting. If you change the verb, people may understand you, but it sounds wrong. There is no simple rule for which verb to choose, so learn the pairs.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers do not build these pairs word by word. They remember make a call as one chunk, the way you remember a phone number.
