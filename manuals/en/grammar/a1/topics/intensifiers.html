strong, informal · REALLYI'm really tired today.
medium · QUITEThe film was quite good.
too much (problem) · TOOThis soup is too salty for me.

Very / really make an adjective stronger (no problem). Too means more than necessary · it usually shows a problem: too hot = we can't drink it yet.

💭 Think like a native speaker

English speakers distinguish 'strong' from 'too much' with completely different words (very vs too) · many languages use just one intensifier and let context carry the difference.
