RuleExample
most verbs: + edwork → worked, play → played
ends in -e: + dlive → lived, like → liked
consonant + y: y → iedstudy → studied, cry → cried
1 vowel + consonant: doublestop → stopped, plan → planned
🔊 Pronunciation

Past tense -ed is pronounced /ɪd/ after /t/ or /d/ (wanted, needed), /t/ after unvoiced sounds (worked, stopped), and /d/ after voiced sounds (played, lived).

💡 Memory trick

STOP is short · one vowel, one final consonant. Picture the last letter looking in a mirror and doubling itself before -ed jumps on: stop → stopped.
