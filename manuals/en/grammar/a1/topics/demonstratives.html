Rule: use this/that before a singular noun and these/those before a plural noun. This book / These books.

This vs. it: use this to introduce something new (This is my brother.); once it's known, use it (It's very tall.).

💭 Think like a native speaker

English only marks distance and number here · not gender. French needs ce/cette, but English keeps it to one less thing to track.

🗣️ Pronunciation Note
