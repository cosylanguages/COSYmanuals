🧠 Logic Map: Core A0–A1 Present & Structure Contrasts

Structure AStructure BCore Logic DifferenceKey Examples
Present Simple Present Continuous Habits, routines & facts vs. Happening now or temporary I drink coffee every day. vs. I am drinking coffee now.
Do / Does Is / Are Action verbs vs. Adjectives, nouns, places or -ing continuous Does she work here? vs. Is she at work? / Is she working?
Don't / Doesn't Not / Am not / Isn't Negative for verbs vs. Negative for adjectives/nouns/be I don't know. vs. I am not tired.
No Not No + noun (zero quantity) vs. Not + adjective/verb There is no water. vs. The water is not hot.
In (Time) On (Time) / At (Time) In + long periods (months/years) vs. On + days/dates vs. At + exact time in July, in 2026 | on Monday, on May 5 | at 8:00, at noon
In (Place) On (Place) / At (Place) In = inside container/city vs. On = surface vs. At = specific point/location in the bag, in Paris | on the table | at the door, at the station
Every All Every + singular noun (each member) vs. All + plural noun (group together) Every student has a book. vs. All students have books.
Every day Everyday Two words = adverb (each day) vs. One word = adjective (normal/routine) I study every day. vs. These are my everyday shoes.

🗣️ Pronunciation & Grammar Hint

Remember the golden rule for DOES: After Does or Doesn't, the main verb drops its '-s' and stays simple! Say: "Does she like tea?" (NOT ❌ "Does she likes tea?").

⚠️ Common Beginner Mistake Flips
