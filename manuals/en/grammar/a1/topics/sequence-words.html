Sequence Words: First, Then, Next, Finally

Putting actions in order

Rule: Put the sequence word at the start of the sentence. First, Next, After that and Finally usually take a comma. Then usually does not. Keep the sentences in the same order as the events.

💭 Think like a native speaker

In English the order of the sentences usually follows the order of the events, and sequence words make that order clear. They are common in instructions, recipes and when we tell a story about our day.

💡 Memory trick

Think of steps on a staircase: First → Then / Next → After that → Finally. One word for each step.
