Like / Love / Prefer / Hate + -ing

Talking about preferences and desires

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can accurately express degrees of preference (love, like, prefer, don't like, hate) and contrast long-term likes with spontaneous desires (feel like / want to).

👀 What do you notice?

Compare how different preference verbs express intensity and choice:

  • I like tea, but I love coffee. (General appreciation vs strong passion)
  • I prefer tea to coffee. (Choosing option A over option B)
  • I don't like fish (mild dislike) vs I hate waiting (strong aversion).
  • I feel like eating pizza tonight. (Spontaneous desire right now)

VerbMeaning & NuanceExample
LOVEStrong passion / favorite activityI love dancing on Friday nights.
LIKEGeneral positive preferenceShe likes reading before bed.
PREFERChoice between options (Prefer A to B)I prefer tea to coffee.
DON'T MINDNeutral / acceptableHe doesn't mind waiting a little.
DON'T LIKEMild negative opinionI don't like cold rainy weather.
HATEStrong negative aversionThey hate cleaning the kitchen.
FEEL LIKESpontaneous desire right now (+ -ING)I feel like watching a movie tonight.

Rule: after like, love, prefer, hate, don't mind, enjoy, feel like, the following verb takes the -ing form. I like swimming. I feel like eating out.

💡 Think like English

Treating a verb as a noun (swimming, reading) by adding -ing is standard in English when discussing preferences. Use prefer A to B when choosing between two options.

⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I prefer coffee than tea.
tap to see the correct form
✅I prefer coffee to tea. (Use 'to', not 'than', with prefer!)
❌I feel like to go to the beach.
tap to see the correct form
✅I feel like going to the beach. (Feel like + -ing!)

🗣️ Pronunciation Note

Focus on natural sentence rhythm: emphasize the main content words when expressing preferences, while keeping grammatical function words unstressed.

More examples
  • I love watching football.
  • She prefers tea to coffee.
  • We hate waiting in long queues.
  • Do you feel like going for a walk?
