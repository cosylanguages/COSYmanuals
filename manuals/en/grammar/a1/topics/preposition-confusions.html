Preposition Confusions Master Guide

Clear A vs B contrast rules for time, location, movement, purpose, and direction

🎯 Communicative Goal: Use the right prepositions for time, place, travel, tools, and movement without confusing similar prepositions.

👀 What do you notice?

Observe how prepositions change meaning:

  • "I went to London." (Destination) vs. "I went to London for work." (Purpose)
  • "I travel by train." (Method of transport) vs. "I cut it with a knife." (Tool/Instrument)
  • "The cat is in the box." (Static position) vs. "The cat jumped into the box." (Movement)

🧠 Concept Check Questions (CCQs)

Score: 0 / 3
1. Do we use 'by' or 'with' for instruments and tools you hold in your hand?
Correct · Use 'with' for physical tools and 'by' for transport methods/agents.
2. What is the difference between 'in front of' and 'before'?
Correct · 'In front of' refers to physical position (in front of the building); 'before' refers to time/order.
3. What preposition follows 'during'?
Correct · 'During' is followed by a noun (during the movie); 'while' is followed by a subject + verb clause.

1. IN vs ON vs AT (Time & Place)

PrepositionTime RulesPlace Rules
IN Long periods (months, years, seasons): in May, in 2026, in summer, in the morning Inside enclosed space, city, country: in the room, in Paris, in France
ON Days and dates: on Monday, on my birthday, on 5 May Touching a surface, floor, street: on the table, on the wall, on the 2nd floor
AT Exact time point: at 5 o'clock, at midnight, at lunchtime Specific location point: at the door, at the station, at work, at school

2. Direction & Origin Contrasts

PairDifference & LogicExamples
TO vs FOR To = Direction / destination
For = Purpose / benefit / duration		 I am going to Paris.
This gift is for you. I studied for 2 hours.
FROM vs OF From = Origin / starting point
Of = Belonging / relationship / part		 I am from Spain.
The capital of France. A cup of tea.
BY vs WITH By = Transport method / agent
With = Physical tool / accompaniment		 I travel by bus. A book by Shakespeare.
I write with a pen. I came with my friend.
ABOUT vs OF About = Topic / subject
Of = Relationship / connection		 We talked about movies.
I know about him. vs. I know him.
INTO vs IN In = Position inside (static)
Into = Movement from outside to inside		 The keys are in the bag.
Put the keys into the bag.
OUT OF vs FROM Out of = Movement inside to outside
From = General origin		 He walked out of the house.
He came from London.

3. Spatial Position & Time Prepositions

  • BETWEEN vs AMONG: Between = two distinct things (between tea and coffee). Among = surrounded by a group (among friends).
  • ABOVE vs OVER: Above = higher level (a picture above the sofa). Over = movement above or covering (the plane flew over the city).
  • UNDER vs BELOW: Under = directly underneath (under the chair). Below = lower level generally (below zero).
  • NEXT TO vs NEAR vs CLOSE TO: Next to = immediately beside. Near / Close to = nearby, short distance away.
  • IN FRONT OF vs BEFORE: In front of = physical position (in front of the shop). Before = time/order (before 5 PM).
  • AFTER vs LATER: After + noun (after work). Later = standalone adverb (See you later).
  • DURING vs WHILE: During + noun (during the lesson). While + clause (while I was studying).
  • UNTIL vs BY: Until = action continues up to a time (wait until 5). By = deadline (finish by Friday).
