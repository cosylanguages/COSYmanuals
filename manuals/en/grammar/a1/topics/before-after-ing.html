Before & After + -ing

Sequencing time and actions clearly

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can sequence your daily activities smoothly by connecting actions with "before + verb-ing" and "after + verb-ing".

👀 What do you notice?

Notice the verb form after before and after when there is no subject pronoun:

  • Before leaving the house, I turn off the lights.
  • After eating lunch, we went for a walk.
  • Wash your hands before eating!

1. What form does a verb take immediately following prepositions like 'before' or 'after'?
Correct · Prepositions are followed by -ing verbs (gerunds) or nouns.
2. Can you also use a full clause with a subject (e.g., "after I ate")?
Correct · If a subject is present, use a conjugated verb; if no subject is present, use -ing.
Structure Pattern Formula Example
Preposition + Gerund Before / After + Verb-ING Before going to bed, read a book.
After finishing work, I relax.
Preposition + Noun Before / After + Noun Before class, drink coffee.
After dinner, watch TV.
Preposition + Subject Clause Before / After + Subject + Verb Before I go to bed, I read.
After we finished work, we met up.

Rule Summary:
• Prepositions (before, after, by, without, for) require a noun or an -ing verb.
• Do NOT use base verbs after before/after (❌ before to go / ❌ before go). Always use before going or before I go.

🧠 Think like English

Prepositions in English can never take an infinitive verb (unlike French or Spanish). Whenever a preposition is directly followed by an action, English automatically transforms the action into a noun form using -ing.

⚠️ Common mistakes
❌Before to leave, check your keys.
tap to see the correct form
✅Before leaving, check your keys. (Never use 'to + infinitive' after before/after!)
❌After eat dinner, I go for a walk.

💡 Memory Trick

The Preposition -ING Rule:
Preposition + Action = -ING Action!
Before starting, after finishing, without stopping!
