Level B2 Grammar

Subject-Predicate Agreement

Make the verb agree with the real subject, not the nearest noun.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can keep the verb in agreement when long subjects and collective nouns intervene.
Examples in context
  • The number of complaints has fallen sharply. — The head is number, singular.
  • A number of complaints have been resolved. — Here a number of means several.
  • The team are arguing among themselves. — British English allows a plural verb for a group in dispute.

👀 What do you observe?

The verb agrees with the head of the subject, whatever sits between the two. Prepositional phrases, relative clauses and lists are noise. Collective nouns take a singular verb when they act as one body and a plural verb when the members act separately.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
the list of + plural noun singular verb the list of candidates was published
a number of + plural noun plural verb a number of complaints have been resolved
the number of + plural noun singular verb the number of complaints has fallen
neither X nor Y verb agrees with Y neither the manager nor the drivers were informed
each of / one of singular verb each of the proposals needs a budget
collective noun singular body, plural members the committee has decided / the team are arguing

Formula / rule: Find the head noun, then make the verb agree with it. Intervening phrases starting with of, with, together with, as well as and including never change the number: the list of candidates was, not were.

★ Why it matters

Agreement errors are the most visible kind of slip in formal writing, and long subjects are where they happen. A reader who sees the list of candidates were stops trusting the sentence, even if the point is sound.

💭 Think like a native speaker

In speech, native speakers often say there's with a plural following (there's three options), and listeners do not notice. In writing, that same sentence is an error, so write there are three options.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The -s ending on a verb marks singular in the present tense, which is the opposite of the noun, where -s marks plural: the driver drives, the drivers drive.

Additional example bank

⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: The list of candidates were published (verb agrees with candidates)

✅ Right: The list of candidates was published

❌ Wrong: Each of the proposals need a budget (plural verb after each)

✅ Right: Each of the proposals needs a budget
