Level B2 Grammar

Whatever, Whenever, Wherever, Whoever, However

Leave the choice open, or concede the extent.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use the -ever words for open choice, concession and degree.
Examples in context
  • Take whatever you need. Open choice, no limit. — Whatever as a pronoun.
  • Whatever the cost, we proceed. Concession. — Whatever + noun.
  • However late it is, call me. Concession of degree. — However + adjective or adverb.

👀 What do you observe?

The -ever words extend a question word to mean it does not matter which, where, who, when or how. Whatever, whoever and whichever work as pronouns or determiners; wherever and whenever work as adverbs; however is followed by an adjective or an adverb, never by a whole clause on its own. However also means but in formal writing, which is a separate job.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
whatever any thing, open choice take whatever you need
whoever any person whoever signed the form is responsible
whichever any one of a set whichever option you prefer
wherever / whenever any place, any time wherever the meeting is held
however + adjective any degree, concession however hard we try
however as a connector contrast in formal writing the terms were fair; however, the timing was not

Formula / rule: Use an -ever form when the exact item does not matter to the statement: whatever you decide, whoever signed, wherever it happens. When the point is degree, however comes first and the adjective follows immediately. Keep however the connector apart from however the intensifier, since the first takes a comma and the second does not.

★ Why it matters

The -ever forms are how English says that a condition is not negotiable: whatever the cost means the cost will not change the decision. In negotiation and policy writing they signal that a variable has been taken out of the argument, which is why they are frequent in contracts and speeches.

💭 Think like a native speaker

In speech, whatever is often a dismissive one-word reply, and that use is hostile; wherever and whenever are neutral. However at the start of a sentence meaning but is common in writing and rare in speech, where but does the work.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The -ever ending carries the stress: wherEVER, whenEVER, howEVER, and in whatever the stress falls on the second syllable.

Additional example bank
  • Choice: Whichever supplier you choose, the lead time is eight weeks.
  • Concession: Whatever the outcome, the process will be published.
  • Degree: However carefully we plan, the weather decides.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: However we try hard, the deadline will not move (adverb after however in the wrong order)

✅ Right: However hard we try, the deadline will not move

❌ Wrong: Whatever decision you take it, we support you (object and pronoun both kept)

✅ Right: Whatever decision you take, we support you
