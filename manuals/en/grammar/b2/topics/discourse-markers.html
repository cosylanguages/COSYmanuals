Level B2 Grammar

Discourse Markers

Show the reader how each sentence relates to the last.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can mark addition, contrast, concession, sequence and conclusion in formal prose.
Examples in context
  • Addition: Moreover, the sample was too small to generalise. — Adds a second point.
  • Concession: Admittedly, the cost is high; nevertheless, the case is sound. — Concedes, then holds.
  • Conclusion: In conclusion, the policy needs an owner. — Signals the end.
  • Reframing: As far as staffing is concerned, the picture is different. — Limits the topic.

👀 What do you observe?

A discourse marker names the relation between two sentences: addition (furthermore, moreover, in addition), contrast (however, nevertheless, in contrast), concession (admittedly, granted that, even so), sequence (first, subsequently, finally), result (consequently, as a result), and stance (arguably, in fact, roughly speaking). Most take a comma, and fronted ones usually take a comma after them.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
addition adds a supporting point furthermore, moreover, in addition
contrast sets two points against each other however, in contrast, by comparison
concession admits a point, keeps the case admittedly, granted that, even so
sequence orders the parts first, subsequently, finally
result states the consequence consequently, as a result, therefore
stance marks the writer's position arguably, in fact, on the contrary

Formula / rule: Choose the marker by the relation, then check the punctuation and the register. However and in contrast start a new sentence with a comma after them; although and whereas join clauses inside one sentence. On the contrary contradicts a previous statement, while on the other hand balances two sides, and confusing the two is the most common marker error at this level.

★ Why it matters

Markers are how a reader knows whether a sentence adds, contradicts or concludes, and a missing marker forces the reader to guess the relationship, which is where arguments lose their shape. They are also a register signal: moreover and consequently belong in reports, while plus and also belong in speech.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers overuse actually and basically in speech, and underuse moreover in speech; in writing they rely on however, therefore and by contrast. Note that In fact introduces a correction of an impression, not an addition.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Most markers of two syllables or more are unstressed except the one that carries the contrast, so however /haʊˈev.ə/ keeps the stress on the second syllable while moreover /mɔːrˈəʊ.və/ keeps it on the second as well.

Additional example bank
  • Contrast: Exports rose; in contrast, domestic demand fell.
  • Concession: Granted that the sample is small, the pattern is consistent.
  • Stance: On the contrary, the evidence points the other way.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: The plans were ambitious, however the funding never appeared (comma splice before however)

✅ Right: The plans were ambitious. However, the funding never appeared

❌ Wrong: On the other hand, the report says the opposite (opposition instead of contradiction)

✅ Right: On the contrary, the report says the opposite
