Mandative & Formulaic Subjunctive

Use the base verb after verbs of demand and adjectives of necessity.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can express formal demands, recommendations and fixed subjunctive expressions.
Examples in context
  • They demanded that he resign. Base verb after demand. — No -s, no tense change.
  • It is vital that the file be kept. Base be after an adjective of necessity. — Be, not is.
  • The board recommended that the plan be revised. Recommendation in formal register. — Same pattern after recommend.

👀 What do you observe?

The mandative subjunctive uses the base form of the verb after verbs of demand and suggestion (suggest, demand, insist, propose, recommend, request, urge) and after adjectives of necessity (essential, vital, crucial, imperative, necessary). The base form does not change with person or tense: it is essential that she be on time, they demanded that he leave, we insist that the report be published.

Formula / rule: Use the base verb, never a tense form, after the that-clause: that he be, that she leave, that it not proceed. In British English the indicative is acceptable in informal writing (that she is on time), but the subjunctive is expected in formal and academic registers. Keep not before the base verb in the negative.

★ Why it matters

The subjunctive is the marker of formal obligation: a recommendation written with the subjunctive reads as binding, while the same sentence in the indicative reads as a description. This is why contracts, minutes and academic recommendations keep it, and why its absence is noticed.

💭 Think like a native speaker

American English uses the subjunctive far more consistently than British English, where the indicative is common in speech (I suggest that he goes). In writing, both varieties accept the subjunctive after demand-verbs, and examiners expect it.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The base verb after that is unstressed, and the stress falls on the adjective of necessity: it is esSENtial that she be on time.

Additional example bank
  • Recommendation: The panel recommends that the report be published in full.
  • Necessity: It is vital that the data be checked twice.
  • Negative: They insisted that the meeting not be recorded.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: It is essential that she is on time in a formal recommendation (indicative in a mandatory clause)

✅ Right: It is essential that she be on time

❌ Wrong: They demanded that he leaves (third person -s in the subjunctive)

✅ Right: They demanded that he leave
