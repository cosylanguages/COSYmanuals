Prefixes & Negative Forms

Negate a word without changing its class.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can choose the right negative prefix instead of not + word.
Examples in context
  • unreliable The source proved unreliable. — un- before a native root.
  • illegal The payment was illegal. — il- before l.
  • misinterpret The press misinterpreted the ruling. — mis- means wrongly.

👀 What do you observe?

The prefix carries the negation, so the word class stays the same: reliable and unreliable are both adjectives. The choice among un-, in-, dis- and mis- is lexical, and in- assimilates to il- before l, im- before m and p, and ir- before r.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
un- negates, native roots unreliable, unclear, unfair
in- / il- / im- / ir- negates, Latinate roots incapable, illegal, impossible, irregular
dis- negates or reverses an action disagree, disconnect, disqualify
mis- wrongly misinterpret, misreport, mislead
anti- against anticompetitive, antidumping
non- not of that type non-binding, non-renewable

Formula / rule: Negate with a prefix when the word has a stable opposite (unfair, illegal, disagree). Use not when the negation is temporary or contrastive (the report is not finished, but not unfinishable). Never write unlegal or inlegal: the prefix is fixed by the root, so learn the pairs.

★ Why it matters

Prefixes are the fastest way to sound precise at B2. Misreport says the press got it wrong, while not report says it did not report at all; anti-competitive describes a design, while not competitive describes a result. The prefix carries the argument.

💭 Think like a native speaker

English has a few false negatives that look like prefixes: invaluable means extremely valuable, and inflammable means flammable. Trust the dictionary, not the prefix, for these.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Prefixes are unstressed except anti- and non-, so unreliable is /ˌʌn.rɪˈlaɪ.ə.bəl/ with the stress on the root, exactly as in reliable.

Additional example bank
  • un-: The evidence is uncontested but thin.
  • dis-: Two members disagreed with the ruling.
  • mis-: The figure was misquoted in three outlets.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: The payment was inlegal (wrong prefix for the root)

✅ Right: The payment was illegal

❌ Wrong: The audit was not completeable (not + derived adjective)

✅ Right: The audit was incomplete
