The whole B2 system on one page, in the order the exam tests it.

Examples in context
  • Mandative subjunctive It is essential that he be informed. — Base verb after demand verbs.
  • Distancing passive The report is said to reflect a trend. — Agent removed, claim reported.
  • Inversion Rarely has the board seen such a split. — Negative fronting inverts.
  • Hedging The proposal is arguably the safest. — Claim marked as debatable.

👀 What do you observe?

The B2 system has four families. Verb forms: mandative subjunctive, mixed conditionals, distancing passives, reporting patterns. Information order: fronting, inversion, cleft sentences. Compression and register: nominalisation, ellipsis, substitution. Stance: hedging, concession, rhetorical structure. An exam task rarely tests one family alone: a reformulation task asks you to move a clause into a noun phrase, and a transformation task asks you to invert after a negative adverb.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
verb forms subjunctive, conditionals, passives It is essential that he be informed
information order fronting, inversion, clefts Rarely has the board seen such a split
compression nominalisation, ellipsis, substitution The implementation of the policy took two years
stance hedging, concession, rhetoric The proposal is arguably the safest
reformulation clause to noun phrase When we automate → the automation of
transformation same meaning, required structure Not only did costs rise...

Formula / rule: In an exam, read the instruction before the sentence: transformation tasks name the structure (begin with Not only, rewrite using a noun phrase), while reformulation tasks only fix the meaning. Then check three things in order - verb form, information order, stance - because that is the order markers are lost.

★ Why it matters

The final unit of the course is a review, not new grammar: it exists so that the structures learned separately appear together, which is the condition under which they are examined. Answer the transformation first, then check the auxiliary and the agreement, because those are where the marks go.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Examiners reward control over ambition. A correctly hedged, correctly nominalised plain sentence scores better than a broken inversion, and the empty structure that reads well aloud is usually the right answer in writing.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

Read transformation answers aloud: the stress pattern tells you whether the auxiliary landed in the right place, since inversion and ellipsis both move the stressed element.

Additional example bank
  • Subjunctive: The committee demanded that the figures be released.
  • Inversion: Under no circumstances should the data be shared.
  • Compression: The refusal to publish delayed the audit.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: Not only the costs rose but quality fell (inversion missing)

✅ Right: Not only did the costs rise, but quality fell

❌ Wrong: It is essential that he is informed (indicative after a mandative verb)

✅ Right: It is essential that he be informed
