Gerunds and Infinitives

Choose the gerund, the infinitive or the perfect form by meaning.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can use the -ing form, the to-infinitive and their perfect forms with the right meaning.
Examples in context
  • He denied having seen the memo. Perfect gerund: earlier action. — Deny + having + participle.
  • I remember locking the door. Gerund: the memory of an action. — Remember + -ing.
  • I remembered to lock the door. Infinitive: the duty performed. — Remember + to.

👀 What do you observe?

The gerund names an activity as a fact or an experience: I enjoy reading, he denied taking it. The infinitive names a purpose, a potential or a duty: I remembered to lock it, we agreed to wait. After deny, admit, regret (a past action), and after prepositions, the gerund is the only option, and the perfect gerund (having + participle) marks an action earlier than the main verb.

Element / structure Role in English Application example
verb + -ing activity or experience he denied taking the file
verb + to-infinitive purpose or duty we agreed to wait
having + participle earlier action he denied having taken the file
after a preposition always -ing before signing, read the terms
mean / try / stop sense changes with the form it means waiting / it means to help
need + -ing or to be + participle passive need the plan needs reviewing

Formula / rule: Ask what the verb is doing: naming an activity (gerund), pointing at a purpose or a duty (infinitive), or reaching back before the main verb (perfect gerund). After a preposition, only the gerund is possible.

★ Why it matters

The choice is a choice of meaning, not of style: regret to inform announces bad news, regret informing admits that you passed it on. In formal writing the perfect gerund is what lets you report an earlier action without a second clause.

💭 Think like a native speaker

Native speakers keep remember, forget and try apart by the form alone, and the difference is taught early, which is why the wrong choice stands out sharply to a listener.

🗣️ Pronunciation note

The -ing ending is unstressed, while the to of the infinitive is weak /tə/ except when it introduces a purpose, where it carries a light stress.

Additional example bank
  • Admit: She admitted having received the figures early.
  • After a preposition: They left without telling the client.
  • Passive need: The contract needs renewing in June.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid

❌ Wrong: He denied to have seen the memo (infinitive with a perfect form after deny)

✅ Right: He denied having seen the memo

❌ Wrong: I am used to work late (infinitive after a preposition)

✅ Right: I am used to working late
