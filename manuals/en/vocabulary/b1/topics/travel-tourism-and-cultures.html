PET Topic: Travel & Places

Travel, Tourism & Cultures

Trip-planning in depth, cultural differences, describing cities, and travel writing style.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can plan complex travel itineraries, describe cultural customs, and write travel blog posts.
DomainVocabularyExample
Itinerary & Travelitinerary, destination, guided tour, excursionOur travel agent organized a full day excursion.
Culture & Customscultural shock, local customs, traditional cuisineExperiencing local customs is part of travelling.
Describing Placespicturesque, bustling, historical landmark, scenicVenice is famous for its picturesque canals.
More Examples
  • We enjoyed strolling through the bustling night market.
  • Follow the planned travel itinerary carefully.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌We made a very long travel last year.
tap to see the correct form
✅We went on a very long trip / journey last year. (Travel is uncountable general noun!)
