B1 Confusing Words Master Guide

Action verbs, knowledge pairs, workplace distinctions, and feeling adjective confusions

🎯 Master Goal: Confidently distinguish intermediate verb pairs, knowledge nuances, work vs career, and -ed/-ing feelings in conversation.

👀 What do you notice?

Compare these two sentences: "Please remind me to call the client." (Cause someone else to remember) vs. "I remember calling the client." (Recall a memory yourself). One word involves prompting another person!

💡 Check your understanding (CCQs)

Score: 0 / 2
1. Verbs check: "The sun rises in the east." Does 'rise' take a direct object or move upwards on its own?
Correct · 'Rise' is intransitive (goes up by itself), while 'raise' requires a direct object (raise your hand).
2. Feelings check: "The movie was very interesting." Does 'interesting' describe the cause or the person feeling the emotion?
Correct · '-ing' adjectives describe the cause/thing, whereas '-ed' adjectives describe the person's feeling (I am interested).

🧠 Logic Map: Core B1 Intermediate Semantic Confusion Groups

Word AWord BCore Semantic DifferenceKey Examples
Rise Raise Go up on its own (intransitive) vs Lift something up (transitive + object) Prices rise quickly. vs Raise your hand.
Lie Lay Rest horizontally yourself (lie down) vs Put an object down flat (lay the book down) I lie on the sofa. vs Lay the towels on the table.
Remember Remind Keep or recall a memory yourself vs Prompt someone else so they don't forget I remember my childhood. vs Remind me to buy milk.
Discover Invent Find something that already existed in nature vs Create a new machine/device for the first time discover gravity / penicillin vs invent the telephone
Job Work / Career Specific countable post (a job) vs Uncountable activity (work) vs Lifetime profession (career) I have a job. | I start work at 9. | a medical career
Fun Funny Enjoyable / pleasant activity (noun/adj) vs Amusing / makes you laugh (adj) The party was fun! vs That comedian is funny.
Interested Interesting Person feeling interest (-ed) vs Thing generating interest (-ing) I am interested in history. vs History is interesting.
