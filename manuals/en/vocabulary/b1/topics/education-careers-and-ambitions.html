Education, Careers & Ambitions

Qualifications, career paths, job applications, workplace relationships, and long-term goals.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can detail professional career ambitions, academic qualifications, and job application processes.
DomainVocabularyExample
Job ApplicationCV / résumé, cover letter, references, vacancyShe submitted her CV and cover letter for the post.
Career Progressionpromotion, internship, resignation, work-life balanceHe earned a promotion to senior project manager.
Qualificationsbachelor's degree, diploma, apprenticeshipShe completed a three-year apprenticeship.
More Examples
  • Achieving a good work-life balance is essential for wellbeing.
  • There is a job vacancy in our marketing department.
