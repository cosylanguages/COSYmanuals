Health, Fitness & Lifestyle

Illness treatment in depth, mental wellbeing, stress management, exercise, and diet.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss physical fitness routines, stress management, and mental health balance.
DomainVocabularyExample
Mental Wellbeingstress management, burnout, anxiety, mindfulnessPracticing mindfulness helps reduce exam anxiety.
Physical Fitnessendurance, strength training, workout routineShe does strength training three times a week.
Medical Treatmentrecovery, therapy, specialist, remedyHe made a full recovery after the surgery.
More Examples
  • Regular exercise is crucial for effective stress management.
  • The doctor referred her to a heart specialist.
