PET Topic: Tech & Media

Media, News & Communication Technology

News vocabulary, social media, smartphones, apps, viral trends, and online safety.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss digital media, social networks, online privacy, and viral technological trends.
DomainVocabularyExample
Digital Mediaheadline, broadcast, fake news, subscriberThe story made national headlines.
Social Networksgo viral, algorithm, notification, postHer video went viral overnight with 2 million views.
Cybersecurityprivacy settings, two-factor auth, scam, cloudAlways update your privacy settings on public apps.
More Examples
  • I turned off app notifications to stay focused.
  • Beware of online scams asking for banking details.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌The news are very interesting today.
tap to see the correct form
