Relationships & Social Life

Friendship dynamics, dating, conflict resolution, and social etiquette.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss interpersonal friendships, resolve minor conflicts, and express empathy.
DomainVocabularyExample
Friendshipclose friend, trustworthy, keep in touch, acquaintanceEven after moving abroad, we kept in touch.
Conflict & Resolutionmisunderstanding, apologize, compromise, resolveThey sat down to resolve their misunderstanding.
Social Etiquettepolite, host, guest, invitation, small talkMaking small talk at networking events is useful.
More Examples
  • He is an acquaintance from university, not a close friend.
  • We reached a fair compromise after talking for an hour.
