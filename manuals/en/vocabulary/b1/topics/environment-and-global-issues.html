PET Topic: Environment

Environment & Global Issues

Climate, sustainability basics, waste reduction, endangered species, and natural disasters.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss environmental protection, renewable energy, and ecological footprint.
TopicVocabularyExample
Sustainabilityrenewable energy, carbon footprint, biodegradableSolar power is a clean source of renewable energy.
Ecologyendangered species, habitat, deforestationLoss of forest habitat threatens many species.
Waste & Pollutionsingle-use plastic, landfill, emissionsWe must reduce our reliance on single-use plastic.
More Examples
  • Electric cars produce zero tailpipe emissions.
  • Tigers are classified as an endangered species.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌We must protect the nature.
tap to see the correct form
✅We must protect nature. (No article before 'nature' in general sense!)
