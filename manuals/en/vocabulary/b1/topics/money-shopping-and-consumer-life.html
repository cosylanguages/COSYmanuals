B1 Everyday Finance & Consumption

Money, Shopping & Consumer Life

Budgeting, saving, value for money, consumer rights, and digital banking.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss personal budgeting, financial transactions, and consumer rights.
DomainVocabularyExample
Finance & Budgetbudget, income, expense, savings account, interest rateI keep a monthly budget to track my expenses.
Consumer Lifevalue for money, warranty, receipt, exchange policyThe laptop comes with a two-year warranty.
Paymentscontactless, direct debit, currency exchangeI paid using contactless on my smartphone.
More Examples

  • Set up a direct debit to pay utility bills automatically.
