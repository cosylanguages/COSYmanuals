PET Topic: Feelings in Depth

Personality, Feelings & Abstract Qualities

Nuanced personality traits, abstract emotional states, and describing personal growth over time.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can describe complex personality nuances and abstract emotional experiences.
DomainVocabularyExample
Personality Nuanceambitious, reliable, open-minded, considerateShe is a very reliable team member.
Abstract Feelingsfrustrated, overwhelmed, content, nostalgicHe felt frustrated by the unexpected delay.
Personal Growthself-confidence, determination, resilienceLearning a language builds self-confidence.
More Examples
  • Looking at old photographs made her feel nostalgic.
  • Her determination helped her pass the B1 exam.
⚠️ Common mistakes
❌I feel very frustration today.
tap to see the correct form
✅I feel very frustrated today. (Adjective -ed for personal feeling!)
