Level A2 Elementary Vocabulary Reference

A2 Confusing Words Master Guide

Semantic confusion groups, word distinctions, and usage boundaries for elementary vocabulary

🎯 Master Goal: Instantly select the correct word in elementary conversations without mixing up similar nouns, verbs, time markers, or places.

👀 What do you notice?

Compare these two sentences: "The store clerk welcomed the new customer." (Buying goods) vs. "The lawyer welcomed her new client." (Professional service). Choosing the right noun shows natural vocabulary mastery!

1. Person check: If someone is visiting a country from another nation, are they a 'stranger' or a 'foreigner'?
Correct · A 'foreigner' comes from another country, whereas a 'stranger' is simply an unknown person.
2. Time check: "I saw her 3 days ago." Does this mean 3 days in the past from now, or 3 days before another past event?
Correct · 'Ago' counts back from the present moment.

🧠 Logic Map: Core A2 Elementary Semantic Confusion Groups

Word AWord BCore Semantic DifferenceKey Examples
Stranger Foreigner Person you don't personally know vs Person born in another country Don't talk to strangers. vs She meets foreigners at the airport.
Customer Client Person buying goods in a shop/restaurant vs Person receiving professional advice/services shop customer vs lawyer's client
Ago Before Time back from NOW vs Time back from a past reference point I arrived two hours ago. vs I had seen him two days before.
Journey Trip The traveling act/distance itself vs The entire visit + travel experience together a long train journey vs a weekend trip to Paris
Price Cost Label price tag on an item vs Total money required or expenses incurred The price is €10. vs The total cost of living is high.
Miss Lose Fail to catch transport / arrive late vs Cannot find an object or lose a game miss the bus vs lose my keys / lose the match
Win Beat Win a game, match, or prize vs Defeat a competitor or opposing team win the game / win a prize vs beat the other team 2-1
