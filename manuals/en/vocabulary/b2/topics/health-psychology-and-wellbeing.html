Health, Psychology & Wellbeing

Mental health, non-clinical psychological terms, emotional intelligence, and resilience.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss psychological resilience, emotional intelligence, and work-life balance non-clinically.
DomainVocabularyExample
Psychologyemotional intelligence, coping mechanism, mindset, perceptionDeveloping emotional intelligence improves team leadership.
Wellbeingwork-life balance, burnout prevention, mental clarityRegular exercise contributes to overall mental clarity.
Behaviorhabit formation, motivation, self-esteem, empathyPositive feedback boosts employee self-esteem.
More Examples

  • A growth mindset encourages viewing mistakes as learning opportunities.
