Environment & Sustainability (Advanced)

Climate change in depth, conservation policy, biodiversity loss, and sustainable choices.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss climate mitigation strategies, ecosystem resilience, and circular economy principles.
DomainVocabularyExample
Climate Actiondecarbonization, carbon neutrality, offsettingThe corporation pledged to achieve carbon neutrality by 2040.
Ecosystemsbiodiversity loss, ecological footprint, resilienceProtecting wetlands prevents further biodiversity loss.
Sustainable Economycircular economy, fast fashion, resource depletionA circular economy minimizes waste by reusing materials.
More Examples
  • Rapid urban expansion leads to severe resource depletion.
  • Many consumers are rejecting fast fashion in favor of durable clothing.
