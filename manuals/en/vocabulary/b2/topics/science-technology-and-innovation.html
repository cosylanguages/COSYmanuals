Science, Technology & Innovation

Scientific methodology, breakthroughs, AI, automation, and technological ethics.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can discuss artificial intelligence ethics, technological breakthroughs, and scientific research methods.
DomainVocabularyExample
Innovationbreakthrough, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, pioneerScientists achieved a major medical breakthrough.
AI & Roboticsartificial intelligence, automation, machine learningWorkplace automation is transforming manufacturing.
Ethicsethical dilemma, data privacy, surveillanceGenetic engineering poses complex ethical dilemmas.
More Examples
  • The laboratory is equipped with state-of-the-art technology.
  • Concerns over data privacy have led to stricter regulations.
