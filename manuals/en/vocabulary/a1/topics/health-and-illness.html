Health & Illness

Talking about feeling unwell

WordMeaningExample
ill UK / sick USnot feeling wellI feel ill today.
headachepain in your headI have a headache.
stomachachepain in your stomachShe has a stomachache.
toothachepain in a toothHe has a toothache.
earachepain in your earThe baby has an earache.
backachepain in your backI have backache from sitting.
a coldrunny nose and coughI have a cold.
a coughnoisy release of airHe has a bad cough.
a feverhigh body temperatureThe baby has a fever.
a sore throatpain when swallowingI have a sore throat.
tiredneeding rest or sleepI'm tired after work.
to hurtto cause or feel painMy leg hurts.
medicinesubstance taken to healTake this medicine twice a day.

💡 Note

Body part + ache = pain in that part (headache, stomachache, toothache, earache, backache).
