Make vs Do

Two of the most confused A1 verbs

WordMeaningExample
make (food, noise, decisions)creating something new that didn't existShe made a cake, make a decision.
do (work, duties, sport)general activities, work and dutiesDo homework, do exercise.

💡 Note

Do is for Duties (homework, housework, sport). Make is for creating something new (cake, decision, noise).

⚠️ Common Mistakes
❌I do a cake for the party.
tap to see correct form
✅I make a cake for the party.
