💡 How to Build a Nationality Word

Ask: Where are you from? → I'm from France. I'm French.
Endings: -ish (English, Polish), -an (American, Russian), -ese (Chinese, Japanese), -i (Pakistani), irregular (French, Greek, Welsh).
