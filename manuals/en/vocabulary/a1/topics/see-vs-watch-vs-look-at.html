See vs Watch vs Look (at)

Noticing, duration & focused direction

WordMeaningExample
seenotice with eyes without tryingI saw a bird in the garden.
watchlook at moving object on purpose over timeWe watched a film last night.
look (at)turn eyes towards something on purpose brieflyLook at this photo!

💡 Note

Watch moving things (film, match). Look at still things (photo, board). See happens automatically.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
❌We saw a film last night.
tap to see correct form
✅We watched a film last night.
