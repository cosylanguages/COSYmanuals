Common Adjectives & Opposites

High-frequency descriptive pairs

WordMeaningExample
big ≠ smallhouse dimensionsBig house, small apartment.
hot ≠ coldtemperaturesSoup is hot, salad cold.
new ≠ oldage of objectsNew phone, old one broke.
fast ≠ slowspeedTrain is fast, bus slow.
easy ≠ difficultdifficulty levelExercise easy, exam difficult.
good ≠ badqualityGood weather yesterday, bad today.
clean ≠ dirtycleanlinessCar is clean, shoes dirty.
full ≠ emptycapacityGlass full, bottle empty.
quiet ≠ noisysound levelsLibrary quiet, street noisy.
boring ≠ excitingengagementFilm boring, game exciting.

💡 Note

Adjectives go before nouns and never take plural -s: two big houses.
