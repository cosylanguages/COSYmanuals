The different parts of a house

WordMeaningExample

garden UK / yard USoutside area with grass and plantsWe had coffee in the garden.
garagebuilding for a carThe car is in the garage.
hallentrance area of a houseLeave your shoes in the hall.
balconyplatform outside an upstairs windowWe have breakfast on the balcony.
