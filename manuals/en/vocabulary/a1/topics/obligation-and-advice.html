Obligation & Advice

Saying what's necessary or recommended

WordMeaningExample
have toexpresses necessity / ruleI have to work on Monday.
don't have toexpresses lack of necessityYou don't have to come if tired.
muststrong necessity / ruleYou must wear seatbelt.
should ≠ shouldn'tgood idea / adviceYou should drink more water.

💡 Note

Followed by base verb: I have to go, You should rest.
