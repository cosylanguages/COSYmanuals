Colours

Basic colour words

WordMeaningExample
redcolour of strawberriesHer dress is red.
bluecolour of skyThe sky is blue.
greencolour of grassThe grass is green.
yellowcolour of sunThe sun is yellow.
blackdarkest colourHis shoes are black.
whitecolour of snowSnow is white.
orangecolour of orangesI like orange juice.
pinkpale red colourHer bag is pink.
purplemix of blue and redThe flower is purple.
browncolour of earth/woodHis hair is brown.
grey UK / gray UScolour between black and whiteThe cat is grey.

💡 Note

UK/AU/CA write colour / favourite. US writes color / favorite. British English keeps 'OU' like 'you'!
