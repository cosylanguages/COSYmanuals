Hear vs Listen (to)

Accidental sound vs deliberate attention

WordMeaningExample
hearnotice sound without tryingI heard a strange noise.
listen (to)pay attention to sound on purposeI listen to music every day.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
❌I hear music every evening.
tap to see correct form
✅I listen to music every evening.

⭐ Memory Trick

LISTEN and SILENT share exact same letters! Be silent to listen.
