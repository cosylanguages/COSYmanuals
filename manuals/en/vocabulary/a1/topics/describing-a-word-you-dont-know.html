Describing a Word You Don't Know

Forgot a word? Don't stop talking — describe it instead

💡 Don't Stop Talking: Describe It!

Instead of stopping, describe it! These patterns sound natural and confident in conversation and exams.

PhraseMeaning / UseExample
It's a place / country where...Locations & countriesIt's a place where you buy bread. (bakery)
It's a job / person who...People & professionsIt's a person who fixes teeth. (dentist)
It's a thing / object which...Objects & toolsIt's a thing which you use to cut paper. (scissors)
It's an animal that...AnimalsIt's an animal that says 'moo'. (cow)
It's when...Events & situationsIt's when you stay up very late. (late night)
It's how you feel when...FeelingsIt's how you feel when you haven't slept. (tired)
It's what you use to...Tools & functionsIt's what you use to open a door. (key)
It's the opposite of...AdjectivesIt's the opposite of 'expensive'. (cheap)
It looks like... / sounds like...Appearance or soundIt looks like an apple, but it's yellow. (lemon)
It starts with the letter...HintIt starts with the letter B.
