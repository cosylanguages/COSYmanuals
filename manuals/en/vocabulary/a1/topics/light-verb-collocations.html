Light-Verb Collocations

Essential high-frequency verb chunks with HAVE, MAKE, DO, GO, TAKE, GET, PLAY, PUT, and TURN for beginner fluency.

📖 Inter-Manual Connections
Pair this collocations reference with matching structural rules & practice systems:

  • A2 Reference: A2 Master Collocations Dictionary

1. HAVE Collocations

have breakfast verb chunk

To eat the first meal of the day.

"I have breakfast at seven o'clock."

have lunch verb chunk

To eat the midday meal.

"We have lunch at school."

have dinner verb chunk

To eat the main meal in the evening.

"They have dinner at home."

have a shower verb chunk

To wash yourself standing under a spray of water.

"I have a shower in the morning."

have fun verb chunk

To enjoy yourself in an activity.

"Children have fun at the park."

2. MAKE Collocations

make breakfast verb chunk

To prepare the morning meal.

"He can make breakfast for the family."

make dinner verb chunk

To prepare the evening meal.

"She makes dinner in the evening."

make tea verb chunk

To prepare a warm cup of tea.

"I make tea for my friend."

make coffee verb chunk

To prepare a cup of coffee.

"He makes coffee in the morning."

make a mistake verb chunk

To do or write something incorrectly.

"It is fine to make a mistake when learning."

3. DO Collocations

do homework verb chunk

To complete school exercises at home.

"The student does homework after school."

do the shopping verb chunk

To buy food and household items.

"We do the shopping on Saturday."

do sport verb chunk

To participate in physical exercise or games.

"Children do sport at school."

do work verb chunk

To perform daily tasks or job duties.

"I do my work at the office."

4. GO Collocations

go home verb chunk

To travel back to your house or flat.

"I go home after school."

go shopping verb chunk

To visit shops to buy items.

"She goes shopping on Friday."

go to bed verb chunk

To lie down in bed to sleep.

"Children go to bed early."

go swimming verb chunk

To swim for exercise or fun.

"We go swimming in summer."

go to school verb chunk

To travel to school for classes.

"They go to school on Monday."

5. TAKE Collocations

take a photo verb chunk

To capture an image using a camera or phone.

"Can you take a photo of my family?"

take a picture verb chunk

To take a photograph.

"She takes a picture of the garden."

take a shower verb chunk

To wash yourself under a shower.

"I take a shower before bed."

take a bus verb chunk

To travel by public bus.

"He takes a bus to work."

take medicine verb chunk

To swallow or use illness medicine.

"Take medicine when you are sick."

6. GET Collocations

get up verb chunk

To rise from bed after waking.

"I get up at six o'clock."

get married verb chunk

To become husband or wife in marriage.

"They get married in summer."

get home verb chunk

To arrive at your home.

"We get home in the evening."

get a job verb chunk

To find employment or work.

"She wants a new job in town."

7. PLAY Collocations

play football verb chunk

To participate in a game of football.

"Children play football in the park."

play soccer verb chunk

To play football/soccer.

"We play soccer after school."

play tennis verb chunk

To play a game of tennis.

"She plays tennis on Saturday."

play games verb chunk

To play board games or sports games.

"Children play games at home."

8. PUT Collocations

put on verb chunk

To place clothing or accessories on your body.

"Put on your coat — it is cold."

put down verb chunk

To place something onto a surface.

"Put down your bag, please."

9. TURN Collocations

turn on verb chunk

To switch on an appliance or light.

"Turn on the light, please."

turn off verb chunk

To switch off an appliance or light.

"Turn off the TV before bed."

turn left verb chunk

To change direction toward the left side.

"Turn left at the corner."

turn right verb chunk

To change direction toward the right side.

"Turn right at the bank."
