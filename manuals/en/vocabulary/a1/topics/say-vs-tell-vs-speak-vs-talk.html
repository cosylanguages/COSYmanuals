Part 13 · Vocabulary Topic

Say vs Tell vs Speak vs Talk

Four communication verbs demystified

WordMeaningExample
saybefore exact words or with 'that' (no listener named)She said, 'Hello!' / He said that he was tired.
tellalways needs a person directly after itShe told me the news.
speakused for languages or formal talkShe speaks French.
talkcasual conversation between peopleWe talked for an hour.

💡 Note

Tell always needs someONE directly after (tell + person). Talk is casual chat; speak works alone or for languages.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
❌She said me the truth.
tap to see correct form
✅She told me the truth.
