Bring vs Take

Direction towards speaker vs away

WordMeaningExample
bringcarry towards place where speaker isPlease bring your book to class.
takecarry away from where speaker is nowTake your umbrella when you leave.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
❌Take your book tomorrow here!
tap to see correct form
✅Bring your book tomorrow here!

⭐ Memory Trick

BRING rhymes with 'here-ing' — bring HERE. TAKE off like rocket — take AWAY.
