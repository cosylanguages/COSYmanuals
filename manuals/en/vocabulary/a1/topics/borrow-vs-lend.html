Borrow vs Lend

Taking with return vs giving for a time

WordMeaningExample
borrowtake and use something belonging to someone elseCan I borrow your pen?
lendgive something of yours to someone else temporarilyCan you lend me your pen?
⚠️ Common Mistakes
❌Can you borrow me your pen?
tap to see correct form
✅Can you lend me your pen?

⭐ Memory Trick

BORROW has 'OW' — 'Ow, now I owe you!' LEND means giving out.
