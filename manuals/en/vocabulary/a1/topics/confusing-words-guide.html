A0–A1 Confusing Words Master Guide

Basic movement verbs, communication actions, possession, and everyday object distinctions

🎯 Master Goal: Confidently choose the right everyday action verb and noun pair in beginner conversations without second-guessing yourself.

👀 What do you notice?

Compare these two sentences: "I live in a big house." (Physical building) vs. "Welcome to my home!" (Emotional place where you belong). Notice how 'home' expresses personal feeling!

💡 Check your understanding (CCQs)

Score: 0 / 2
1. Movement check: "Please bring your textbook tomorrow." Is the book coming towards the speaker or moving away?
Correct · 'Bring' means carry towards the speaker, while 'take' means carry away from the speaker.
2. Work check: Is 'job' countable or uncountable?
Correct · 'Job' is a countable noun (a job, two jobs), while 'work' is an uncountable activity noun or verb.

🧠 Logic Map: Core A0–A1 Beginner Semantic Confusion Groups

Word AWord BCore Semantic DifferenceKey Examples
Come Go Movement towards the speaker / current place vs Movement away to another place Come here! vs I go to work at 8 AM.
Bring Take Carry something towards the speaker vs Carry something away to another place Bring your homework here. vs Take your umbrella when you leave.
Borrow Lend Take something temporarily FROM someone vs Give something temporarily TO someone Can I borrow a pen? vs I will lend you my pen.
Say Tell Focus on words spoken (say something) vs Focus on the recipient (tell someone something) Say hello to John. vs Tell me your name.
Hear Listen Passive sound perception (ears receive sound) vs Active conscious attention (listen to music) I hear a noise outside. vs I listen to the teacher.
House Home Physical building/structure vs Personal place where you live and feel belonging They built a new house. vs I feel at home here.
Job Work Countable position of employment (a job) vs Uncountable general labor activity (work) I got a new job! vs I have a lot of work today.
