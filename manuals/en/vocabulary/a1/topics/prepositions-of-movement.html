WordMeaningExample

ontomovement to surfaceCat jumped onto table.
throughentering one side and leaving otherWe walked through park.
acrossfrom one side to otherShe ran across road.
alongfollowing length/directionWe walked along river.
uptowards higher placeGo up the stairs.
downtowards lower placeGo down the stairs.
pastto and beyond placeWalk past bank and turn left.
