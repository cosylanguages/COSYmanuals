Part 1 · Vocabulary Topic

Jobs & Occupations

Common jobs, including modern ones — ask 'What's your job?' or 'What do you do?'

WordMeaningExample
doctorhelps sick people get betterThe doctor works at the hospital.
nursetakes care of patientsThe nurse gives medicine to patients.
teacherteaches studentsMy teacher is very kind.
engineerdesigns machines and structuresShe is an engineer at a car company.
waiter / waitressserves food in a restaurantThe waiter brought our food.
shop assistant UK / sales associate UShelps customers in a shopThe shop assistant was helpful.
police officerkeeps people safeThe police officer helped us.
firefighterputs out firesThe firefighter saved the cat.
driverdrives a vehicle as a jobThe bus driver was friendly.
chef / cookcooks food in a restaurantThe chef made a delicious soup.
farmergrows food or keeps animalsThe farmer has many cows.
dentisttreats teethI saw the dentist yesterday.
hairdressercuts and styles hairThe hairdresser cut my hair.
software developerwrites computer programsShe's a software developer.
delivery driverdelivers goods to homesThe delivery driver left the parcel.

💡 Note

Suffixes for jobs: -er/-or (teacher, actor), -ist (artist, scientist), -ian (musician, librarian).
