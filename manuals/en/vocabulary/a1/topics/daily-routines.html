WordMeaningExample
get upleave your bed in the morningI get up at seven o'clock.
have breakfast / lunch / dinnereat a mealWe have breakfast together.
take a showerwash body standing upHe takes a shower before work.
brush your teethclean teeth with toothbrushBrush your teeth twice a day.
get dressedput on clothesShe gets dressed quickly.
go to work / schooltravel to job or studyI go to work by bus.
go hometravel back to houseWe go home after class.
do homeworkcomplete study tasksHe does homework after dinner.
watch TVlook at televisionThey watch TV in the evening.
go to bedlie down in bed to sleepI go to bed at eleven.
fall asleepstart sleepingShe fell asleep on the sofa.

💡 Note

Pair specific verbs: say 'have breakfast' (not make), 'take a shower' (not do), 'do homework' (not make).
