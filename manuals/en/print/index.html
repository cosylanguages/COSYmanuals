Unit 1: Present Simple — Verbs & Copula "to be"

📖 Full Interactive Lesson: See this unit's complete interactive lesson on COSYplatform.

💭 Think Like a Native Speaker

English verbs require explicit subjects. Unlike pro-drop languages, every sentence needs a pronoun or noun (e.g., "It is raining", not "Is raining").

Subject Pronoun Affirmative ("to be") Negative ("to be") Regular Verb (Work)
I am am not work
You / We / They are are not (aren't) work
He / She / It is is not (isn't) works (+s)

Core Rule: Add -s or -es to the third-person singular (he/she/it) in present simple statements.

★ Irregular Verb Pattern Groupings

Rather than memorizing alphabetical lists, group irregular verbs by sound change pattern:

Pattern Group Base Form Past Simple Past Participle
Group 1: No Change cost, cut, hit cost, cut, hit cost, cut, hit
Group 2: Vowel Change Only sing, drink, swim sang, drank, swam sung, drunk, swum
Group 3: Add -t / -d keep, sleep, sell kept, slept, sold kept, slept, sold
Group 4: Highly Irregular be, go, do was/were, went, did been, gone, done
