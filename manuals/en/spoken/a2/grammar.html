Unit 1: Interactive Everyday Speech & Social Connections

Lesson 1: Making Plans & Comparing Options

Grammar Focus Points:
  • Going to vs Present Continuous
  • Comparatives (better, cheaper, more convenient)
🎯 I can: Can discuss plans, make arrangements, and compare alternatives in conversation.
Task
Weekend activity planning: negotiate a shared schedule with a partner using comparatives.
Speaking focus
Natural stress on comparative adjectives (BET-ter, MORE convenient).

Lesson 2: Telling Short Personal Stories

Grammar Focus Points:
  • Past Simple (regular & irregular)
  • Time connectors (first, then, after that, suddenly)
🎯 I can: Can narrate a short personal story or past experience.
Task
Story circle: tell a 2-minute true or fictional personal anecdote to a small group.
Speaking focus
Pacing narrative transitions using connectors like 'suddenly' and 'after that'.

Lesson 3: Describing Places & Travel Experiences

Grammar Focus Points:
  • Superlatives (the most beautiful, the oldest)

🎯 I can: Can describe a past holiday, travel destination, or favorite spot vividly.
Task
Travel pitch: describe your best holiday destination and answer partner questions.
Speaking focus
Vivid vocal intonation when describing memorable landscapes.

Lesson 4: Keeping a Conversation Going: Active Listening

Grammar Focus Points:

Lesson 5: Social Connection Roleplay & Unit Review
