Grammar Focus Points:
  • Can I see your...?
  • I have / I don't have...
🎯 I can: Can follow airport security instructions and answer basic check-in questions.

Lesson 3: Luggage Claim & Flight Departure

Grammar Focus Points:
  • What time is...?
  • Which gate...?
🎯 I can: Can identify flight information boards and locate baggage carousel.

Lesson 4: Public Transit & Airport Express

Grammar Focus Points:

Lesson 1: Hotel Check-In & Reservations

Grammar Focus Points:
  • I have a reservation for...

Lesson 2: Room Amenities & Requests

Grammar Focus Points:
  • Is there... in the room?
  • Could I get extra...?

Lesson 3: Breakfast & Hotel Facilities

Grammar Focus Points:
  • What time is breakfast served?
  • Where is the...?

Lesson 4: Hotel Check-Out & Storing Luggage

Grammar Focus Points:

Lesson 1: Cafes & Fast Food Ordering

Grammar Focus Points:

  • To go / For here
🎯 I can: Can order simple drinks and snacks at a cafe or fast food counter.

Grammar Focus Points:

  • Do you have a table near...?

Grammar Focus Points:
  • I will have the...
  • I don't eat...
🎯 I can: Can order main dishes and state basic dietary preferences.

Grammar Focus Points:
