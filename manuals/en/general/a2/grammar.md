## Unit 1: Unit 1: Personal Profiles, Upbringing & Life History

### Lesson 1: Past Simple vs Past Continuous

**Grammar Focus Points:**
- Past Simple for completed past actions and main storyline events
- Past Continuous for background actions in progress (was/were + -ing)
- Interrupted actions using when and while

**Teaching Notes & Rules:**
- **Lesson Code:** `EN-A2-001`
- **Goal (Can-Do):** Can narrate a simple past event and describe background actions using when and while
- **Pronunciation Focus:**
  - **Past simple -ed endings**: Distinguish between /t/, /d/, and /ɪd/ endings for regular past tense verbs.
    - *Examples:* `started` /wɜːkt/ / /pleɪd/ / /ˈstɑː.tɪd/

### Lesson 2: Past Habits & Life Experiences (Used to & Present Perfect)

**Grammar Focus Points:**
- Used to + infinitive for past habits and states no longer true
- Present Perfect with ever and never for life experiences
- Contrast between Past Simple (finished time) and Present Perfect (unfinished time)

**Teaching Notes & Rules:**
- **Lesson Code:** `EN-A2-002`
- **Goal (Can-Do):** Can describe past habits and talk about general life experiences
- **Pronunciation Focus:**
  - **The used to liaison**: The final d in used and initial t in to blend into a single /tuː/ sound.
    - *Examples:* `used to` /ˈjuːs.tə/
