CEFR A1 Hospitality & Socializing

Offers & Hospitality

Offering food, drinks, and assistance, welcoming guests, and accepting or declining graciously.

💬 How do I offer something to a guest and respond politely?

Welcoming people to your home or office and offering refreshments forms the core of daily hospitality.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can welcome guests ("Welcome! Please come in"), offer food/drink ("Would you like some water?"), and accept or refuse warmly ("Yes, please!", "No, thanks, I'm good").

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Offer Chips

Welcome! / Please come in! Have a seat! / Make yourself comfortable. Would you like a glass of water? Can I get you some tea or coffee? Yes, please! That's very kind. No, thank you! I'm good for now. Here you go! / Help yourself. Enjoy! / Thank you so much!

🗺️ Hospitality Flow Map

1
Welcome Guest: "Welcome! Come in and have a seat."
2
Offer Refreshment: "Can I get you something to drink? Tea or coffee?"
3
Gracious Response: "A cup of tea would be great, thank you!" OR "No, thank you, I'm fine."
4
Serve & Enjoy: "Here you go! Help yourself to sugar." — "Thanks a lot!"

👀 Notice: Gracious Refusals

Notice how "I'm good" or "I'm fine, thank you" softens a refusal when you don't need a drink. It signals satisfaction rather than rejection.

🧠 Think in Conversation

Hosts in English-speaking cultures offer drinks almost immediately upon arrival. Saying "Yes, please" or "No, thank you" right away puts both host and guest at ease.

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "I refuse liquid."
✅ Natural: "No, thanks! I'm good for now."

💡 Pragmatic Memory Trick

Remember the Host Rule: Welcome ➔ Seat ➔ Drink ➔ Enjoy!

💬 Sample Exchange

A: Welcome to our office, David! Please come in and sit down.
B: Thank you so much!
A: Can I get you a coffee or a glass of water?
B: A glass of water would be lovely, thanks!
