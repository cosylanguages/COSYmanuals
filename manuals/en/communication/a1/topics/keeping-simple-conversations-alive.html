CEFR A1 Active Listening

Keeping Simple Conversations Alive

Using active listening signals, 'And you?', and small talk bridges.

💬 How do I keep a conversation going without long silent pauses?

Active listening reactions and quick follow-ups keep exchanges fluid and natural.

🎯 What you can communicate after this page: You can react with enthusiasm ("Really?", "Great!"), pass the turn back ("How about you?"), and show attention.

🧩 Modular Building Blocks: Continuity Chips

Really? That's great! Oh, I see! / That's interesting! And you? / How about you? That sounds nice! It's a beautiful day, isn't it? I'm so sorry! / My mistake. Congratulations! / Good luck! It was nice talking to you!

☀️ 1. Weather Small Talk

Context Examples
  • What's the weather like today? — It's sunny and warm.
  • It's a beautiful day, isn't it?
  • It's raining and cold today.

👀 What do you notice?

English speakers frequently use weather remarks as a friendly opener or conversation bridge.

🩺 2. Health & Feeling Unwell

Context Examples
  • How are you feeling? — I feel sick today.
  • I have a headache / cold.
  • What's wrong? — I don't feel well.

💡 Check your understanding

Score: 0 / 1
1. What is a polite response when a friend says "I feel sick"?
Expressing sympathy when someone feels unwell builds supportive conversation.

🙏 3. Extended Social Apologies

Context Examples
  • I'm so sorry for being late!
  • I apologize — my mistake.
  • Don't worry about it! / It's fine!

🥖 4. Expressing Physical Needs & Wants

Context Examples
  • I'm hungry. Let's get some food!
  • I'm thirsty. Can I have some water, please?
  • I'm tired. I need to go to sleep.

🎉 5. Congratulating & Social Celebrations

Context Examples
  • Congratulations! That's wonderful news!
  • Happy birthday! Have a great day!
  • Good job! / Good luck!

🗣 Natural Speech Add-on

❌ Stiff: "I have processed your statement."
✅ Natural: "Oh really? That's awesome!"
